# Task 01 — find the film's grade

Read `AGENTS.md` first. This is your whole assignment; there is nothing after it until
we have looked at what you come back with.

---

## The situation

We have a finished 7-minute film whose clips were all generated against one written
look, and they did not all arrive there. Some came out neutral where they should be
warm. One came from a different model and sits apart from the rest. Nobody has graded
any of it yet.

**We would like you to work out what the grade should be.**

---

## What we are hoping for

Take the clips in `./clips/`, look at them, and **design a grade** that takes this
footage to the look described in `AGENTS.md`. Build it in Resolve, on the footage, and
judge it with your own eyes.

**Please give us two or three distinct options rather than one answer.** Not
variations of a slider — genuinely different readings of "warm shadow, cold white".
One might be restrained and filmic, one might lean harder into the amber, one might
do something we have not thought of. We would rather choose between real alternatives
than approve the first thing that works.

For each one, a frame or two so we can see it, and a sentence on what you were going
for.

---

## The question we most need answered

`S21-TheNewsWall-Fix1.MP4` came from **Seedance 2.0 Fast**. Everything else came from
**2.5**. It is more saturated and sharper than its neighbours.

**Can one grade carry both, or does this film need two?**

You are better placed to answer that than we are, and the answer changes how the rest
of the week is planned. If the honest answer is "two looks", say so — it is not a
failure, and we would much rather know now than discover it in the timeline.

---

## What we would like back

Whatever you would hand a competent operator who has no colour judgement of their own
and has not seen any of this: the node structure, the values you chose, the menus and
buttons, screenshots of the panels. Enough that they can rebuild it exactly and then
apply it across forty more clips without asking you anything.

And please mark clearly which part is **the shared look** — the thing that becomes a
LUT for the whole film — and which part is **per-clip correction**. That division is
the difference between a day of work and three.

**Then stop.** Do not start applying it to the rest. Someone else does that, and we
will come back to you with what they find.

---

## Things worth knowing

- **Time is short — three days to delivery.** If something is going to be a problem,
  we would rather hear it early and half-formed than late and certain.
- Say if you think the written look is wrong for the footage. You can see it; we are
  working from a sentence written before any of it existed.
- If a shot needs something beyond grading — a fix, a patch, a comp — note it and move
  on. There is a list of those and yours would be a useful addition to it.
- `REFERENCE.md` has what we have measured so far, so you do not have to repeat it.
