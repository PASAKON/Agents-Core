**(CMO-corrected)** Two studies we opened found no sex difference in cats: one owner survey in families with a child (C3-1) and one small hormone study (C3-2). That is a difference not detected, in two specific samples; it does not show that none exists. Nothing here is specific to male cats.

## C4. How do a dog and a cat living in one home relate?

**(CMO-corrected)** In an owner-reported survey of 1,270 homes with both species, most pairs are peaceful, dogs more sociable and cats more aloof (C4-1). In a controlled test, dogs tried to get between the owner and a dog-like toy more often when the owner petted it than a non-social object (C4-2). No row has a CAT as the rival, so 'a dog's reaction when the owner favours a cat' is not tested here.
