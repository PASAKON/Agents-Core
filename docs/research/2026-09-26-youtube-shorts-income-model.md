## 🟡 PERISHABLE (spot-check the line you rely on)

**YPP (official, read 2026-09-26):**
- Today: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours/12 mo OR **10M qualified Shorts views/90 days** → ad revenue.
  Lower tier (500 subs + 3M Shorts views) unlocks fan funding only, no ads. Thailand is eligible.
  Review "typically in about 1 month".
- **From 1 Feb 2027 (blog.youtube, 10 Aug 2026, re-read by CTO):** new applicants need 8,000 hours/365 d or
  **20M Shorts views/90 d**; every channel (incl. existing partners) needs **10M Shorts views in the
  last 90 days** to earn from Shorts that month. New terms must be accepted by 31 Jan 2027.
- Shorts pool: creator keeps **45%** of allocated revenue; music takes ½ (1 track) or ⅔ (2 tracks) of a
  Short's slice before the split. Studio "Estimated revenue" is already net of YouTube's share.
- Views: since 31 Mar 2025 "views" count every start/replay; pay follows "engaged views". (official)

**Payout (official):** month M finalized in AdSense 7th–12th of M+1; paid 21st–26th if balance ≥ US$100;
wire up to 15 business days. First payment needs identity + tax info + address PIN by post (3–4 weeks;
unverified in 4 months → demonetized). Realistic: a view becomes cash ~6–9 weeks later; the first payment
2–4+ months after YPP approval. (inference on the totals)

**Tax (official + treaty):** no tax info → 24% withheld on worldwide YouTube earnings; W-8BEN without
treaty → 30% of US-viewer earnings; US–Thailand treaty Art. 12 caps copyright royalties at **5%** (how
Google applies it to Thailand: inference). Thai side: foreign income taxed when brought in (Por.161/2566,
from 2024), usually §40(8). (practitioner)

**What demonetizes / terminates (official unless marked):**
- Inauthentic content (renamed from "repetitious" 15 Jul 2025): "AI-generated content made with generic or
  unoriginal templates", "templated storylines", "characters put in the same situation over and over".
  Reused content = others' material without significant original value. Judged on the channel as a whole.
- **16 Jul 2026 rewrite** (TechCrunch 20 Jul 2026, re-read by CTO; policy page official): three headings —
  Generic or Repetitive · Unsatisfying or Off-putting (e.g. animal-in-distress → rescue) · **AI Personas
  Related to Sensitive Topics** (health, legal, **finance**, politics — "AI podcast hosts offering financial
  guidance"). ⚠ Relevant to any AI-avatar finance channel we might put on YouTube (BLACK LIQUIDITY). (inference)
- Spam: "automated or synthetic mass-production"; evasion tricks (sped-up audio, cropping) banned.
- AI disclosure required for realistic synthetic content; disclosing "won't limit a video's audience or
  impact its eligibility to earn money".
- Fake engagement (bought views/subs, sub4sub) banned — you are liable for anyone you hire.
- 3 strikes in 90 days → termination; a terminated owner may not use or create any other channel;
  creators report YPP blocked on every channel on the same AdSense (secondhand).
- Location: must live in a YPP country; AdSense country cannot be changed. No official line on VPNs.

**Market (practitioner, 2026):** Thai "ช่อง ตปท" courses sell faceless + AI voice + English tier-1 audience
(MicRubik ฿990–9,990, Skool ฿4,990/yr ~3,800 members; SkillLane courses). Shorts RPM: US $0.33, UK/CA/AU
~$0.17–0.19, SEA $0.01–0.02 (AIR Media-Tech 2025). Thai practitioners report AI channels demonetized as
"เนื้อหาไม่ใช่ต้นฉบับ" in Mar–Apr 2026 (Noodchannel's "3,000+ channels" figure unverified).
