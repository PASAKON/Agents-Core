# BL x TikTok Shop — first checks (free, no spend, no login)

## 1. Policy: the CTO's claim, checked [M]

Source: seller-th.tiktok.com "Prohibited and Unsupported Products" (page date 05/08/2026 as fetched) and "Prohibited Products Guidelines". Both pages read twice through WebFetch; open the page itself once more before a listing decision.

| Item | TikTok Shop TH status | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| Crypto / digital tokens | PROHIBITED | "Financial digital assets, including cryptocurrencies and digital tokens" |
| Financial products and services | UNSUPPORTED | "provision of funds, insurance, stocks, loans, money transfer" |
| Foreign coins / paper money | PROHIBITED | "Any coins or paper money (whether collectible or circulating) in foreign currency" |
| Thai coins / paper money | UNSUPPORTED | same line, Thai currency |
| Spy gear, hidden cameras | UNSUPPORTED | "Spy equipment and hidden photographic devices" |
| Lock-picking tools and books | UNSUPPORTED | "Lockpicking or locksmithing devices, tools, related books and tutorials" |
| Counterfeit-note detector, safe, shredder, privacy screen, RFID sleeve, piggy bank, finance books | not listed | absence on this page is not approval; category approval shows at listing time |

Verdict: the CTO is right on financial products and crypto. The hardware-wallet question is still open: the page bans crypto assets, not wallets, but a wallet basket on a scam channel invites a manual review. Skip it. Ordinary CCTV is not listed, but "hidden cameras" is, so avoid anything sold as hidden or spy.

Not checked: whether TikTok's community rules limit the video itself when it talks about forex or scams. BL already lives with that risk on the open feed.

## 5. Operator-lens notes (competitor-questioning playbook)

- Job the viewer hires BL for [O]: "do not let me or my family get scammed". That is a protection job, not a trading-edge job. Trader desk gear (monitor arm, bias light) answers a different job.
- Real rivals for the basket slot [O]: the viewer's wallet on a ฿200 item right after a loss story; free "scam-prevention" tips; doing nothing. No rival sells "protection kits" through scam videos that I found, but I did not search for one.
- Scary question: after a story of a family losing savings, does a ฿200 item at about ฿19 net per order [O, 10% commission, 24% returns, from the 2026-09-28 note] justify any loss of trust, and does BL reach the roughly 0.87M basket-video views a week that $100 a week needs?
- Uncontested axis [O]: "protect" (safe, shredder, card sleeve, privacy screen) instead of "get rich" gear.
- Cheapest test [O]: three baskets from three groups (protect, trader desk, saving box), one week each on organic posts, compare product clicks per 1,000 views. Needs the CEO's ID check and at least 1,000 followers; no ad spend.
