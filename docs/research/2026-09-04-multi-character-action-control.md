The rules we should adopt\n\n**R1 — Cap bound Elements at 8 per generation.** Above 8, demote the least load-bearing characters to *unbound background bodies*: describe their appearance in text, attach no chip. Rotate who is active across beats. A prohibition leaves every non-reaction unspecified, and the model fills the gap with the nearest strong attractor, which is *copy the person who is doing something*. This is the direct fix for group-reaction bleed.\n\n**R4 — Front-load the beats, not the roster.** The first 20–25% of the prompt is who-does-what-when. Optics, grade, lens, film stock and look go at the **end** — they are the cheapest content to lose and they are also the most redundant with the previz and our house look.\n\n**R5 — Shrink the POSITION MAP to one line per character.** Proxy colour + proxy shape + screen position + `@handle` + a three-word wardrobe anchor. Repetition is documented to increase accuracy, and the appositive gives the model a second, non-@ handle on the same person.\n\n**R7 — Disjoint verb sets.** No two characters active in the same beat may share a verb. If @mali *turns*, nobody else turns in that window — pick *shifts weight*, *leans*, *lowers*, *glances*. Shared verbs are the transmission vector for bleed.\n\n**R8 — One primary action per character per beat. No time-varying attributes.** \"Calm then furious\" is the ≈0.06 cell. Cut to a second shot.\n\n**R9 — Flat, independent beats. No causal or conditional links between characters.** Ban \"because\", \"which causes\", \"after he sees her\", \"in response\". Depth-4 dependencies land at 5.6%.\n\n**R10 — Group reactions become a named sequence, never a group instruction.** `[6s-8s] @mali freezes. [8s-10s] @prasit raises the folder. What we should stop doing\n\n1. **Stop putting anything load-bearing in the last 40% of the prompt** — including the anti-clone block the official docs tell you to append there.\n2. **Stop using prohibitions for per-character behaviour.** \"X does not react\", \"nobody else moves\", \"no one turns\" — these have no per-subject addressing and, on overlap with a positive instruction, actively damage it.
