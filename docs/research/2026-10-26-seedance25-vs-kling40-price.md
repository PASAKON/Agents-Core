# Seedance 2.5 vs Kling 4.0 — round by round

## Basis, dates, and what “best price” means

**All linked web sources below were opened or attempted on 2026-10-26.** Each source label links to its URL in the dated source register. All monetary figures are advertised web list prices or calculations from those prices, before tax, card fees and FX spreads. No generation, payment, trial, signup or login occurred. Counts and performance observations from the brief or internal files are identified separately; nothing was measured by us in this research.

Use **USD 1 = THB 33.6359** throughout: derived from Mataf’s opened conversion table showing USD 100 = THB 3,363.59, dated **2026-10-25**, read 2026-10-26. The search snippet showed a different date/rate; the opened page wins. This is a budgeting rate, not a Thai card settlement quote. [FX]

“Cheapest” means cheapest sufficiently specified offer found in this bounded survey, not proof of a worldwide minimum. Separate:

- **PAYG consumption:** generation charges without committing to an annual subscription; prepaid wallet minimums remain unverified.
- **Allocated subscription cost:** plan price divided by credits, assuming all credits are used; not the cash required for one job.
- **Owned Unlimited marginal cost:** zero extra money only while the account’s grant actually applies.
- **Usable cost:** rate × generated seconds ÷ accepted fraction. Neither acceptance fractions nor provider reliability were tested.

The original September table and research index were read. No newer dedicated Kling/Seedance price comparison appeared in the index; the October Higgsfield promotion note is relevant but does not establish today’s entitlement.

## Costs hidden by headline rates, payment and output rights

- **Video references:** PoYo Kling bills output + max(rounded total video input, output), so its $0.085/billed-second 720p rate has a minimum effective **$0.17/output-second (฿5.72)**. A 10 s output with a short video reference costs at least **$1.70/฿57.18**, not $0.85. fal has the same minimum-double-duration principle. Do not enter these input-conditioned rates in the cheapest-output round. [P2] [Kfr]
- **Seedance references:** BytePlus applies input+output tokens with a minimum when video is supplied. Runware separately lists video-to-video rates, not interchangeable with text-to-video. Seedance supports up to 30 images, 10 videos and 10 audio references at model level; Kling supports up to 15 combined reference assets. Both offer native dialogue; quality and Thai lip-sync were not tested. [B] [R] [S] [K]
