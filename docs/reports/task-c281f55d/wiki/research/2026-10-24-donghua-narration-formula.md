## FORMULA CARD

[U] **Provisional original-production rules — audition and test, not measured source prescriptions.** All 24 rules below are proposals. They transfer functions, never source names, character designs, plot incidents or dialogue.

1. [U] **Writer:** Define one desire, one obstacle, one distinctive advantage and one cost before scripting.
2. [U] Start on an understandable action whose outcome matters; explain history when a present problem needs it.
3. [U] Build each unit as attempt → changed information → reaction → new decision.
4. [U] Let an advantage solve something and expose a limitation; avoid effortless escalating wins.
5. [U] Use relational embarrassment, resource scarcity, negotiation and loyalty alongside combat.
6. [U] Make numbers legible stakes: what is needed, available and lost; do not insert empty power statistics.
7. [U] Place comedy after sufficient setup; preserve a reaction beat before the next fact.
8. [U] Resolve at least one promised question before opening the next; make an ending's unresolved thread deliberate.
9. [U] **15 s writer:** Test 0–3 s action/stake, 3–8 s obstacle, 8–12 s reversal, 12–15 s reaction/payoff or one precise open question.
10. [U] **15 s editor:** Omit long ritual intros and flashbacks; make the crucial action understandable on a small screen.
11. [U] **3 min writer:** Test 0–20 s premise, 20–60 s first attempt, 60–120 s complication/cost, 120–165 s countermove, 165–180 s payoff/new direction.
12. [U] **3 min director:** Give the protagonist a relationship or vulnerability so competence has emotional meaning.
13. [U] **10 min writer:** Compose several causal units with local payoffs; use the middle to deepen cost and end with a materially changed situation.
