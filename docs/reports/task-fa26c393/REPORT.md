## One-Line Summary per Claim

1. **Claim 1 (`#1 on GDPval at 68.9%, beating Claude Sonnet 5.5`) — `partly true`:** The clip conflates the GDP-weighted **Vals Index** with **GDPval**; Gemini 4 Argon is **#1 on the Vals Index at 68.90% (68.9%)**, beating **Claude Sonnet 5.5 (67.04%)** and **Claude Opus 5.5 (66.97%)** ([vals.ai](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/vals_index): *"As of October 13, 2026, Gemini 4 Argon ranks first on the Vals Index with 68.90%, followed by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (67.04%) and Claude Opus 5.5 (66.97%)."*), whereas on **GDPval-AA v2.1** it scores **56.2% / 1,624 Elo** behind Claude Sonnet 5.5's **1,844 Elo** ([artificialanalysis.ai](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdpval-aa)).
2. **Claim 2 (`#1 on coding benchmarks (DeepSWE and others)`) — `partly true`:** Gemini 4 Argon is **#1 on DeepSWE v1.1 at 77.9%** ([blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/): *"It sets a new state of the art on DeepSWE v1.1 (77.9%), which measures a model's performance in real-world long-horizon software engineering tasks."*) and leads **LABBench 2 (88.8%)**, but trails competitors on several other coding benchmarks in Google's Model Card ([deepmind.google](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon)), including **FrontierSWE v2 (55.0% vs. GPT-6 Astra's 65.5%)**, **Terminal-bench 4.0 (57.4% vs. Claude Opus 5.5's 66.4%)**, and **PostTrainBench (45.3% vs. Claude Opus 5.5's 49.3%)**.
3. **Claim 3 (`1M-token context and up to 1M output tokens`) — `true`:** Gemini 4 Argon supports a **1M-token unified input-output context window** and expands maximum output generation to **1M tokens** ([blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/): *"To support Gemini 4 Argon's capabilities across longer, more complex use cases, we are significantly expanding the model's output token limit to an industry-leading 1M tokens, up from the previous 64K tokens."*).
4. **Claim 4 (`Hallucination rate 15%, the lowest of any model`) — `partly true`:** Gemini 4 Argon achieves a **15% hallucination rate** on **AA-Omniscience**, which is the lowest **among leading models scoring 45+ on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index** rather than across every model regardless of tier ([artificialanalysis.ai](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gemini-4-argon): *"On AA-Omniscience, Gemini 4 Argon has a 15% hallucination rate, the lowest of any model scoring 45+ on the Intelligence Index, compared with 51% for GPT-6 Astra (max) and 54% for GPT-6.1 Sol (max)."*).
5. **Claim 5 (`Cyber: finds and patches vulnerabilities, 2× better than other models`) — `partly true`:** Gemini 4 Argon autonomously finds and patches vulnerabilities and found *"over two times more vulnerabilities than baseline models"* in healthcare software evaluations, but on primary multi-model benchmarks it **ties for #1 at 68% on CWE-bench v1** with GPT-6 Astra (68.0%) and just ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 (67.0%) ([blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/): *"On CWE-bench v1, which evaluates the model's ability to remediate security vulnerabilities, Argon ties for first place with a top score of 68%, building on 3.8 Flash Cyber's frontier performance on CWE-bench v0."*).
6. **Claim 6 (`"Team Preview": dozens of agents working together inside Antigravity`) — `partly true`:** The multi-agent feature inside Google Antigravity is called **Teamwork** (`https://antigravity.google/blog/teamwork-when-ai-becomes-a-research-partner`) and is invoked via **`/teamwork-preview`** (not `"Team Preview"`), where an orchestrator coordinates agent teams by *"spawning dozens of specialized sub-agents to write, test, and debug code"* ([research.google](https://research.google/blog/analyzing-technical-documents-with-deep-review/)).
7. **Claim 7 (`60% cheaper (API) until the end of November`) — `partly true`:** The promotional API discount runs **through November 30, 2026**, but the introductory rate (**$2.00 / 1M input tokens** and **$10.00 / 1M output tokens**, with cached input at **$0.10 / 1M** or **95% off**) is **50% cheaper**—not 60% cheaper—than the standard pricing taking effect **December 1, 2026** (**$4.00 / 1M input** and **$20.00 / 1M output**) ([ai.google.dev](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing), [blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/): *"The model launches today at an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens (with cached input tokens at 95% off)."*).
