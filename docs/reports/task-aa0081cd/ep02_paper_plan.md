## How to read

- Times: `take-local` = seconds inside the voice take (from the ASR words file, snapped to a pause). `global` = estimated film time. Global is an estimate because the clean take wavs are not on this host (see Not checked).
- Film estimate: 798 s (13.3 min): intro 1.4 s, 6 animated bumpers of 1.5 s, take length = last word + 1.0 s.
- **io** = where the camera stands. **rain** = visual rain: only `outdoor` scenes get rain drawn (rule 2). Indoor scenes during the storm get no drawn rain, only a muffled audio bed (CMO call below).
- **grade**: N normal, **FB flashback grade** (desaturated sepia, lifted blacks, cold shadows, grain, vignette), CCTV (mono monitor look, not a flashback), D dawn. A flashback scene is always followed by an N scene.
- Every mover is a thing that really moves, and carries a one-line reason. Dust, light and shadow are extra layers on every scene and are not counted as movers.
