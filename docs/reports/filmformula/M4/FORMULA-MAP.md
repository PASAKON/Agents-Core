## Length check
- 949 words ÷ 115 words/min = 8.3 min of voice; + 24 s marked silences + chapter holds + card = 10.3 min.
- The formula's 143 words/min would give 6.7 min of voice: too short for 10 min; a slower, darker read suits one night. Assumed until a voice take is timed.
