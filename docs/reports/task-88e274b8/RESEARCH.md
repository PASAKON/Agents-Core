## Ten concrete rules for these covers

1. **One visual question per cover.** Example: the [ธี่หยด](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=58nkqv8Pm9k) title identifies one story. Our C asks why the previous clerk is still inside the freezer; do not add rule sheets, clocks or a second scene.
2. **Make the face large enough to read, when a face is the hook.** Production target: visible face height roughly 30–50% of frame, equivalent to 28–47px at 94px height. This is our target, not a competitor measurement. The [Mr. Nightmare debut screenshot](https://medium.com/@asl2011/a-documentation-of-mr-nightmares-downfall-7c667fb0d32a) supplies a face-led example. C retains s61's wide eyes and frost; B retains the eyes above the counter.
3. **Preserve the eye-line and obstacle.** Keep text outside the eyes and fingers. In C, direct gaze through glass is the threat; in B, the counter conceals the face. Do not force all eyes onto a rule-of-thirds line. Character grouping in the [นนท์ EP21 proxy](https://www.bilibili.tv/th/video/4792164981542912) motivates keeping an expression visually distinct.
4. **Use at most four Thai lexical words, in two large lines.** C: คน / เก่า / ยัง / อยู่. A: ห้าม / เปิด / ตู้ / แช่. B: อย่า / มอง / ข้าง / ล่าง. Thai compounds may segment differently; each remains within four under this conservative segmentation. The two-word True Stories overlay in the [Nightmare example](https://medium.com/@asl2011/a-documentation-of-mr-nightmares-downfall-7c667fb0d32a) illustrates economy. No episode number, guillemets or em dash.
5. **Size letters for the final mobile tile.** Start at 110–153px font size at 1280 wide (14–20px nominal at 168 wide). Actual Thai ink height differs from font size. Use RAQM shaping, bold weight and enough vertical space for tone marks. The dense [Ghost Radio thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/58nkqv8Pm9k/maxresdefault.jpg) is a reason to prioritise its short title over small supporting information.
6. **Separate type by luminance, not just hue.** Put white and bright red lettering over a dark gradient; never over bright frost. Our #FF444C is deliberately brighter than dark burgundy. The red text on dark environmental art in the [Nightmare channel screenshot](https://flarecut.com/blog/channels/nightmare.jpg) is the reference; legibility still needs a local reduction check.
7. **Reserve red for words, keep skin cold and neutral.** Red/white is the requested type brand, not a requirement to paint the entire image red. The [Ghost Radio example](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/58nkqv8Pm9k/maxresdefault.jpg) separates title colour from a dark scene. Our source grade is blue-gray, removing warm/red skin globally without redrawing anatomy.
8. **Use a scene-based alternate, not three near-identical crops.** [Mr. Nightmare's environmental approach](https://flarecut.com/blog/channels/nightmare.jpg) supports a faceless option. A uses s63's freezer handle, hanging uniforms and corridor vanishing point. It trades facial immediacy for a forbidden-room question.
9. **Judge at 168x94 before choosing.** Export each at that size with Lanczos and view without enlarging. The face/obstacle, main phrase and contrast must survive; small costume detail need not. The [MrBallen grid](https://packapop.com/blogs/youtube-success-blog/30-halloween-youtube-video-ideas) is a reminder that discovery occurs among competing tiles. This is a proposed QA gate, not a measured outcome for the referenced grid.
10. **Test the promise against viewing, not a made-up quality score.** Keep title/video constant when comparing the three thumbnail concepts. [YouTube's native A/B tool](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400) compares up to three and selects by watch time. No external aesthetic score can establish Thai horror CTR or CEO acceptance. No upload/test was run in this task.

- Exact current top-channel ranking and current view totals were not verified. High views do not imply high CTR, and thumbnail versions may have changed.
