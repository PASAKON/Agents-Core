Mechanism (`lib/db.py:1360-1430`, `tools/delegate.py:2703-2726`, `tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:71, 346-368`):
- `db.acquire_lock` (`lib/db.py:1360-1370`) executes a non-atomic `SELECT owner, expires_at FROM locks WHERE key=?` followed by `INSERT OR REPLACE INTO locks`, and `db.find_conflicts` (`lib/db.py:1388-1421`) scans all tasks in `ACTIVE_STATUSES = ('pending', 'in_progress', 'rate_limited', 'conflict')` (`lib/db.py:1384-1385`) for prefix-overlapping `touches` (`_paths_overlap`, `lib/db.py:1378-1381`).

## 3. Structural Flaws in Current Lock & Queue Implementations (`file:line`)

1. **Non-atomic `db.acquire_lock` (`lib/db.py:1360-1370`):**
   - `lib/db.py:1362-1369` runs `SELECT owner, expires_at FROM locks WHERE key=?` and then `INSERT OR REPLACE INTO locks (key, owner, expires_at) VALUES (?, ?, ?)`. Two concurrent transactions that both pass the `SELECT` overwrite each other, unlike the atomic `INSERT INTO locks ... ON CONFLICT(key) DO NOTHING RETURNING` later used in `lib/router.py:319-321` and `docs/reports/task-42fdcda7/REPORT.md:31`.
2. **`status='conflict'` is included in `ACTIVE_STATUSES` (`lib/db.py:1384-1385`):**
   - `ACTIVE_STATUSES = ('pending', 'in_progress', 'rate_limited', 'conflict')` (`lib/db.py:1384-1385`). Because `find_conflicts` (`lib/db.py:1388-1421`) treats both `pending` and `conflict` tasks as active path holders, unspawned and already-rejected tasks block other tasks (`2026-10-27` 3-way deadlock on `task-503505d8` / `task-3a803be1` / `task-c25c07b6`).
3. **`tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:254` forgets `host_queue` and reads only local `.jsonl` files:**
   - Line 254 checks `if disk_queue.is_queued(t["id"]) or budget_queue.is_queued(t["id"]): continue` — missing `host_queue.is_queued(t["id"])` (`tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:64, 254`) and reading only the local machine's `state/*.jsonl` (`tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:27-29`).
4. **No ` flock ` in `tools/disk_queue.py:34-42`:**
   - Unlike `tools/host_queue.py:31-38` and `tools/budget_queue.py:25-33`, `tools/disk_queue.py:34-42` (`_load` / `_save`) performs an unlocked read-modify-write on `state/disk_queue.jsonl`.
5. **`runners/watchdog.py:1254` pops `budget_queue` before knowing `delegate_task` can spawn:**
   - `_drain_budget_queue` (`runners/watchdog.py:1244-1285`) calls `entry = budget_queue.pop()` first, and if `delegate_task` fails or hits a non-budget gate, the task is dropped from `budget_queue.jsonl`.
6. **`tools/home_lock.py:92-97` has no wait queue or wake mechanism:**
   - `acquire()` raises `HomeLockHeld` immediately on HTTP 409 from Console `/api/relay/locks`, and relies on a fixed TTL (default 1800s, `tools/home_lock.py:22`) rather than dead-holder PID detection.

## 4. Ranked Top 5 Resources to Move First

To achieve the goal of **one unified lease/queue system in the hub** (supporting kinds `mutex`, `pool`, and `rate`, where releasing a lease immediately wakes the next FIFO waiter and a dead holder PID/session automatically frees the lease), move these 5 resources first:

### Rank 1 — Task Path / `touches` Leases (`proj:<project>:path:<norm_path>`, kind: `mutex`)
- **Why #1 (Impact):** 49 conflict events across 33 tasks in 30 days, **11 tasks permanently killed by `gc_stale_tasks` after 60 minutes**, and two 3-way mutual deadlocks (`2026-10-27` and `2026-11-06`).
- **What changes:**
  1. Replace `SELECT` + `INSERT OR REPLACE` in `lib/db.py:1360-1370` and the `ACTIVE_STATUSES` scan in `lib/db.py:1384-1421` with atomic hub lease acquisition (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) held **only** by granted/running tasks (`in_progress`, `queued_remote`, `rate_limited`), never by `pending` or `queued` waiters.
  2. Expand `docs/reports/task-*/REPORT.md` and bare `REPORT.md` to `docs/reports/<task_id>/REPORT.md` before locking, and reject non-list string `touches` (`lib/db.py:1402`).
  3. On conflict, place the task in the hub waiter queue (`status='queued'` or lease waiter row, exempt from 60-min conflict GC in `tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:347-368`) and **wake the next FIFO waiter immediately inside `db.update_status` (`lib/db.py:1435-1461`)** when the holder enters `review`/`done`/`failed`/`cancelled` or its PID dies.

### Rank 2 — Host Capacity, Disk Band (`orange` + `red`), & Contabo CPU-Exception Slot (`host:<host>:workers` kind: `pool`, `host:<host>:disk` kind: `pool`, `host:contabo:cpu-exception` kind: `mutex`)
- **Why #2 (Impact):** **290+ `no_host:` retry events** across 39 tasks (>36 task-hours of waiting, including 16 hours and >180 retry events on `task-52c5c97a` alone) plus **23 unqueued `disk orange` refusals** (`tools/delegate.py:2539-2547`) that caused `task-897debd6` to be killed by 30-min GC (`tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:254`).
- **What changes:**
  1. Retire local `state/host_queue.jsonl` (`tools/host_queue.py:22`) and `state/disk_queue.jsonl` (`tools/disk_queue.py:25`) in favor of the hub lease waiter table (eliminating the `tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:254` bug where `host_queue` isn't checked).
  2. Route Contabo `disk orange` (`tools/delegate.py:2539-2547`) into the same disk waiter queue instead of leaving tasks unqueued in `pending`.
  3. Trigger immediate waiter wake when any worker on `<host>` finishes (`tools/worker_reap.py:close_dev`, `lib/router.py:330` `release_exception`) or when `tools/disk_steward.py` frees disk space, instead of re-running `delegate_task` every 300s (`runners/watchdog.py:1219, 1288`).

### Rank 3 — Runner Quota Buckets & Budget Queue (`quota:<bucket>` for `codex`, `agy-claude`, `agy-gemini`, `claude`, kind: `rate`)
- **Why #3 (Impact):** **200+ `tier_defer: queued for budget` retry events** across 25 tasks (including 98 retries over 7h 49m on `task-7124afcb`), **7 budget-queued tasks killed by `gc_stale_tasks`** due to per-host `state/budget_queue.jsonl` desync (`tools/budget_queue.py:18`, `tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:254`), and **28 wasted/failed spawns** from exhausted pins and unknown-quota routing.
- **What changes:**
  1. Move `state/budget_queue.jsonl` (`tools/budget_queue.py:18`) into the shared hub lease table so every host's GC (`tools/gc_stale_tasks.py:254`) sees queued tasks and never cancels them at 30 minutes.
  2. Store bucket `cooldown_until` / `resets_at` on the `rate` lease row so waiters sleep until `resets_at` or a `quota_reset` event (`tools/quota_reset.py:100-108, 149-150`) instead of popping and re-queuing every 300s (`runners/watchdog.py:1244-1285`).
  3. Re-evaluate `(runner, runner_model)` at lease-grant time (`tools/delegate.py:2065-2067, 2632-2633`) so a task woken onto a different runner never inherits a stale model.

### Rank 4 — Browser Operator & Chrome CDP Port / Profile Leases (`browser_operator:<host>` kind: `pool` + `chrome:<host>:<port>` kind: `mutex`/`pool`)
- **Why #4 (Impact):** 6 `browser cap` conflicts (`tools/delegate.py:2650-2667`) with **3 browser tasks permanently killed by 60-min GC** (`task-3b0b5401`, `task-fbf0afe1`, `task-dfe3f610`), plus live Chrome CDP clashes on `winbox:9224` (ChatGPT Plus foreground focus & Cloudflare cold tab, `docs/reports/last-bell-refs-r1/REPORT.md:49, 75`, `docs/reports/task-1251a3bf/REPORT.md:22, 24`) and `winbox:9226` (YouTube Studio active channel switched between Jade Tally and Ghost Rules mid-session, `docs/reports/ghostrules-ep3-ep4-schedule-20261114/REPORT.md:34, 48-49`).
- **What changes:**
  1. Replace the `status='conflict'` refusal in `tools/delegate.py:2650-2667` and the HTTP 409 `HomeLockHeld` exception in `tools/home_lock.py:92-97` with hub `pool` (`browser_operator:contabo` size 1, `browser_operator:winbox` size 2) and `mutex` (`chrome:winbox:9224`, `chrome:winbox:9226`, `chrome:mac:9223`) leases with FIFO wait+wake and dead-PID release.
  2. Require `tools/chatgpt_images.py:960`, `tools/flow_shoot.py:64`, and `scripts/browser/ghostrules_yt_upload.py:55` to acquire the `chrome:<host>:<port>` lease before attaching over CDP.

### Rank 5 — Local Video Render Mutex & Per-PH Worker Pool (`render:<host>` kind: `mutex` + `ph:<owner>:workers` kind: `pool`)
- **Why #5 (Impact):** Parallel Contabo renders caused swap thrashing (10–25 min/window vs 1–3 min serially) and OOM kills (`docs/reports/task-cc55e620/REPORT.md:45, 54`), while the Linux-only `/opt/MoonieXHQ/Work/.bl-render.lock` (`tools/ilag_cut.py:62, 988-1016`) blocked sandboxed worker `task-d4c1f234` (`docs/reports/task-d4c1f234/REPORT.md:47`); meanwhile, 6 tasks hit `Project Head worker cap: 2/2 workers live` (`tools/delegate.py:2303-2334, 2577-2589`) and were left unqueued in `pending`.
- **What changes:**
  1. Replace `/opt/MoonieXHQ/Work/.bl-render.lock` (`tools/ilag_cut.py:62`) with a hub-backed `render:<host>` mutex lease (with PID liveness and RAM gate) that works across Linux, macOS, Windows, and read-only worktree sandboxes.
  2. Convert `_ph_cap_refusal` (`tools/delegate.py:2303-2334`) and `unmet_dependencies` (`tools/delegate.py:2679-2696`) from unqueued `pending` refusals into hub waiter queues that auto-wake when a sibling PH worker or prerequisite task completes.
