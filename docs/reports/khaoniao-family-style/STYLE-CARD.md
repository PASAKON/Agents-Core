## 1 · The style (CEO brief, 2026-09-30)

> "แนวการ์ตูนคล้ายชินจัง แต่เป็น 3D ที่มีแสงเงาสวยงาม · Design เป็นเอกลักษณ์ทุกๆ คน"

- Funny family-cartoon proportions: head ≈ 40 % of body height, short limbs, small hands, simple expressive eyes.
  Shin-chan is a **feel**, not a model: no thick short eyebrows, no round potato head, no red-shirt boy.
- Rendered as a high-end 3D animated film: warm key light, cool rim light, soft global illumination,
  a little subsurface scattering on skin, visible fabric and fur detail, soft contact shadow under the feet.
- Not photoreal, not anime, not flat 2D. Saturated but natural colour.
- **Every character must pass the silhouette test** (all black, still told apart). Four shape languages:

| | Shape | Why it reads |
|---|---|---|
| Mimi | fluffy mop with two huge drop ears | ears are the signature |
| Mother | dumpling / pear, widest at the hips, shortest human | soft, low centre |
| Brother | tall inverted triangle, square shoulders, square jaw | "power suit" block |
| Sister | angular: sharp chin, ponytail standing up like a flame | diagonals, nothing round |

Colour tones (guides only; an image model cannot hit a hex, so judge by eye):
Mimi coat `#1B1B1F`, silver tufts `#CFCFD2`, chest `#F2EEE6`, tan `#C9A27A` · Mother turmeric `#F6D365`, chili `#D9382B`,
cream `#FFF3D6` · Brother navy `#14233F`, gold `#D4A73A`, mint `#9ED8C4` · Sister black `#101014`, crimson `#C4122F`, silver `#B9BCC4`.

## 2 · The cast

Small, long-haired, fluffy; wavy **black** coat; silver-white eyebrow tufts; silver beard with tan under the chin; white
chest patch and white bib; cream-tan on two paws; long drop ears with silver tips; dark brown eyes; a little pink tongue.
Older-looking face. Stylised: rounder, bigger ears and eyes, but **every marking stays**. Never brown, never all-black.

### แม่ (Mother) — imagined · sweet, dreamy, "ซื่อบื้อ", genius cook
Short and round; big sleepy half-closed warm eyes, dot nose, rosy cheeks, a slow open-mouth "เอ๋?" face. Short black hair
with a few grey strands in a messy bun **with a wooden ladle pushed through it**. Faded oversized pale-yellow T-shirt,
floral sarong-print trousers, half apron with chili stains, pink rubber slippers. Faint aroma steam curls around her.
- Signature: the ladle; she is clueless about phones and gossip, and unbeatable at the stove.
- **Voice:** woman ~55, soft mid-high, slow, breathy, vowels stretched ("เอ๋ออ~"). Lines: "กินข้าวยังลูก?" · "เอ๊ะ… ใช่เหรอ".
