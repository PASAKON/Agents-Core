## ⚠️ Two things the CEO needs to see before anyone treats a date as final

**A. PT vs UTC for the final-submission deadline — a possible 7-hour gap.**

- Official Rules (Rules tab): *"Final submission deadline	Sep 14, 2026, 11:59 PM UTC"* — `?tab=rules`
- Same page, Create-in-Public Rules → "Continuity": *"A Final Submission may be re-submitted, replacing the prior Final on the same Project, at any time up to September 14, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT."* — `?tab=rules`

One clause names the deadline in **UTC**, the other in **PT** (Pacific
Time). If PT is meant literally, 11:59 PM PT on Sep 14 is **06:59 AM UTC
Sep 15** — seven hours *after* the UTC deadline quoted elsewhere. Treat
**Sep 14, 2026, 11:59 PM UTC** (§1 table below) as the binding number and
finish well ahead of it; do not rely on the PT wording as extra runway
without CEO/Higgsfield confirmation.

**B. Screening-window dates disagree between the Official Rules and the blog.**

- Official Rules (Rules tab): *"Screening & shortlist compilation	Sep 15 – Oct 5, 2026"*
- Blog (`/blog/higgsfield-global-film-festival`): *"Screening & shortlist compilation	Oct 5 – Oct 15, 2026"*

These do not overlap at all. The Official Rules page states it governs over
marketing material ("If anything on the website or in marketing materials
conflicts with this document, these Official Rules govern" — §2 Overview,
quoted below), so **Sep 15 – Oct 5, 2026** should be treated as authoritative,
but this is worth a direct confirmation given how far apart the two ranges
are.

## 2) The Minimal Viable Submission (MVS)

From the Official Rules, Section 4, verbatim:

> "Every Entry must include, before the deadline:
> Final video — with the official Higgsfield watermark and packshot.
> Social media post — a valid public link on Instagram, YouTube, X
> (Twitter), or Reddit, showing your final film with watermark and packshot
> intact. Posting is a mandatory step, not optional." — `?tab=rules`

> "Project covers, briefs, and additional materials are optional but
> encouraged — they help discovery and your chances in the Audience Choice
> Award. Any Entry missing an MVS component will be marked incomplete." —
> `?tab=rules`

From the blog: *"A film only counts if it clears the Minimal Viable
Submission: the final video with Higgsfield's watermark and packshot, and a
public post of that same video on Instagram, YouTube, X, or Reddit."* —
`/blog/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

The Definitions section (Official Rules §1) defines it as: *"Minimal Viable
Submission (MVS) — the mandatory components listed in Section 4 that an

For a Create-in-Public project to become *eligible to publish at all* (a
separate, lower threshold, not the MVS): *"Your project needs at least 10
generations inside it and a video cut that's 15 seconds or longer. Add a
poster and a short logline, and you're ready to go public."* —
`/global-film-festival` FAQ

## 3) Social post rules

**Is it mandatory? Yes.**

> "Social media post — a valid public link on Instagram, YouTube, X
> (Twitter), or Reddit, showing your final film with watermark and packshot
> intact. Posting is a mandatory step, not optional." — Official Rules §4,
> `?tab=rules`

FAQ confirmation (General tab): *"Do I have to post my film on social
media? Yes, it's a required part of submitting. Post your finished film
(with watermark and packshot) as a public link on Instagram, YouTube, X, or
Reddit, from a public account, and keep it up through the end of the
festival. The jury never judges social performance; it's purely a
submission requirement."* — `/contests/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

**Which platforms:** Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), or Reddit — stated
identically across all three primary sources (Official Rules §4, the
General-tab FAQ above, and the blog).

**Must it be public?** Yes, explicitly:

> "Your post and account must both be public. The post must be published
> from a public account, not a private, protected, restricted, close-
> friends, or followers-only account, and must be viewable without logging
> in or requesting to follow. Please double-check this before you submit —
> posting a valid link from an account that is set to private (or that you
> set to private later) is the most common reason we mark an otherwise-
> strong Entry non-eligible." — Official Rules §4, `?tab=rules`

**Required hashtag / @mention / caption text / title format?** **NOT
STATED.** No source names a required hashtag, mention, caption text, or
title format for the post.

**Deadline of the post — same as submission?** The rules do not give the
post a separate deadline number; instead they require it to be up *at*
submission and to *stay* up:

> "The post must remain publicly available through the end of the
> Festival: deleting or hiding it may make your Entry non-eligible.
> Involuntary removals — such as an account suspension or a platform action
> outside your control — are excused, provided the link was valid at the
> time of submission." — Official Rules §4, `?tab=rules`

**Is the jury judging the post itself?** No: *"The social media post is a
submission requirement, not a judging criterion — the jury evaluates the
film itself, never its social performance, views, or engagement."* —
Official Rules §4, `?tab=rules`

## 4) Watermark / packshot

**Automatic, or must the editor add it?** The sources are not perfectly
uniform — quoted exactly, both ways it appears:

> "Where do I get the official watermark and packshot, and how do I apply
> them? Both are added automatically when your project is created, and
> they live in the Watermarks folder." — General-tab FAQ,
> `/contests/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

> "The official Higgsfield watermark and packshot are available inside
> your submission project." — blog, `/blog/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

> "Watermark	The official Higgsfield watermark and packshot are available
> inside your submission project. They must appear on your final video and
> on the version you post to social media." — Official Rules §4,
> `?tab=rules`

For Create-in-Public specifically: *"Publication is initiated via the
Submit control inside the festival card on the Project's Cinema Studio
screen. The Project transitions to Published WIP and appears in public
feeds and, if eligible, in the Race view. The official Higgsfield watermark
and packshot are applied automatically to every video published in the
Cinema Studio."* — Create-in-Public Rules, "Publishing a Project",
`?tab=rules`

**Reading this together:** the watermark/packshot assets are placed
automatically in a "Watermarks folder" inside the project, and Higgsfield's
own CIP publish flow applies them automatically to WIP videos — but the
MVS wording ("Final video — *with* the official Higgsfield watermark and
packshot") and "They must appear on your final video" put the *obligation*
on the entrant to make sure they appear on the exported final cut and on
the social post. **Open question:** whether the Submit/export flow for the
*Final* film (as opposed to a WIP cut) burns them in automatically or
requires manually inserting the Watermarks-folder asset — NOT explicitly
stated. Flagged again in §12.

**Is a film without them disqualified?** Not stated as an automatic
disqualification in those words, but it is a named MVS component ("Final
video — with the official Higgsfield watermark and packshot") and "Any
Entry missing an MVS component will be marked incomplete." — Official Rules
§4, `?tab=rules`

## 5) Length and content rules

**Runtime — minimum/maximum:**

> "Runtime	Minimum 3:00 minutes — a hard requirement. We recommend up to
> 5:00 minutes; longer films are fully eligible. Submissions must be
> uploaded by 11:59 PM UTC on the deadline day." — Official Rules §4,
> `?tab=rules`

> "Runtime	Minimum 3:00 minutes (hard requirement). Recommended maximum
> 5:00 minutes — longer films remain fully eligible." — Official Rules "At
> a Glance", `?tab=rules`

No maximum runtime cap is stated anywhere — 5:00 is a recommendation only.

**Format / aspect ratio / language:**

> "Format	MP4 or MOV, up to 4K." / "Aspect ratio	16:9 or 21:9." — Official
> Rules §4, `?tab=rules`

> "Language	Any language, real or fictional, with English subtitles or
> voice-over." — Official Rules §4 / "At a Glance", `?tab=rules`

**Subtitles/captions — burned-in vs. file, specific language rule beyond
English?** **NOT STATED.** The rule only says "English subtitles or
voice-over" is required when the film's dialogue is in a non-English
language; no source specifies whether subtitles must be burned into the
video or may be a separate file, or names any language other than English
as acceptable for the subtitle/voice-over track itself.

**Create-in-Public publish threshold (separate, lower bar than the final

before September 12, it has at least one work-in-progress cut plus a
poster and a project brief on the page."* — `/global-film-festival` FAQ

## 6) Tools / AI-only rules

**Must every shot be generated inside Higgsfield?**

> "AI generation must happen on Higgsfield. AI video and image generation
> must take place inside the dedicated Cinema Studio submission project on
> the higgsfield.ai platform. Higgsfield aggregates models from multiple
> companies — any model available through Higgsfield is eligible.
> Generating AI video or images outside of Higgsfield — on any other AI
> platform or third-party AI tool — is not allowed." — Official Rules §4,
> `?tab=rules`

FAQ confirmation: *"Can I generate AI video or images somewhere other than
Higgsfield? No. All AI video and image generation has to happen on
Higgsfield, inside your Film Festival Cinema Studio project. Using any
other AI platform or third-party AI tool for visuals isn't allowed."* —
General-tab FAQ, `/contests/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

**Are external edits allowed?** Yes, explicitly, for non-AI-generation
purposes:

> "Traditional (non-AI) tools are allowed. Traditional 3D, animation,
> editing, compositing, and design software — such as Blender, After
> Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Photoshop, and similar — may be used
> freely at any stage of your film, provided they are not used to generate
> new AI imagery. Content produced in these tools counts as your own
> hand-made work." — Official Rules §4, `?tab=rules`

> "External editing rules. External tools may be used for cutting and
> assembly, color grading, precise mask-based retouching, titles,
> transitions, compositing, and traditional (non-AI) 3D and animation." —
> Official Rules §4, `?tab=rules`

FAQ: *"Can I use tools like Blender, After Effects, DaVinci, Premiere, or
Photoshop? Yes. Traditional (non-AI) software is welcome at any stage —
editing, compositing, color, titles, 3D, animation."* — General-tab FAQ,
`/contests/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

**Audio — must it be AI-generated? Can it be made outside the window?**

> "All audio must be AI-generated. Music, voice, lipsync, dialogue, and
> sound design in your film must be produced with AI audio tools.
> Human-composed music, licensed stock music, recorded acoustic
> instruments, live vocals, human-recorded voice-over, and any other
> non-AI-generated audio are not allowed — including tracks you own or
> hold a license for. AI-generated audio may have been created at any
> point, before or during the Generation Window. Every audio file you use
> must be uploaded into your submission project, and you must be able to
> identify the AI tool used to generate it on request." — Official Rules
> §4, `?tab=rules`

**Can prior Higgsfield generations (made before the contest) be used as
finished footage?**

> "The generation has to happen during the competition window, and it all
> lives inside your Cinema Studio Film Festival project. Your film's video
> and images are generated on Higgsfield within that window, so you can't
> drop in generations you made before the festival as finished footage.
> Two things are exempt from the window: your own reference inputs (see
> below), and audio, which can be made anytime, but has to be
> AI-generated." — General-tab FAQ,
> `/contests/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

**Own references allowed?**

> "Can I use my own images, footage or characters as references? Yes.
> Feel free to guide a generation with your own material, Soul characters,
> 3D models, and the like. They don't need to be created during the
> competition window. The hard rule is likeness: no real person's face or
> voice in your references, and that includes your own. Films should
> feature only fully fictional AI-generated characters and voices." —
> General-tab FAQ, `/contests/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

**Official Higgsfield material exemption:** *"Official Higgsfield material
and integrations are exempt. Official Higgsfield open-source packs,
Higgsfield projects such as HellGrind, Academy drops and similar may be
used freely."* — Official Rules §4, `?tab=rules`

## 7) Eligibility, teams, multiple entries

**Who can enter:**

> "Who can submit an Entry. You must be a registered Higgsfield user — an
> account in good standing — both when your submission project is created
> and at the time of submission. A paid subscription is not required to
> enter or win." — Official Rules §2, `?tab=rules`

> "All participants must be 18 or older, or the age of majority in their
> jurisdiction, whichever is higher." — Official Rules §2, `?tab=rules`

> "The Festival is open worldwide, except where prohibited by law or where
> sanctions make participation or payouts impossible. Sanctioned persons
> and residents of embargoed territories are not eligible." — Official
> Rules §2, `?tab=rules`

**Excluded persons:** *"employees, officers, and directors of Higgsfield
and its affiliates; members of the jury; anyone involved in screening or
winner selection; and the immediate family or household members of any of
the above are not eligible to enter or win. Higgsfield creative partners
and contractors who are not involved in judging or selection may
participate. Persons subject to US sanctions or listed on a designated
nationals list are not eligible."* — Official Rules §2, `?tab=rules`

**Teams:**

> "You may enter solo or as a team of up to 4 people. Team composition is
> locked at submission. Every team member must individually meet all
> eligibility requirements." — Official Rules §2, `?tab=rules`

> "The Team Leader is the owner of the submission project and the team's
> payee." — Official Rules §2, `?tab=rules`

**Can one account submit MORE THAN ONE film?**

> "Entry limits. There is no cap on the number of Entries a participant or
> team may submit, but: Each Entry must be a separate, standalone film. The
> same film — or substantially the same film — may not be entered more
> than once. Higgsfield may treat near-duplicate Entries as a single Entry
> for judging purposes." — Official Rules §3, `?tab=rules`

But: *"One prize per participant or team. A participant or team may
receive only one Festival prize in total across all their Entries —
whether that's a Track Prize (1st, 2nd, or 3rd Place), the Audience Choice
Award, or an Honorable Mention. Prizes do not stack."* — Official Rules §3,
`?tab=rules`

**Can a film be replaced/updated after submitting?**

> "A Final Submission may be re-submitted, replacing the prior Final on
> the same Project, at any time up to September 14, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT."
> — Create-in-Public Rules, "Final Submission and Continuity", `?tab=rules`
> *(note the PT wording flagged in the warning box above)*

For a project that was published as a Create-in-Public WIP, continuity is
required on the Final: *"If a Project was published as WIP, the Final must
be a good-faith continuation of the WIP Cuts previously published on the
Project page. Cosmetic revisions, restructuring, extension, and creative
iteration between WIP and Final are expected and permitted. Wholesale
substitution of an unrelated film for the WIP shown throughout August is a
material Rules violation and grounds for disqualification."* —
Create-in-Public Rules, "Final Submission and Continuity", `?tab=rules`

**Depicting characters under 18:**

> "Does a story with characters under 18 disqualify me? No, coming-of-age
> and family stories are welcome. Child characters have to be entirely
> fictional and kept within the content and child-safety rules, see the
> Prohibited Content section for the specifics. Real children can't be
> depicted, and their voices can't be used." — General-tab FAQ,
> `/contests/higgsfield-global-film-festival`

Full Prohibited Content clause: *"Depictions of children (under 18).
Fictional AI-generated characters that appear to be children are permitted
only when all of the following conditions are met: (i) the character is
entirely fictional and does not resemble any identifiable real child; (ii)
the character does not appear in any sexualized, romantic, suggestive,
exploitative, or otherwise harmful context; (iii) the character is not
depicted as a victim or perpetrator of graphic violence, self-harm, or
abuse; (iv) the depiction complies with all applicable laws, including
child protection laws in the participant's jurisdiction and in the United
States. Real children — including your own, your family's, or any other
identifiable child — may not be depicted, and their voices may not be
used." — Official Rules §5, `?tab=rules`

## 8) Create in Public track

> "Publishing is optional, but a private project gets none of this until

> "Going public is optional: you can keep your project private and submit

> "Do I have to publish early, or can I stay private? Either way works.

> when it's ready, which is a perfectly normal way to enter. You can also
> start private and publish later whenever you feel ready." —
> `/global-film-festival` FAQ

**What it requires and by when:**

> "What do I need before I can publish my project? Your project needs at
> least 10 generations inside it and a video cut that's 15 seconds or
> longer. Add a poster and a short logline, and you're ready to go public."
> — `/global-film-festival` FAQ

> "How do I publish my project? Open your project in Cinema Studio, click
> Submit in the top-right corner, and select Submit to Create in Public.
> To go live, your project needs at least 10 generations and a video of at
> least 15 seconds." — `/global-film-festival` FAQ

**Race Eligibility (Official Rules-linked Create-in-Public rules), in
full:**

> "A Project participates in the Race only if all of the following are
> simultaneously true: (i) the Project is in the Published WIP State and
> remains publicly viewable; (ii) the First Publication Date is on or
> before September 12, 2026, 11:59 PM PT; (iii) the Project contains at
> least one WIP Cut, a poster, and a project brief on its public page; (iv)
> the Team has activated the Race participation toggle and holds at least
> one available Slot." — Create-in-Public Rules, "Race Eligibility and
> Slots", `?tab=rules`

**Slots (cap on concurrent race participation):**

> "Slots. Each Participant and each Team may hold a maximum of two (2)
> active Slots in the Race at any given time... A Slot may be reassigned to
> a different Project at any time up to and including September 12, 2026,
> 11:59 PM PT; reassignment removes the previously occupied Project from
> the Race and freezes its accumulated votes. After that moment, Slot
> assignments are locked." — Create-in-Public Rules, `?tab=rules`

FAQ version: *"How many projects can I enter in the race? Two at a time.
You can publish as many projects as you want, but only two can hold a race
slot at once. A slot can be moved to a different project anytime up to
September 12 at 11:59 PM PT, which pulls the old project out of the race
and freezes the likes it had gathered. After that deadline, slots are
locked."* — `/global-film-festival` FAQ

**Shortlist mechanics:**

> "Race Shortlist. At 11:59 PM PT on September 12, 2026, Higgsfield ranks
> Published WIP Projects that meet Section 5 eligibility and adds up to
> fifty (50) of them to the Festival shortlist. In the event of a tie in
> ranking, the Project with the earlier First Publication Date ranks
> higher... Fifty is a ceiling, not a floor: if fewer qualifying Projects
> clear the compliance review, the Race Shortlist consists of the
> qualifying Projects only." — Create-in-Public Rules, `?tab=rules`

FAQ version: *"What is the deadline for publishing? Community reactions
close on September 12 at 11:59 PM PT, and up to 50 top-ranked projects are
added to the festival shortlist. If two are tied, the one that published
earlier ranks higher."* — `/global-film-festival` FAQ

**Voting mechanics:** *"Only users with an active paid Higgsfield
subscription may vote, and no Participant may vote on a Project on which
the Participant or any of their Team members is listed as an author. On
any given Project, an eligible voter may cast either one (1) up-vote or one
(1) down-vote — not both simultaneously."* — Create-in-Public Rules,
`?tab=rules`

**Team Picks / Early Picks:** *"At any time during August 2026, Higgsfield's
creative team may designate a Published WIP Project as an Early Pick
candidate for the Festival shortlist, independently of the Race and without
regard to any ranking or engagement value."* — Create-in-Public Rules,
`?tab=rules`

**Grants:** *"No application, discretionary... The maximum aggregate amount
awarded to any single Project is up to one hundred thousand (100,000)
credits... Grant credits... are attached to the specific Project for which
the Grant was issued and may not be spent on any other Project or on
generic account activity; (ii) are non-transferable; (iii) expire on
September 14, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT; unused credits are not refunded or
carried over."* — Create-in-Public Rules, "Grants", `?tab=rules`

**Is this the same as the Audience Choice Award?** No: *"Create-in-Public
voting happens during the generation window and decides who gets added to
the shortlist. The likes you gather here do not carry over and do not
count as Audience Choice votes."* — `/global-film-festival` FAQ

## 12) Open questions (things the rules do not say)

Every item below was checked against all four sources and is genuinely
**NOT STATED**, or stated ambiguously enough to need a direct confirmation:

1. **No required hashtag, @mention, caption text, or title format** for the
   mandatory social media post — only "public link... showing your final
   film with watermark and packshot intact" is required.
2. **No separate deadline for the social post distinct from the submission
   deadline** — it must be valid at submission time and stay up "through
   the end of the Festival," but no independent post-by date is given.
3. **Whether an "unlisted" YouTube video satisfies "public"** is not
   explicitly addressed. The rule requires "a valid public link," a
   "public account, not a private, protected, restricted, close-friends,
   or followers-only account," and content "viewable without logging in or
   requesting to follow" — an unlisted video is technically viewable via
   direct link without login, but is not conventionally "public." **Confirm
   with Higgsfield or the CEO before relying on an unlisted upload.**
4. **Whether the watermark/packshot is burned in automatically on the
   Final-film Submit/export flow, or must be manually added from the
   Watermarks folder** — sources say both "added automatically when your
   project is created" (implying it's placed as an asset in-project) and
   "applied automatically to every video published in the Cinema Studio"
   (stated specifically for the Create-in-Public WIP publish flow, not
   explicitly for the Final-submission export). See §4 above.
5. **Subtitles/captions — burned-in vs. a separate file**, and whether any
   language besides English is acceptable for the subtitle/voice-over
   track. Only "Any language works, with English subtitles or voice-over"
   is stated.
6. **The exact submission-form field labels** (e.g., is there a literal
   "post link" box, a "title" field, a "synopsis" field?) beyond "mandatory
   metadata and tags" — not enumerated on any of the four pages read; only
   visible by opening the actual Cinema Studio Submit flow, which this
   read-only task was not authorized to do.
7. **The exact Race/Community-vote weighting formula** — explicitly
   withheld: "Race positions are determined by Higgsfield based on
   community engagement, including up-votes, down-votes, followers, and
   comments. The specific weighting is set by Higgsfield and is not
   disclosed." — Create-in-Public Rules, `?tab=rules`
8. **Exact winner-announcement date** — only "End week of October 2026
   (exact date to be confirmed)" is given everywhere it appears.
9. **The PT-vs-UTC discrepancy on the final deadline**, and the **Sep
   15–Oct 5 vs. Oct 5–Oct 15 screening-window discrepancy** — flagged in
   the warning box at the top of this document; both need a direct
   confirmation rather than being resolved by inference.
10. **Whether the Cinema Studio Submit-flow's literal field for the social
    post is validated automatically** (e.g., checked for public
    reachability at submit time) or is a self-attested text box — not
    stated.
