## Rules and editorial contract

The eight CEO rules in the live PH brief apply. Every named event has a transcript anchor; no invented Foley, music stingers, thunder, or gratuitous whooshes. Three movers per scene with reasons below; architectural shells remain locked. Any door or chair movement is limited to its narrated action. Paper depth and clean silhouettes; no headless cutouts, red sky or moon. Camera motion does not count as a mover. Hard cuts between unique scene-action plates. Never reuse a plate, including in recalls.

Five 2.4 s animated Roman chapter cards and one 3.2 s ending graphic use unique paper graphics; remove inaccurate source clock labels. Reveal, settle and exit animation, no freeze-frame bumpers. End on the completed escape and final warning, no sequel tease. No EP or episode/case ordinal in video, cover, title or description. Covers later: white normal type and larger red emphasis only; no blush, Thai guillemets or em dash.

Rain particles only in open air, masked outside glazing and roofs. Interior rain is an attenuated, low-pass exterior bed. Loop >=1.5 s crossfades and scan M2 mix; no thunder in this story. Golden dawn replaces storm bed at c21. Flashbacks use desaturated sepia and return to cool paper grade at the next marked present scene.

All shot plates below are unique PLANNED assets, not files already made. Existing plate/cutout paths are reference inventory in JSON. Allocate a new frame for each beat, not a renamed crop counted as a unique plate. Preserve Karn (26), landlady (70), room geography, hinge sides, four shirts plus fifth figure, and wardrobe closed before opening one inch.
