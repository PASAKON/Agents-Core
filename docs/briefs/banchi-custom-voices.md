# «บัญชี» — custom voices, one per character

Five characters. For each: pick the named base preset, paste the English
description, preview, wait, name it exactly as written, save.

**The base preset is already chosen. Do not substitute one.** The bases were
picked to put the three men in three different registers — the last shoot
measured them at 132, 148 and 150 Hz and the CEO could not tell them apart.

---

## 1. `@lung_somchai` — สมชาย, 58

**Base preset: `Algenib`** (Male, gravelly, low pitch) — keep the low register.

**Name it exactly:** `Algenib @lung_somchai`

**Description (paste verbatim):**
```
A 58-year-old Thai man who has run a small noodle shop his whole life. A low,
gravelly voice, worn thin by thirty years of heat and smoke and early mornings.
He speaks slowly and never finishes a sentence in a hurry. There is warmth
underneath, but it is tired warmth - the voice of a man carrying something he
has decided not to say out loud. He never raises his voice, not even when
frightened; under pressure he gets quieter and flatter, not louder. A slight
roughness at the bottom of the register, like a voice used all day every day.
Not theatrical, not gruff, not stern. An ordinary exhausted father.
```

## 2. `@nong_daeng` — ต้น, 24

**Base preset: `Fenrir`** (Male, excitable, younger pitch) — chosen ONLY for the
younger register. The description below removes the excitability.

**Name it exactly:** `Fenrir @nong_daeng`

**Description (paste verbatim):**
```
A 24-year-old Thai man, a year out of university and still without work. A
clear, light, youthful voice, noticeably higher and thinner than a
middle-aged man's. He is polite and quick when serving customers, and careful
and a little tight when talking to his father, holding something back. Not
excitable, not bubbly, not energetic - calm, level, slightly flattened by
repeated disappointment. When he is alone he sounds younger and more defeated
than when anyone can hear him. Clean articulation, no rasp, no gravel.
```

## 3. `@grandma_pranom` — ประนอม, 79

**Base preset: `Vindemiatrix`** (Female, gentle, mid pitch) — gentler starting
point than the preset she has now, which the CEO hears as far too young.

**Name it exactly:** `Vindemiatrix @grandma_pranom`

**Description (paste verbatim):**
```
A 79-year-old Thai woman, seriously ill, bedridden, on oxygen. A very old
voice: thin, papery, breathy, with almost no volume behind it. She runs out of
air after two or three syllables and has to stop. Words come out slow and
incomplete, often trailing away before the end. There is a faint tremor in the
sustained sounds. Gentle and lucid - she understands everything, she simply
cannot push the air. She must sound decades older than a middle-aged woman:
frail, quiet, and short of breath, never bright, never clear, never youthful.
```

## 4. `@cop_wit` — วิทย์, 32

**Base preset: `Achird`** (Male, friendly, mid pitch) — keep the middle register,
between the father's low and the son's younger.

**Name it exactly:** `Achird @cop_wit`

**Description (paste verbatim):**
```
A 32-year-old Thai man, warm and easy in company, with a steadiness underneath
that he does not advertise. A mid-pitched male voice with a little more body and
resonance than a young man's - settled, unhurried, comfortable. He talks to an
older shopkeeper the way you talk to someone who fed you as a child: relaxed,
respectful, faintly amused. He does not perform authority. When he becomes
serious the voice slows and drops slightly rather than hardening. Clearly a
different man from a 58-year-old and from a 24-year-old: fuller than the young
one, higher and smoother than the old one.
```

## 5. `@lender_cherd` — เชิด, 45

**Base preset: `Umbriel`** (Male, smooth, lower pitch).
This also settles a standing question: the character currently carries `algieba`,
which does not match the production record. Build the custom voice on `Umbriel`
and bind it; that supersedes both.

**Name it exactly:** `Umbriel @lender_cherd`

**Description (paste verbatim):**
```
A 45-year-old Thai money lender. A smooth, controlled, lower-pitched voice,
pleasant on the surface and completely unhurried. He sounds friendly - he is
friendly, right up until he is not. There is no aggression in the sound at all;
the threat is in how calm and patient it stays while it says something
frightening. He never shouts, never hurries, never sounds angry. A salesman's
warmth with nothing behind it.
```
