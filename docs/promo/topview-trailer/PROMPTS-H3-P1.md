**These prompts are for our H3 only.** TopView's Wan3 has no named Elements: references are uploaded per generation
and cited by position (`@Image 1`, `@Image 2`, or `<<<Image1>>>`), at most 10 images
(`docs/promo/TOPVIEW-WAN3-REFERENCES.md`). The Wan3 pass rewrites each @Name as its position in that shot's upload list.

## Before you render: two H3 rules that decide whether a shot works

1. **Every reference must be ONE picture, never a sheet.** H3 renders a multi-panel reference sheet AS a grid
   video, and no prompt wording prevents it (tested on the pod 2026-09-03, memory `reference-h3-grid-ref-fails`).
   So the character, prop and creature Elements below use SINGLE-PANEL CROPS (`ref-*.png`), not the design sheets.
2. **At most 9 images per shot, and the first @ mentioned claims the slots first.** Each prompt lists its most
   important Element first and uses 7 or fewer.

## M1 · The village at morning · 6 s · DAY

@Village at morning on an ocean planet with no land: giant mangrove-like trees stand in glass-clear turquoise water, wooden houses grown over with glowing neon-green grass, a huge moon hanging close in a bright blue-white sky. @Villagers climb the rope ladders and wooden pegs up the roots; glowing manta-like creatures rest at the driftwood dock. Wide shot from water level, slow push in. Calm, colourful, peaceful. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M2 · The empty net · 6 s · DAY

@Strong, a big green amphibious man, stands on the driftwood manta dock of @Dock and hauls a woven net up out of glass-clear shallow water like @ClearWater. The net comes up completely empty. Below, the pale sandy seabed is bare, not a single fish. Two of @Villagers watch behind him, their gill frills drooping. Medium side shot, still camera. Bright blue-white daylight, a heavy silence. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M3 · The elder chooses the child · 6 s · DAY

On a wooden platform high in @Village, @Elder, an old amphibious man, kneels and ties @Necklace around the neck of @Young, a small child of the same species. The child's gill frills glow faint gold. The elder says quietly: "The fish went past the line. Only a child's eyes can still find them." Close two-shot, gentle handheld. Soft blue-white morning light. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M4 · Leaving · 6 s · DAY

@Manta, a glowing manta-like sea creature with a hand-built driftwood seat on its back, glides away from the dock of @Dock over glass-clear turquoise water. On the seat: @Strong at the front steering with @Pole, @Elder and @Young behind him, @Lantern glowing gold, hanging from the seat. The village of giant trees shrinks behind them under the huge close moon. Wide tracking shot from behind. Bright, colourful, hopeful. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M5 · The glass creatures · 6 s · TURNING

Underwater in open blue water turning violet-grey: @Manta glides with @Young on its back through a field of enormous glass-clear drifting creatures, @GlassSpiral, @GlassHalo, @GlassBloom and @GlassCathedral, each as big as a house, their see-through bodies shimmering with rainbow light. The child reaches out a hand towards one. Wide underwater shot, slow drift. Wonder, with a first hint of unease. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M6 · The storm pillars · 6 s · TURNING

@Manta carries @Strong, @Elder and @Young between thin, perfectly straight storm pillars of swirling cloud that rise from the sea up beyond the top of the sky, as in @Pillars. The air is still; a small whirlpool turns at each pillar's base. The three riders look up, tiny. Low camera near the water, tilting up. Violet-grey bruised sky, dim moon, silver light. Otherworldly and silent. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M7 · The line · 6 s · TURNING

High angle over @Line: a razor-sharp line across the sea, glass-clear turquoise water on one side, pitch-black water under a purple storm on the other. @Manta stops right at the line with @Elder, @Young and @Strong on its back. The elder says: "No one has crossed it in a hundred years. Something sleeps out there." Slow crane down towards them. Silence, a threshold. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M8 · Crossing · 5 s · DARK

@Manta crosses the line from clear water into the pitch-black sea of @BlackSea. Violet lightning cracks over the black water and rain begins. On the seat, @Lantern flickers and dies, becoming @LanternDark. @Strong grips the steering pole; @Elder and @Young hold on. Side shot at water level. Dark, with violet flashes. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 5 seconds.

## M9 · The empty sea · 5 s · DARK

Close-up of @Young leaning over the edge of the seat on @Manta, staring down into the pitch-black water of @BlackSea. Nothing moves in the water, not one fish. The child's gill frills drain from gold to pale white. The child whispers: "There's nothing here... not one." Violet lightning flickers on the wet face. Handheld, close. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 5 seconds.

## M10 · The giant wave · 6 s · DARK

A colossal wave as tall as a mountain rises against the wind, like @Waves, glowing faintly cyan and magenta inside, curling over @Manta. @Strong shouts "Hold on!" and drives @Pole down; the manta dives under the wave with @Elder and @Young clinging on. Low angle looking up, the camera shakes. Dark storm, violet lightning. Small action, terrifying scale. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M11 · The mountain that should not be · 6 s · DARK

Past the waves the storm quiets. Seen from behind, @Manta with @Strong, @Elder and @Young floats on black water, facing a vast dark mountain on the horizon, @Mountain, where no land can exist. @Young whispers: "I can see them... inside it." For a moment a faint gold glow shows deep under the mountain's dark surface. Wide shot from behind the riders, locked off. Awe and dread. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 6 seconds.

## M12 · The eyes open · 5 s · DARK

The dark mountain is the head of a colossal sea creature, @Eye. Its two enormous eyes open, pale yellow-green with thin vertical slit pupils, each bigger than a whole village; water pours off its dark smooth skin, violet lightning behind. The eyes stare straight into the camera. Extreme close, slow push in. Silence, then menace. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot, 5 seconds.

## M13 · Pull out to space · 6 s · DARK

Pull back from the black stormy sea, up through the clouds and out into space until the whole planet is seen, @Planet: split into two hemispheres, one bright blue-white ocean with white clouds, the other purple-black under a storm with violet lightning, a huge moon beside it half the planet's size. One continuous pull-out, ending still. Majestic and ominous. Photorealistic, cinematic, 6 seconds.
