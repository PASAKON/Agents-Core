## How it scores (the CEO's rubric)

Creativity 30%: all-alien, landless civilisation. Wan3 25%: the same three
characters held across 14 shots above and below water. Story 20%: joy, then
dread, then awe. Sharing 15%: shots 16-17 (the mountain moves) are the clip
people send. Quality 10%: the colour script does the heavy lifting.
