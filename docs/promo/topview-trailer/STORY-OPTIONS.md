# TopView Wan3 Challenge: thriller trailer, story options (draft 1, 2026-09-23)

Brief from the CEO: a thriller-style trailer, 1-2 minutes. The music comes from
Google AI Ultra and drives the cut, but the story comes first. Every option below
is written against the judging weights in `docs/promo/TOPVIEW-WAN3-RULES.md`.

Constraints every option keeps: 60-90 s, one or two characters, one or two
locations (Wan3 reference consistency is easier and credits go further), about
8-9 generations of 30 s at 720p, a single question left open, a last-second sting.

## A. SAME FACE

A night guard in an empty office tower sees one stranger on every CCTV monitor at
once, on every floor, in the same second, standing still. Then the monitor for his
own booth shows the stranger sitting in his chair.

- Question left open: who is he, and why is he everywhere?
- Wan3 (25%): reference consistency IS the scare. The same face must hold across
  nine screens and places; that is exactly what TopView sells. The CCTV grid is
  shot-level direction in one generation.
- Sting: he turns, the chair behind him is empty; the monitor shows the stranger
  standing right behind him. Black. A chair creaks.
- Cost: two faces, one building. Cheapest of the three.

## B. VOICEMAIL

A woman gets voicemails from her own number, in her own voice, describing what she
is about to do ten seconds before she does it. The last one whispers "Don't answer
the door."

- Question left open: who is calling, and what is outside?
- Wan3 (25%): audio control (the voicemails are the narration), her face held
  across day and night, actions synced to the voice.
- Sting: a new voicemail, her voice: "It's already inside."
- Risk: Wan3 native audio is unconfirmed; the voice may have to be generated
  elsewhere and must be licensed.

## C. THE SPIRIT HOUSE (ศาลพระภูมิ)

A young couple rents a cheap house outside Bangkok. Every morning the offerings on
the old spirit house are gone. One morning a new offering waits for them: a framed
photo of the two of them asleep, taken from inside their bedroom.

- Question left open: who is feeding at the shrine, and who watches them sleep?
- Creativity (30%): a Thai setting stands out in a gallery full of JP/KR/CN titles.
- Wan3 (25%): photoreal Thailand, the couple and the shrine held across day and night.
- Sting: he films the shrine overnight; at 3 a.m. his wife walks out, eats the
  offerings, and turns to the camera.
- Risk: leans horror rather than thriller; three locations.

## CTO recommendation

**A, set in Bangkok.** A wins the 25% outright because Wan3's headline feature is
the plot, and moving it into a Thai tower at night borrows C's originality at no
extra cost. B is the most shareable idea but depends on audio we have not confirmed.
The CEO decides.
