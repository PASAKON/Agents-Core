Title:
Show HN: What 781 AI generations actually cost to make a 9-minute film

Text:
I made two AI short films and submitted them to a film festival.
I kept notes on everything that broke.

Same prompt, same references, fired back to back:

  Seedance 2.5:      98 credits, 11-19 min render
  Seedance 2.0 Fast: 53 credits, 4.5 min, 17.0 Mbps
  Seedance 2.0 Mini: 38 credits, 3.5 min, 4.3 Mbps

Both cheap tiers bind character references. That was the thing I
assumed would rule them out, and it did not.

Things that cost real money:

1. One button fires a generation with no confirmation step. It is
   next to the one that only loads the prompt. That mistake was 130
   credits.

2. Reference plates die retroactively. One passed the content scan
   at 10:41 and was permanently dead at 11:47 with nothing changed.
   A plate is never banked.

3. A clip was refused twice for copyright. Everyone assumed it was
   the image. It was the character's NAME. The refusal never tells
   you which string it objected to. I found it by checking which
   names had already rendered successfully somewhere else.

4. "No running" in the prompt produced running. What fixed it was
   giving a number (metres per second) and "one foot always on the
   ground". A prohibition with nothing put in its place does not hold.
