## Entry

- "The primary AI video generation for the entry must be completed with the Wan3
  model on the Topview platform."
- At least 30 s, 720p or better, any aspect ratio, any theme.
- Made after 4 Sep 2026: **Sorry Sir and Do Not Disturb are not eligible.**
- Official challenge watermark on the video; TopView project link submitted for checking.
- Public post on at least one of X, IG, YouTube, TikTok with
  `#TopviewAI #Wan3 #TopviewWan3Challenge` and the platform's TopView account tagged.
- Form: title, 16:9 cover, creator name, profile image, post link, project link,
  video file up to 500 MB.
- Editing outside TopView appears allowed (inferred from "Edit the video and add
  the official challenge watermark"). Music and audio must be licensed.
- Worldwide, 18+, free to enter. Paid by PayPal, Payoneer or bank; winner pays tax.
