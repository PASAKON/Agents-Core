Status: DRAFT v3 for CEO approval, 2026-10-09. What this minute is: the real first minute of EP1 (Orpheus). 16:9, 1080p, 4 clips of 15 s, Thai narration (ElevenLabs v4), one spoken word by Orpheus on screen. It tells the story in order, like the World of Legend videos, and stops at the loss.

## Why it is built this way (each row is a finding from the one channel)

| World of Legend does | This script does |
|---|---|
| Title = "ตำนาน" + a name the viewer already owns | Title card: "ตำนานออร์เฟียส / ชายผู้ลงไปรับภรรยากลับจากยมโลก". **Weak point: Thai viewers do not already own the name Orpheus** (see the demand gap in the wiki note). The card names the picture they do know: ยมโลก |
| No greeting; a title card, then a plain sentence of setting and subject | First sentence: "ในกรีซโบราณ มีนักดนตรีคนหนึ่งชื่อออร์เฟียส." Who, what, where. No hello |
| Chronological order, no flash-forward | Musician, wife dies, descent, deal, the look, the loss. One line of premise for what is still to come, plainly stated |
| Small act, huge price, the monster is a person with a reason | One look backward costs the whole thing. She does not blame him |
| Real places and numbers | กรีซโบราณ, ยมโลก, 7 เดือน (Virgil IV) |
| A rule the viewer applies to themself | The condition: do not look back until the light. The viewer asks "would I?" without being asked |
| Neutral written Thai from the narrator, slang only in voiced characters | No particles, no questions to the viewer, no ครับ or ค่ะ yet. Orpheus' one word is the voiced line |
| Ritual of the channel only at the end | **Deviation:** the brand ritual line stays at 0:15 (3 s of it). World of Legend has none at the start. CEO may move it to the end |

## Structure gate (CMO_Gate_Story_SceneEngine, IRON-RULES section 51), audit of this sequence

- **Goal:** bring Eurydice back from the dead (fix what is already established).
- **Obstacle:** one condition, no looking back, with her silent and out of sight the whole way. Jeopardises the last stage and the whole goal (global).
- **Tactic:** he climbs on trust, listening for her. The silence returns nothing, so his doubt grows. The tactic is forced and a reasonable guess.
- **Reversal:** "my own action flips on me": the look he takes to be sure of her is what loses her.
- **Value shift:** audience verdict on Orpheus goes from "the man who beat death with a song" to "the man whose doubt undid it". Clip 4 shifts it again toward "a man something worse is still coming for". The shifts deepen, they do not oscillate.
- Weakest point: Orpheus only reacts in clip 3; the doubt is carried by sound and the one beat of him almost turning, inside the 15 s.

## Rules that bind every prompt (do not drop)

- Orpheus is an adult, 18+. No blush, no red cheeks, no red ears, even natural skin tone (H3 hard rule, CEO 2026-09-28). Embarrassment, fear and grief are shown by action.
- Eurydice is never shown as a face. Silhouette only.
- The women of the next minute are NOT in this cut except as silhouettes on the ridge. No violence on screen in minute 1.
- Palette words in the look line: firelit ember warmth against deep verdigris cold, parchment highlights, near-black shadow. Painterly-cinematic, no photoreal faces (BRAND.md visual rules).
- No em dash in anything public (IRON-RULES section 39). Series name never inside a video title.
