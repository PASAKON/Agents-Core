idea that survives being shrunk to 210x118 px, which is the size a YouTube

film, and it asks a question the title answers.

into texture and an empty one keeps its subject. Dark + one lit thing is the
strongest small-size shape there is.

DELIVERABLE
A finished 16:9 cover image for a horror short film. Photographic, very dark,
built around one lit doorway. Print quality.

SUBJECT
@Motel-Walkway — the open-air walkway of an old roadside motel at night, seen

single caged wall lamp above it throws a hard cone of dirty amber light onto the
concrete.

@Prop-DNDTag — hanging on that one lit door's handle, a small paper DO NOT
DISTURB tag, catching the lamplight. It is the brightest small object in the
frame and it hangs perfectly still.

@Mother — a housekeeper, very small and far away, standing in the dark near the
lit door with her back to the camera, motionless, looking at it. She is not
walking. She occupies no more than one eighth of the frame height.

and a weak red EXIT glow at the far end of the walkway. Wet concrete holding a

vanishing point slightly left of centre. Enormous dark negative space across the
top half where the title sits. The lit door is the only bright area in the
lower two thirds. Long lens, flat and still.
