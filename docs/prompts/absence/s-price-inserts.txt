--- P1 · THE POLISH ($2,000,000) — plays before S4 ---
The plaque reads **$2,000,000**. A gloved hand enters with a folded cream cloth
and polishes the brass in slow circles — unhurried, devoted — gives one last
wipe to the "$", and withdraws. Hold the gleaming plaque one beat too long.

--- P2 · THE SWAP ($20,000,000) — plays before S6 ---
The plaque reads $2,000,000. **Two gloved hands enter with a small screwdriver,
unscrew the four corner screws with tiny precise turns, lift the plaque away —
a clean unfaded rectangle left on the wall — and screw a new plaque into its
place: $20,000,000.** One flat wipe of the cloth. Withdraw. Hold.

--- P3 · THE SWAP ($100,000,000) — plays before S11 ---
Identical choreography, identical pace, no escalation in the hands whatsoever:
off comes $20,000,000, on goes **$100,000,000**. The hands remain exactly as
calm as they were at two million. That is the joke. Wipe. Withdraw. Hold.
