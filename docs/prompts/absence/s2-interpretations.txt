================================================================
WHAT THIS SCENE IS DOING
================================================================

Four people take turns explaining what a crack in a wall means. **Every explanation is confident, and every one of them is about something the crack has nothing to do with.** Nobody checks. Nobody disagrees. Each new reading is treated as an addition to the last, never a contradiction.

The scene is not laughing at them for being stupid. **It is showing the pressure everyone in that room is under to prove they understood.** Silence would read as not getting it, so nobody is silent.

Under that runs the one image the whole film turns on. The critic's line asks **who decides what labour is worth** — and while she says it, **the man whose labour actually made the thing in front of them is mopping the floor twenty feet behind him, and not one person turns around.** He does not react. He does not know he is the answer to the question. He just finishes the floor.

The scene ends with the group agreeing, warmly, with all of it.
