THE THREE EXTRAS — applies to every angle below
---------------------------------------------------------------
**At least three extras, and no two of them alike.** They do **not** have to be
in the main frame — far off looking at pictures, or crossing the back of shot, is
exactly what the CEO asked for. The room currently reads too empty for a museum.

- **A** — man, forties, **deep teal**, standing well down the hall with his back
  to us, looking up at a painting. He never moves.
- **B** — woman, twenties, **mustard**, walking slowly across the far end of the
  hall, right to left, out of frame.
- **C** — older woman, **plum**, sitting on a distant bench, hands in her lap,
  looking at nothing in particular.

**They must not resemble each other or any named character.** Different ages,
builds, hair. **No gold V on any of them** — that mark is staff only. They never
interact with Dupe and they never look at camera.

---------------------------------------------------------------
S1A · THE FLOOR — camera at ankle height
---------------------------------------------------------------
**10s. No dialogue.**

**The camera sits ON THE TERRAZZO FLOOR**, lens a few inches up, looking along the
polished floor down the length of the hall. Extreme low angle. Chromium column
bases and terracotta floor fill the bottom of frame; the orange cove lighting runs
away to the vanishing point; **the red door is small and far off at the end.**

**DUPE WALKS PAST, VERY CLOSE TO CAMERA** — his shoes, the hem of his white
trousers, the wheels of the cart rolling by. He crosses frame and continues away
down the hall, getting smaller.

Extra A is visible far down the hall, back to us, looking up at a painting.

*The point: the film's most ignored man, shot from the height of the floor he
cleans.*

---------------------------------------------------------------
S1B · WAIST UP — straight on
---------------------------------------------------------------
**10s. No dialogue.**

Camera straight on at chest height, no tilt — **a FRONTAL PORTRAIT, perfectly
symmetrical, DUPE DEAD CENTRE FROM THE WAIST UP**, facing almost into the lens as
he dusts the top edge of a frame that sits just off-frame, slow careful strokes. His
hands, his sleeve, the gold V on his chest, his face in three-quarter.

He is content. He hums a little. He is entirely absorbed in a job nobody will
notice.

Behind him, softly out of focus, extra B crosses the far end of the hall.

*The point: the only angle in the film that treats him as the subject.*

---------------------------------------------------------------
S1C · THE CART — detail
---------------------------------------------------------------
**8s. No dialogue.**

Close on **`prop_cart_b`** as it rolls: mop, orange bucket, bottles, folded
cloths, brush, the folding ladder on the side, **the gold V on the front panel**,
and the painting slotted upright in its rack, front face turned outward.

The camera tracks alongside at cart height, terrazzo sliding past.

*The point: establishes the cart as an object before it starts carrying the
story.*

---------------------------------------------------------------
S1D · OVER THE SHOULDER — what he is looking at
---------------------------------------------------------------
**10s. No dialogue.**

From just behind and above Dupe's shoulder. The back of his cap with the gold V,
and beyond it **the hero wall — BARE, with the small crack and the brass plaque.**
His own accident, an hour old.

**He wipes the wall NEXT to the crack, carefully, and never once looks at the
crack itself.** Not a glance. He cleans around it the way you avoid a bruise.

Extra C sits on a distant bench, out of focus.

*The point: the film's best guilt beat — a man polishing the scene of his crime
while refusing to see it.*

---------------------------------------------------------------
S1E · THE LONG HALL — he is very small
---------------------------------------------------------------
**12s. No dialogue.**

Very wide, camera at eye height, **locked dead on the room's centreline — the
definitive ONE-POINT symmetrical master.** Columns in mirrored pairs, and **the
red door exactly centred at the vanishing point.** Chromium columns
march away on both sides, artworks on plinths between them.

**DUPE IS TINY**, off to one side, working along the wall with his cart. All three
extras are in this shot — **A** at a painting, **B** crossing the distance, **C**
on the bench — spread far apart, none near each other, none near Dupe.

Nobody looks at anybody.

*The point: the geography of the whole film in one frame, and the man who matters
least occupying the least of it.*

---------------------------------------------------------------
S1F · THE HANDS
---------------------------------------------------------------
**8s. No dialogue.**

**GOD'S-EYE OVERHEAD, locked off:** straight down on **his hands and the cloth**
against the terrazzo — wringing it, folding it into a neat square, checking the
fold, going again. The hands work dead centre of frame like a cooking-show insert.
Warm light, flat and even.

No face. Just the care.

*The point: eight seconds that make the accident hurt later.*

---------------------------------------------------------------
S1G · PAST THE COLUMN — watched by nobody
---------------------------------------------------------------
**10s. No dialogue.**

Camera behind a chromium column, part of the column down one edge of frame, as if
we are half-hidden. **Dupe works in the middle distance.** Extra B walks between
camera and Dupe, crossing close to the lens and briefly blocking him completely,
then clearing.

Dupe never notices either of them.

*The point: the film's only voyeur shot, and there is nothing to see.*
