@project_absence_loc_hall_big_d — THE ONE PICTURE THIS SHOT IS BUILT FROM, and the only room

THE SHAPE OF THE CLIP: sixteen seconds of a loud, overlapping, thoroughly undignified argument in a
very elegant room, and then four seconds of absolute frozen silence. The camera watches from one
spot like a security camera that happens to be beautifully composed.

WE ARE BEHIND THEM. Every person stands between the camera and the wall, facing the wall, so for the
first sixteen seconds THE CAMERA SEES THEIR BACKS AND THE BACKS OF THEIR HEADS. They are gathered
loosely across the middle of the frame, at different distances from the wall and from each other,
never in a line and never evenly spaced.

WHAT THEY ARE ARGUING ABOUT: the mark on the wall in front of them, which the five of them have
decided is a work of art. Each of them is completely certain and each of them means something
different by it. They gesture at the wall, step in to peer closely, step back to take it in, and
talk over one another without pause. The cleaner goes on mopping around them.

THE COMPLETE CAST IS SIX PEOPLE AND NOT ONE MORE. There are no extras in this film. No bystanders,
no other visitors, no gallery staff, no guards, no reporters, nobody holding a camera, nobody
walking through. If a figure is not named below it must not exist.

@project_absence_char_cleaner_c — DUPE: white uniform with orange trim, cap, gold V, moustache. He
