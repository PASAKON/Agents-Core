S2b · THE PHONE CALL · 8s · SILENT
---------------------------------------------------------------
References: loc_hall_big_e · char_cleaner_c

A quiet corner of the museum — a plain wall section by the service
side. **A retrofuturist WALL TELEPHONE**: cream and chrome, rotary
dial, coiled cord, a small brass shelf. Nothing digital.

CAMERA: locked profile, waist height, perfectly symmetrical — Dupe
dead centre at the phone.

**DUPE, worried, glances over his shoulder — nobody — then dials.** A
rotary number, deliberate. He waits. He cups his hand around the
mouthpiece and speaks — **we hear nothing intelligible, a low murmur
under the room tone.** He nods. In his other hand, **a small fold of
worn banknotes — his own money.** He hangs up, straightens his cap,
breathes once, and walks out of frame with the cart.

*What this plants: the workman of S13/S14 was called by Dupe, this
morning, and the eight hundred is Dupe's own savings. "Someone asked
me to fix it." now has a face.*

Sound: rotary dial clicks, the murmur, room tone. No dialogue proper.

THE ARRIVALS — shared frame for A1–A5
---------------------------------------------------------------
References every time: **loc_wall_pov_e** (the hand-built frame — the
camera is BEHIND the wall looking out through the crack; the brass
plaque low in frame, MIRROR-REVERSED, reading $2,000,000; **the RED
DOOR dead centre at the vanishing point**) · char_cleaner_c ·
prop_cart_b · plus that scene's arrivals.

**THE FRAME NEVER MOVES, in any of the five.** Locked off. Identical.
The only thing that changes from scene to scene is who stands in it.

**Each arrival:** the red door opens far away, dead centre. The new
character walks the centreline toward camera, growing from tiny to
full figure, stops at the row, looks at the crack — which is the
camera — and **delivers their interpretation straight into the lens.**

**THE ROW ACCUMULATES.** Everyone who has arrived STAYS, standing in a
neat line facing camera like a class photograph assembling one pupil
at a time: 1 → 2 → 4 → 5 → 6 across the five scenes. Costume colours
alternate warm and cool along the line.

**DUPE IS IN EVERY FRAME** — at the edge, cleaning the wall BESIDE the
crack, close to it, **and never once looking at it.** The painting
rides his cart in plain sight, face outward. **Nobody ever looks at
the cart. This is deliberate — the truth sits in the corner of every
shot in the film and no one, ever, looks at it.**

His greetings (canon lines, CEO: only some arrivals keep one, so the
joke never wears out): **A1, A3 and A5 only.** Spoken quietly, warmly;
**never answered, never acknowledged, and he never reacts to that.**

A1 · THE ELDERLY GENTLEMAN · 15s — the first man in the world
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cast: char_oldman

The empty frame. The crack. The plaque. A long still beat.

The red door opens. **THE ELDERLY GENTLEMAN** — heavy, about eighty,
bottle-green leather overcoat, chrome ring-handled cane — comes down
the centreline SLOWLY, cane first, a small figure becoming a large
one. He stops. He looks at the crack a very long time. Dupe, at the
frame's edge, wipes the wall and says: **"Excuse me, sir."** Nothing.
The old man does not hear him — or does not distinguish him from the
building.

Then, slowly, with the certainty of a man reading scripture:

    "It's about death."

Hold him alone in the frame, one beat too long.

*The first human being ever to see the crack as art. The patient zero
of the whole film. Give the silence its full weight.*

---------------------------------------------------------------
A2 · THE ART CRITIC · 10s
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cast: char_oldman (in the row) + char_critic_b

The old man now stands in the row, still. The door opens again.
**THE ART CRITIC** — Asian woman about sixty, deep magenta fur, cream
gloves; **half-Chinese, and when she speaks it is fluent English with
a Chinese accent, the cadence of a Chinese speaker who uses English
well.** She walks the axis, joins the line beside him, studies the
crack, weighing it, and says slowly:

    "It asks who decides what labour is worth."

Dupe cleans on at the edge. No greeting in this one.

---------------------------------------------------------------
A3 · THE COUPLE · 12s
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cast: the row (oldman, critic) + char_visitor_b + char_husband

The door opens on TWO figures: **THE WOMAN IN THE CHESTNUT FUR**
(~55, rust dress, warm ordinary face) with **HER HUSBAND** half a step
behind in his deep-ochre wool coat. They come down the axis together.
As they pass him, Dupe: **"Excuse me. Just a moment."** — finishing a
wipe at their feet. Neither of them registers he exists.

She takes her place in the row, looks at the crack, and quietly, a
single tear running down her cheek that she does not wipe:

    "It made me think of my mother."

**Her husband glances at her. He does not touch her. He does not know
what to do.** Nobody else reacts at all.

---------------------------------------------------------------
A4 · THE ART STUDENT · 10s
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cast: the row (4) + char_student_c

The door again. **THE ART STUDENT** — about twenty-two, acid-green
dyed hair, paint-stained denim — walks the axis FASTER than anyone
else, already unimpressed, plants herself at the end of the row, and
fires it flat:

    "It's anti-capitalist. Obviously."

**NOT ONE PERSON IN THE ROW TURNS TOWARD HER.** The same silence Dupe
gets. She joins the line anyway. Dupe cleans on. No greeting here.

---------------------------------------------------------------
A5 · THE WOMAN IN COBALT · 20s — the consensus
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cast: the full row (5) + char_woman

The door, one last time. **THE WOMAN IN COBALT** — ~50, glossy cobalt
leather, tall sculptural crest, cat-eye sunglasses indoors — the
slowest, most deliberate walk of the five. As she passes him Dupe
says: **"Sorry, madam."** Nothing, as always.

She takes the centre of the row, looks at the crack, and — **perfectly
still, only her mouth moving:**

    "This is the real thing."
    "I have spent my whole life in rooms like this one."
    "I have never once stood in front of anything like it."
    "That is not a picture of something. That is the thing itself."

A beat — then **a low warm murmur across the whole row: "Ohhh."** Nods
spread. Brief, quiet, sincere applause.

**And at the edge of frame Dupe stops wiping for one second — his only
reaction in the whole act — then goes back to work.**

*The consensus is born. From the next scene it has a price.*
