## S3 · The interpretations · shot from inside the wall

Six people facing camera, eyelines locked on the crack. Sincere, quiet, serious.
Nobody amused. In this order:

> **THE ELDERLY GENTLEMAN** *"It's about death."*
> **THE WOMAN IN THE CHESTNUT FUR**, one tear she does not wipe *"It made me think of my mother."*
> **THE ART STUDENT**, fast and flat *"It's anti-capitalist. Obviously."* — **nobody turns toward her**
> **THE ART CRITIC** *"It asks who decides what labour is worth."*
> **THE WOMAN IN COBALT**, perfectly still, only her mouth moving:
> *"This is the real thing."*
> *"I have spent my whole life in rooms like this one."*
> *"I have never once stood in front of anything like it."*
> *"That is not a picture of something. That is the thing itself."*

Then a low warm *"Ohhh."* across the group. Nods. Brief, sincere applause.

**Dupe is cleaning throughout**, drifting closer, leaning his head into frame
once. Nobody looks at him. He never reacts.

## S7 · Valder arrives · **LOCKED**

He walks in **standing straight, wearing the warm ingratiating smile**, past

## S8 · The five pieces, then the sixth · **LOCKED**

> **the chair** — *"This chair was in the room where the armistice was signed. Not at the table. In the corner. Nobody sat in it."*
> **the fish trap** — *"A fish trap. It caught nothing for forty years. The man who made it never sold a single one."*
> **the millstone** — *"A millstone. It ground grain for two hundred years. Then somebody stood it upright. And it stopped being a tool."*
> **the vessel** — *"Seventeen of these were made. He destroyed sixteen. He kept the one he liked least."*
> **the painting** — *"He painted this in one afternoon, to test a colour. The finished work is in a vault in Geneva. Nobody has ever asked to see it."*

**People and a column block the wall the entire time. He never once sees it.**

He turns, raises a hand to introduce the last piece. **The crowd parts.** He sees
the crack. The painting is gone.

**The smile — that same warm, charming, ingratiating one — does not change.** He goes silent — two seconds, three — hand still
raised, the room waiting. Then, in exactly the same voice:

> **VALDER** *"And this one."*
> *"I did not commission it. I did not buy it."*
> *"It happened to my wall while nobody was looking."*
> *"Every other thing in this room, someone decided to make."*
> *"That one decided by itself."*

A pause.

> **VALDER** *"I have been collecting for forty years to find something that did not need me."*

The raised hand lowers, slowly.

**He never lies.** Every word is true; he has only rearranged them into a reason.
The last line is the one that makes him believe it himself — which is exactly
what the CEO locked: *Valder genuinely believes his own invention by the end.*

## S9 · Dupe hears it · no lines of his own · **LOCKED**

**Valder's S8 audio runs continuously over this whole scene — the same take, not
re-recorded, not cut.** The CEO cuts between S8 and S9 himself in the edit, as
often as he likes.

He is mopping at the edge of the room, back against the shadow of a column.

> *"…It happened to my wall while nobody was looking."*

**His hands stop.**

> *"…Every other thing in this room, someone decided to make."*

**Sweat runs down his temple. He does not wipe it.**

He looks at **the real painting, leaning on his own cart.** Then back at the crowd.

> *"…That one decided by itself."*

**He opens his mouth.**

One second.

**Closes it.**

He looks down and goes back to mopping. Same rhythm, same speed.

## S15 · Dupe confesses · **LOCKED**

**Dupe has been watching from close by.** He runs in and **puts himself between
Valder and the workman.** He is frightened.

> **DUPE**, frightened *"Sorry, sir."*

He looks at the wall. He looks at Valder's face. Then he goes on, **without any
idea what he is doing:**

> **DUPE** *"I cracked it. Last hour."*

**"Last hour", never "last week"** — CEO, explicitly. The whole film happens in
one morning, so it is literally true.

## S18 · The interview, then the hammer

> **THE REPORTER** *"How did you know?"*
> **DUPE**, flat, telling the same truth he has told all along *"I dropped a painting."*

The reporter nods slowly, moved, and writes something else down.

**Cut** — Dupe alone in front of a fresh white wall, a hammer in his hand.

He raises it.

**Stop.**

He lowers it. He stands there. **Hold long, then cut to black.**
