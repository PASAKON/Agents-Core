================================================================
WHAT THIS SCENE IS DOING
================================================================

This is the opening of the film, and the film is told through THE CLEANER.

**IT OPENS ON HIM AND IT ENDS ON HIM.** In between, a crowd is born in front of a wall he damaged by accident. He is the only person in the building who knows what the crack really is. He never says so. That silence is the film.

The scene has three movements:

**ONE — THE MAN WHO KNOWS.** The hall is empty. The cleaner walks the length of it pushing his cart, and on the cart is the painting that used to hang on the hero wall, taken down and carried away by him. He passes the bare wall with its crack and its plaque. **He does not look at it.** He keeps walking.

**TWO — THE CROWD IS BORN.** Two people stop at the bare wall. Then four. They begin to argue about it, completely seriously. Then more drift in, one at a time and then in twos and threes. Nobody is summoned and nobody hurries. They arrive, join the back, and start looking at whatever the others are looking at.

**THREE — HE WATCHES AND SAYS NOTHING.** The last shot returns to the cleaner at the edge of the room, his cart beside him, the real painting still leaning against it, watching a crowd worship the damage he caused. He does not step forward. He does not speak. Hold on that.

Underneath runs the second idea: **THE HALL IS FULL OF REAL ART AND ALMOST NOBODY IS LOOKING AT IT.** A few people are, properly, quietly, alone. Everyone else walks straight past those paintings on their way to the bare wall.

**THERE IS NO PRESS PHOTOGRAPHER IN THIS SCENE.** Nobody is reporting on this yet. It is still just people arguing in a room.

ERA: a retrofuturist world — the future as people in the past imagined it. Swept aerodynamic curves, cantilevers, moulded plastic, chromium, terrazzo, formica, starburst motifs, boomerang shapes, tapered splayed legs. THERE IS NOTHING DIGITAL ANYWHERE. No screens, no LEDs, no digital numerals, no cables. An invented place — no country and no year is identifiable.

COLOUR IS THE CLASS SYSTEM: wealth is DEEP SATURATED COLOUR IN LARGE FLAT PLANES, and emptiness. Every named person wears ONE saturated colour, and no two of them wear the same one.

THE LIGHT IS WARM AND BRIGHT, never moody and never dim. The orange ceiling coves throw warm light down the hall and it bounces off the polished red floor. Highlights bloom around the lamps.

THE CAMERA IS ALWAYS LEVEL. No dutch angles, no tilting, ever.

PHOTOGRAPHED, NOT RENDERED: fine film grain, halation around bright highlights, faint gate weave, softness and slight colour fringing at the edges of the lens. The grain must NOT desaturate the colour. NO HDR, NO digital glossiness, NO CGI sheen.

PERFORMANCE IS DEADPAN AND COMPLETELY SINCERE. They are arguing seriously, never comically. Gestures are small and precise. Nobody smiles, nobody laughs, nobody mugs, nobody shouts. THE CLEANER'S FACE NEVER CHANGES.

**EVERY NAMED CHARACTER APPEARS EXACTLY ONCE IN ANY SHOT. There are no duplicates, no twins, no repeated faces in the crowd.**
