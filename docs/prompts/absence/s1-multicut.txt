================================================================
WHAT THIS SCENE IS DOING
================================================================

This is the opening of the film. It shows **THE BIRTH OF A CROWD.**

It begins with **FOUR PEOPLE** in front of one white wall, **arguing about it.** Not a mob — four ordinary gallery visitors having a real disagreement, gesturing at the wall and turning to answer each other. They are completely serious.

Over the scene, **MORE PEOPLE DRIFT IN.** One at a time, then in twos and threes. Nobody is summoned and nobody hurries. They arrive, join the back, and start looking at whatever the others are looking at. By the end the four have become a mass.

Underneath runs the second idea: **THE HALL IS FULL OF REAL ART AND ALMOST NOBODY IS LOOKING AT IT.** Three people are — properly, quietly, alone. Everyone else walks past those paintings without turning their heads, on their way to the white wall.

**WE NEVER SEE WHAT THEY ARE LOOKING AT.** The camera stays behind them, beside them, past them — never square on to the hero wall. The scene ends on their backs. Withholding it is the entire structure of the opening.

**THERE IS NO PRESS PHOTOGRAPHER IN THIS SCENE.** Nobody is reporting on this yet. It is still just people arguing in a room.
