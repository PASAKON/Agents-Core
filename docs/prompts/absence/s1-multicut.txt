================================================================
WHAT THIS SCENE IS DOING
================================================================

This is the opening of the film and its whole job is ONE IDEA, delivered without a single word:

**THIS BUILDING IS FULL OF REAL ART, AND NOBODY IS LOOKING AT ANY OF IT.**

The camera travels down a hall lined with genuinely good paintings — properly made, competently composed, the kind of work that took someone years — and the crowd flows past every one of them without stopping. Not one person turns their head. **The paintings are not the joke. They are real and they are good, and that is exactly the point.**

Then the camera finds where everybody actually is: a mass of people packed tight at the far end of the hall, in front of one white wall.

**WE NEVER SEE WHAT THEY ARE LOOKING AT.** The scene ends on their backs. Withholding it is the entire structure of the opening.

================================================================
STYLE — APPLIES TO EVERY SHOT
================================================================

ERA: a retrofuturist world — the future as people in the past imagined it. Swept aerodynamic curves, cantilevers, moulded plastic, chromium, terrazzo, formica, starburst motifs, boomerang shapes, tapered splayed legs. THERE IS NOTHING DIGITAL ANYWHERE. No screens, no LEDs, no digital numerals, no cables. An invented place — no country and no year is identifiable.

COLOUR IS THE CLASS SYSTEM: wealth is DEEP SATURATED COLOUR IN LARGE FLAT PLANES, and emptiness.

THE LIGHT IS WARM AND BRIGHT, never moody and never dim. The orange ceiling coves throw warm light down the hall and it bounces off the polished red floor. Highlights bloom around the lamps.

THE CAMERA IS ALWAYS LEVEL. No dutch angles, no tilting, ever.

PHOTOGRAPHED, NOT RENDERED: fine film grain, halation around bright highlights, faint gate weave, softness and slight colour fringing at the edges of the lens. The grain must NOT desaturate the colour. NO HDR, NO digital glossiness, NO CGI sheen.

PERFORMANCE IS FLAT AND CEREMONIAL: nobody smiles, nobody laughs, nobody grimaces, nobody weeps. Movements precise, slow and formal. Total sincerity delivered completely deadpan.

CROWDS ARE LOOSE AND SCATTERED, NEVER IN FORMATION. No rows, no lines, no ranks, nobody facing camera together, nobody moving in step. People stand at different distances and different angles, in ones and twos and threes, the way people actually stand in a room.
