# PLATE SPEC — `loc_wall_pov_f` · THE INVISIBLE WALL
CEO 2026-08-29, from his own reference (a C4D "invisible walls" cutaway).
**Supersedes `loc_wall_pov_e` everywhere.** Generate as an IMAGE first, file
it as an Element, then re-fire every scene that used the old frame.

## The idea
The gallery is a BOX with its fourth wall removed. The camera stands exactly
where the hero wall was — so the audience is *inside the missing wall*,
looking in at a room that is fully open to them. Same conceit as before,
pulled all the way back: we are the wall, and now we can see the whole room.

## The frame
- **A three-sided interior box seen from outside**: left wall, right wall,
  far wall, floor and ceiling all visible at once, like a doll's-house room
  or a stage set with the front removed.
- **Camera slightly ELEVATED, looking gently down** into the box — high
  enough to read the whole floor, not a top-down.
- **DEAD-ON AND SYMMETRICAL, not a three-quarter angle.** ⚠️ CTO note: the
  CEO's reference is angled, but this film's entire grammar is one-point
  symmetry. Straight-on elevated is the Anderson version of the same idea
  (the Life Aquatic cross-section, not an architectural render). Flagged for
  him to overrule if he wants the angle.
- **THE CRACK IS IN THE NEAR PLANE**, across the invisible wall itself —
  clearly visible, large, unmistakable, floating in the empty air where the
  fourth wall used to be. It is the first thing the eye lands on.
- **The brass plaque sits low in the near plane too, MIRROR-REVERSED**,
  because we are reading it from behind.
- **The red door stays dead centre in the far wall**, at the vanishing point.

## Room contents (match `loc_hall_big_e`)
Chromium columns in mirrored pairs, orange cove lighting washing the ceiling,
terracotta terrazzo floor, artworks on white plinths along both side walls.
Warm shadow / cold white grade.

## Hard rules
- **NO PEOPLE IN THE PLATE.** Faces in a location plate trip the Face/IP
  scanner and the verdict is retroactive and terminal. The people arrive in
  the video prompts.
- One crack. Same crack in every scene that binds this plate.
- 16:9, 2K.

## What changes in the scenes
People now stand INSIDE the box FACING the camera — facing the missing wall,
which is the thing they are looking at. That is unchanged in meaning and much
more legible: the crowd reads as a row in a lit box rather than shapes seen
through a hole.
