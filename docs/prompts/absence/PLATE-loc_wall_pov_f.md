**V3 · THE PROSCENIUM** — eye level, not elevated. Dead-on, perfectly

**V4 · THE DOLL'S HOUSE** — steeply elevated, near a high-angle plan: we look

**V5 · THE CRACK IS THE FRAME** — the open box of V3, but the crack is
enormous and sits in the clear near plane across the whole opening, running
top to bottom through the middle of frame, the room seen behind and through
