THE SCENE
---------------------------------------------------------------
SIX PEOPLE stand beyond the wall facing camera, eyelines locked on the crack —
which means on us. Completely sincere, quiet, serious. Nobody amused, nobody
performing.

They speak in this order. **These lines exactly, and no others:**

1. **THE ELDERLY GENTLEMAN** — heavy, about eighty, bottle-green leather, chrome
   ring cane — slowly, with great certainty:
   **"It's about death."**

2. **THE WOMAN IN THE CHESTNUT FUR** — about fifty-five, rust dress, a warm
   ordinary face — quietly, unsteady, **a single tear running down her cheek that
   she does not wipe**:
   **"It made me think of my mother."**
   **HER HUSBAND STANDS BESIDE HER IN THE OCHRE COAT.** He glances at her. He
   does not touch her. He does not know what to do. Nobody else reacts.

3. **THE ART STUDENT** — about twenty-two, **acid-green dyed hair**, paint-stained
   denim, standing upright — fast and flat:
   **"It's anti-capitalist. Obviously."**
   **NOT ONE PERSON TURNS TOWARD HER.**

4. **THE ART CRITIC** — Asian woman about sixty, deep magenta fur, cream gloves.
   **She is HALF-CHINESE and speaks fluent English with a Chinese accent** — the
   cadence and vowel shapes of a Chinese speaker using English well. Slowly,
   weighing every word:
   **"It asks who decides what labour is worth."**

5. **THE WOMAN IN COBALT** — about fifty, tall sculptural crest of hair, cat-eye
   sunglasses worn indoors. **She does not move at all while she speaks, only her
   mouth:**
   **"This is the real thing."**
   **"I have spent my whole life in rooms like this one."**
   **"I have never once stood in front of anything like it."**
   **"That is not a picture of something. That is the thing itself."**

Then a **low warm murmur of agreement across the group: "Ohhh."** Nods spread.
Brief, quiet, sincere applause.

**AND THROUGHOUT, DUPE IS CLEANING NEARBY.** He works at the edge of frame with
his cart — **the painting slotted upright into it, face outward** — drifts closer
as the scene goes on, and **at one point leans his head into shot** before
withdrawing. **Nobody looks at him. He never reacts.** He is the only person in
the building who knows where the crack came from.

**No gold V on any visitor.** Only Dupe and his cart carry one.
