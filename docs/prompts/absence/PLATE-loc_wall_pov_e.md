## In prompt terms

1. **A CRACK, not a star.** Write "a black crack, star-shaped". Never "a star" as the noun.
2. **Solid BLACK.** Not grey, not pale, not glowing.
3. **NEVER CHANGED.** Same shape, colour and size in every scene, copied from the plate.
4. **SMALL.** About a hand across. A big crack kills the film.
5. **FRONTMOST.** It floats at the very front of the frame, in the plane where the wall was;
   every person and every part of the room is behind it. No hole, no edges, no black
   surround — the wall is not there. (The layering in S2K take 1 was already right — CEO.)
