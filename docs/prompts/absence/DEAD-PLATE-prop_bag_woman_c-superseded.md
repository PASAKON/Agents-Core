## Review before the CEO sees it
1. **The green is HER green** — hold it against her sheet. A bag that is a
   shade off will read as borrowed on screen.
2. **Crocodile scale, clearly readable.** If the surface comes back smooth, or
   as diamond quilting — the default handbag texture every model reaches for —
   re-fire. The scale pattern is what makes the prop expensive on screen.
3. **Rigid, standing on its own.** If it slumps, re-fire.
4. **Gold hardware, and only the two handle anchors plus one plain clasp.**
5. **Nothing written on it anywhere.**

## Where it is used
She carries it in the bidding battle: she arrives LAST, after Valder has
finished showing the room, walks in like a model on a runway, and out-bids the
gold-toothed gentleman. The bag is in shot the whole time, so it has to be an
Element rather than something the model re-invents on every fire.
