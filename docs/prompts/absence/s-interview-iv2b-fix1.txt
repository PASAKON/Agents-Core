WHAT CHANGED AND WHY. IV2 already shot and filed as IV2-Fix1.MP4; that take
stays on Drive and `s-interview.txt` stays exactly as it is. The CEO asked for
the whip-pan gag to land somewhere better: the camera turns and finds THE FILM
CREW WHO ARE SHOOTING THIS INTERVIEW — a camera pointed back at Dupe, two or
three crew running the take, lights and grip gear — while the room behind them
is still his enormous house.

The old version turned and found a housekeeper with a dropped tray. The joke
was slapstick. The new one is better and it is a different joke: the moment the
camera turns, the audience learns this is a production, that Dupe has been
performing sincerity into a lens the whole time, and that the film they are
watching is being made by someone. Then the camera turns back and he carries on
as if nothing happened, which is the same beat as before but now means
something about him rather than about a dropped tray.

The dropped object is kept — it is what motivates the whip — but the person who
drops it is now one of the crew. That is exactly what the CEO asked for.

⚠️ THIS FILE OVERRIDES A STANDING NEGATIVE. `s-interview.txt` bans camera
equipment, microphones, booms and studio lighting in every clip, for good
reason: the other four must never betray that a crew exists. IV2b is the single
deliberate exception, and it is only an exception INSIDE the whip pan. Dupe's
own locked frame stays clean — no gear, no crew, no cable, nothing. That is why
this shot lives in its own file rather than as an edit to the shared one; a
blanket edit would have leaked the crew into IV1, IV3, IV4 and IV5.

Plate: @project_absence_char_dupe_interview_house — the same one all five clips
used. No new plate, no image generation, nothing to approve.

Word budget: 30 spoken words in 20s, unchanged from IV2. Well inside the
measured 45-50 ceiling.

Filing: All Scene/Fix-1/ as IV2b-Fix1.MP4. IV2-Fix1.MP4 is NOT replaced and NOT
