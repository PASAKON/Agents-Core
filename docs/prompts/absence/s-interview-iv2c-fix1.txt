WHY A THIRD VERSION. Takes 1 and 2 both came back with the same defect: the
crew-reveal half read as a studio instead of Dupe's house, and take 2 carried
five negatives aimed straight at it which changed nothing. A defect that
survives that much hardening is not a wording problem — the model was being
asked to invent a reverse angle of a room it has only ever seen from one side,
and with nothing to go on it reached for the commonest thing behind a camera,
which is a studio.

So this version stops banning and starts describing. The CEO's instruction: say
exactly how many people there are and exactly what each one looks like, because
a model left to guess gives every crew member the same face.

THE THREE, as specified:
  1. seated, operating the camera
  2. standing with a long boom microphone
  3. the one who knocked the light over — crouched on the floor picking it up

And the beat: we NEVER SEE the drop, only hear it. By the time the camera
arrives the other two have already looked at what fell and are turning back to
their work like professionals. Only the one who knocked it over is out of
position, crouched on the floor picking his light stand back up.
Then the camera whips back and Dupe simply carries on.
