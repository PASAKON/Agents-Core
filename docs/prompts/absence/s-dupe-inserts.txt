===============================================================
D1–D5 · DUPE REACTION INSERTS · 5s each · CEO-approved
===============================================================
References: loc_hall_big_e · char_cleaner_c

Five tiny portraits for the edit room — cut in after each
interpretation line, or wherever the cut needs Dupe's face. **Fire
them between renders; they cost nothing and bind almost nothing.**

SHARED FRAME: **a locked FRONTAL PORTRAIT, Dupe dead centre from the
chest up**, waist-height lens, the hall soft behind him, cloth in
hand, mid-work but momentarily still. Deadpan — the reactions are
TINY. One micro-event per insert, nothing else moves.

- **D1 · after "It's about death."** — he blinks. Once. Slowly. That
  is the entire clip.
- **D2 · after "…what labour is worth."** — his eyes drop to his own
  two hands holding the cloth, then come back up.
- **D3 · after "…my mother."** — he almost nods — the nod begins,
  perhaps three millimetres — and stops.
- **D4 · after "It's anti-capitalist. Obviously."** — his eyes slide
  sideways to his own mop leaning on the cart, and return.
- **D5 · after "That is the thing itself."** — his eyes go to the real
  painting riding his cart, hold half a second, and snap back — the
  only guilty movement he makes all act.

Sound: room tone only. No dialogue.
