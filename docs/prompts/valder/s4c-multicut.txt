WHO AND WHAT EACH REFERENCE IS. READ THIS BEFORE ANY SHOT BELOW.
================================================================

@project_valder_char_mother — THE MOTHER. A woman in her forties who stands very straight, hands habitually clasped. SHE CHANGES COMPLETELY DURING THIS SCENE. In shots 1 to 4 she wears her old clothes: PALE, washed out, CLEAN, PRESSED and CAREFULLY MENDED, and her skin is WARM and slightly ruddy. In shots 5 to 7 she wears a Valder garment in deeply saturated colour, and her skin has begun to go PALE. She is the same woman and must be recognisably the same face throughout. SHE IS ONE PERSON.

@project_valder_loc_shop_ext — THE VALDER SHOP, FROM OUTSIDE. An enormous retail building: one vast flat plane of deeply saturated colour, almost no windows, a queue of people waiting along its front. There is no shop name and no writing on it anywhere.

@project_valder_loc_shop_int — INSIDE THE VALDER SHOP. Nearly empty. An enormous saturated colour plane, a polished terrazzo floor, and only three or four garments in the entire space, each on its own stand, spaced far apart like sculptures in a gallery. One long low chromium and moulded-plastic counter. No racks, no price tags, no writing.

@project_valder_prop_mark — THE GOLD V. Valder's maker's mark: a capital letter V in polished warm gold, thick even strokes, tapered to a sharp point at the bottom. It is stamped into everything Valder designs and worn by everyone who belongs. IT IS THE SAME SHAPE AT EVERY SIZE — no simplified version exists.

@project_valder_prop_dress_c1 — THE VALDER GARMENT. A woman's garment built from flat geometric panels of three or four intensely saturated colours meeting at hard edges, deliberately asymmetric — one sleeve longer than the other. the gold V as a belt buckle — on the garment's hardware, never pinned to the chest.

@project_valder_prop_shopping_bags — THE VALDER SHOP BAGS. Three or four rigid structured carrier bags in one deeply saturated colour with flat ribbon handles. The gold V is the only thing printed on them — centred and large. NO shop name, NO words, NO text at all.

@project_valder_prop_market_bag — THE MARKET BAG. A woven cloth bag from the family's old life: pale, faded, clean, visibly mended at one handle, FULL of ordinary cheap food — a paper sack of rice, vegetables, a wrapped parcel. NO MARKING OF ANY KIND ON IT. It is the one object in this film that Valder never touched.

THERE IS EXACTLY ONE MOTHER IN THIS ENTIRE FILM SCENE. Never two, never a copy, never a double in the background, never a mirror or window reflection that produces a second woman. NO DUPLICATE CHARACTER.

WHAT THIS SCENE IS ABOUT — READ THIS, IT DECIDES EVERY CHOICE
================================================================

The mother queues, goes into the Valder shop, and comes out transformed.

THE WHOLE JOKE IS HOW LITTLE TIME IT TAKES. Crossing from one class to another costs exactly one hard cut. Nobody sees the money change hands. Nobody sees her decide. She goes in one person and comes out another, and the film does not dignify it with a process.

THE ONE IMAGE THIS SCENE MUST DELIVER: inside the shop, SHE PUTS THE BAG OF FOOD DOWN ON THE FLOOR so that her hands are free to hold the clothes. That is the trade, made literal, without a word.

When she comes out she walks straight past the people still queueing and DOES NOT LOOK AT ANY OF THEM — the same way the neighbour did not look at her.

NOBODY SPEAKS IN THIS SCENE. Not one word.

THE QUEUE IS A QUEUE OF SHOPPERS, NOT A CROWD AT A RALLY. People stand in loose ones and twos, some bored, some checking the door, some talking quietly. NOBODY faces a leader or a speaker. There are NO guards holding anyone back, NO banners, NO signs, NO raised hands, NO uniform formation.

================================================================
POOR IS NOT DIRTY — THIS IS THE MOST MISREAD INSTRUCTION IN THIS FILM
================================================================

In her old clothes the mother is not shabby and NOT DIRTY. Clean, pressed, neatly mended. Hair combed. Face washed. Shoes old but polished. Her poverty reads through the AGE of her things and through careful visible repairs — a seam sewn twice, a cuff turned, a button that does not quite match. It NEVER reads through stains, grime, grease, dust, tears or messiness.

NO dirt. NO stains. NO grime. NO smudges. NO greasy hair. NO torn fabric. NO dust. NO mud. NO sweat. NO unwashed skin.

================================================================
WHAT PALE MEANS HERE
================================================================

The people in the queue are PALE, and by the end of this scene the mother has started to be. Pale means ORDINARY HUMAN INDOOR PALLOR — the skin of people who live indoors and do not see the sun. IT IS NEVER GREEN, NEVER GREY, NEVER BLUE, NEVER SILVER. Normal warm undertone, visible pores, real human skin texture. Think of a clerk at the end of a long winter. NOT a creature, NOT a corpse, NOT anything from science fiction or horror.

Everyone must read as REAL PHOTOGRAPHED HUMAN BEINGS.

================================================================
STYLE — APPLIES TO EVERY SHOT
================================================================

ERA: a retrofuturist world — the future as people in the past imagined it. Swept aerodynamic curves, cantilevers, moulded plastic, chromium, terrazzo, formica, starburst motifs, boomerang shapes, tapered splayed legs. THERE IS NOTHING DIGITAL ANYWHERE. No screens, no LEDs, no digital numerals, no cables, no card machines. An invented place — no country and no year is identifiable.

COLOUR IS THE CLASS SYSTEM: wealth is DEEP SATURATED COLOUR IN LARGE FLAT PLANES, and emptiness. Poverty is the SAME HUES BUT PALE AND WASHED OUT. Inside the shop, the emptiness IS the luxury — three garments in an enormous room.

THE CAMERA IS ALWAYS LEVEL. No dutch angles, no tilting, ever.

LIGHTING IS FLAT AND FRONTAL, like a property brochure — minimal shadow, everything legible.

PHOTOGRAPHED, NOT RENDERED: fine film grain, halation around bright highlights, faint gate weave, softness and slight colour fringing at the edges of the lens. The grain must NOT desaturate the colour. NO HDR, NO digital glossiness, NO CGI sheen.

PERFORMANCE IS FLAT AND CEREMONIAL: nobody smiles broadly, nobody laughs, nobody grimaces. Movements precise, slow and formal. Total sincerity delivered completely deadpan.

NOBODY LOOKS AT THE CAMERA. Every gaze is aimed at a person or an object inside the frame.

ALL SPOKEN DIALOGUE IN THIS FILM IS IN ENGLISH. THERE IS NO DIALOGUE AT ALL IN THIS PARTICULAR SCENE.

================================================================
WHAT MARKS A PERSON AS ONE OF THEM — READ THIS, IT REPLACES AN EARLIER RULE
================================================================

**THE GARMENT IS WHAT READS ON SIGHT. THE GOLD V IS A TRADEMARK, AND ANYONE MAY HAVE ONE.**

A Valder coat is unmistakable on sight — floor-length or near it, built from large flat panels of several intensely saturated colours meeting at hard edges, deliberately asymmetric, worn with matching coloured gloves and matching coloured shoes and a scarf at the throat. **Wearing one is what says you belong**, because it is instantly legible across a street. The trademark is not doing that job; the coat is.

**THE GOLD V IS VALDER'S TRADEMARK.** It is stamped, cast, embossed or printed on everything he makes and sells — the shopfront, the shopping bags, a belt buckle, a door, a wall, a plan, a frame, the hardware on a garment. **It is not restricted and it is not a rank.** Anyone who owns a Valder object owns the mark that is on it, so the V turns up wherever his products turn up, including on what people are wearing and carrying. What it is NOT is a membership pin handed out to insiders — nobody is admitted or refused because of it.

**THE CHROME STARBURST BELONGS TO VALDER HIMSELF AND TO HIS STAFF.** Valder wears an enormous chrome starburst at his throat; his guards carry a small chrome starburst on their cap badges. Customers do not wear it. The founder does not wear his own product mark and his customers do not wear his personal emblem — that separation is deliberate and it is part of the joke.
