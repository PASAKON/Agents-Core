10s · 360p · 16:9 · ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE, NO CUTS. An extreme wide shot on a wide lens, a slow lateral track from left to right keeping pace with THE MOUNT; no cut, no zoom.

THE GLASS SEA, LATE IN THE DAY. A vast underwater space: THE THREE RIDERS cross it, tiny, among giant glass creatures, as the day above begins to turn toward evening.

REFERENCES, each with a job:

Strapped to its back is a hand-built seat of weathered driftwood lashed with green kelp rope, pale bone ribs for a backrest, seashells and small glowing seed-pod lamps tied along it, tufts of glowing green grass at the corners. Take nothing of the white background.

Face, body and colours only; take nothing of the white background.
@Strong: THE STRONG ONE, the biggest man of the same people, about 1.9 m, broad and heavily muscled: deep moss-green skin with darker mottling on the shoulders and back, lime-green glowing freckles along the arms, emerald gill frills tipped gold, a woven kelp belt, a coiled kelp rope over one shoulder. Face, body and colours only; take nothing of the white background.
@Elder: THE ELDER, an old man of the same people, taller and thinner: deep teal-blue skin, violet gill frills with one frill torn, a small bead necklace, an upright, unhurried stance. Face, body and colours only; take nothing of the white background.

@GlassCathedral: THE DOME: a giant drifting dome of layered transparent veils with a rainbow core.
@GlassHalo: THE RINGS: giant floating rings of clear membrane, one inside another, trailing threads of light.
