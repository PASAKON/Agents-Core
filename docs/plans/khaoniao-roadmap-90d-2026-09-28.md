Why self-contained short clips get big views:
- Reels are served to non-followers; a self-contained episode needs no prior context, so any episode can break out (Meta ranks Reels by skip likelihood, MEASURED).
- The title asks "why did the cat do that?", which holds the first seconds.
- The warm ending gets shared to family (shares above).
- Volume: ~26 episodes produced 3 above 30k reactions; the rest 1–21k.
- The same character in every episode turns viewers into followers.

## 2. Audience: "mostly women 35+"?

Partly. Women 35+ are the largest single segment, not a majority: 27.2% of Thai Facebook users (ESTIMATE from NapoleonCat MEASURED splits), 23.9% of Thai TikTok adults (CLAIM, agency Ads Manager pull). The microdrama women-35+ skew (Digital i) covers 20 markets without Thailand. Comment signal on the competitor's 1M clip: 7 of 32 end with "ค่ะ/คะ" vs 2 with "ครับ"; 3 call themselves grandma or mum (MEASURED counts; the gender reading is a GUESS, n=32). Target women 25–54 with a 35+ core. Measure for free: Meta Ads reach estimate by age × gender (needs the Meta token renewed), then our own FB Insights after 8 episodes.

## 8. TikTok Shop

- Unlock: ≥1,000 TikTok followers, 18+, ID check before shoppable video or withdrawal (MEASURED). Target 1k before CP2 for 11.11 and 12.12. Account and ID are the CEO's.
- Buyer: women 25–54 (35+ core) with a dog or cat, tied to a home up-country, caring for parents or grandchildren, buying for the house (GUESS; check with Insights after CP1).
- Sell, in order: (1) pet supplies — food, treats, bowls, beds, collars, neckerchiefs; (2) Isan kitchen — หวด, กระติ๊บ, steamer pot, ข้าวเหนียว, แจ่ว; (3) household staples; (4) ผ้าขาวม้า, ผ้าถุง, cotton; (5) own merch after 50k.
- Never: supplements, health or beauty efficacy claims, medical advice; no basket in the emotional reveal (TH bans misleading invented stories; VN bans AI sad stories used to sell); no AI-drawn product images — real product footage or seller samples, AI label on every clip (TikTok Shop TH, 2026-03-16, MEASURED).
- Format: separate 15–30 s «ของในบ้านยายคำ» clips, one ข้าวเหนียว line, basket attached. Facebook: Shopee tag (1 per post, 2% base, cap ฿225) on ordinary posts only.
- Test: 10 product clips in 2 weeks across 3 categories; keep a category at ≥0.2 orders per 1k views.
