## 1. Answer first

1. **Animal: a dog.** «ข้าวเหนียว» is a cream Thai village dog with dark-brown ears and a faded red-and-white checked neckerchief. He lives with ยายคำ in an Isan village. **Runner-up: a baby elephant.**
2. **The page:** a **new** Facebook page, «บ้านนี้มีข้าวเหนียว», not the ILAG page. Episodes run 3:10–3:30. The dog speaks in the first person with a light Isan lilt, and each story runs misdirection → turn → heartwarming reveal. Episodes are numbered, each one closes, and a light 8-episode season line runs under them.
3. **First test:** 8 episodes posted over 14 days. **Cap: 3,800 Flow credits**, about ฿1,330 of the Ultra pool we have already paid for (~$39). New cash: ฿0. Cost per episode is **~460 credits (ESTIMATE, range 384–613)**. An **18-credit smoke test** comes before anything else.
4. **Kill:** the median episode gets under 3,000 3-second views at 72 h **and** no episode passes 20,000. **Or** views come but follows stay under 0.5 per 1,000 views. **Or** Meta marks any post unoriginal or not recommendable.
5. **Continue:** a median of ≥ 10,000 3-second views, or one episode ≥ 100,000, **with** ≥ 2 follows per 1,000 views and average watch time ≥ 40 s. Anything in between gets one more 4-episode round, then a final decision. All thresholds are GUESSES fixed before the test; §11 gives the reasons.
6. **The scary question:** do viewers follow *กาฟิว* rather than the *format*? If they do, a copy gets views but no follows. The follow-rate gate answers it.
7. **Money:** Thai ad-share is tiny at any reach we can plan for (ESTIMATE, §9). Brand deals and an owned LINE OA list are the real path.
8. **Timing:** the Flow balance read **~3,010 on 2026-09-28** (MEASURED [F]), and ILAG films draw from the same pool. **I recommend doing the free prep now and shooting after the monthly refill, around 2026-10-18** (ESTIMATE: one month after the 2026-09-18 subscription).

## 2. The viewer's job (JTBD)

**The job.** *"When I have three free minutes (lunch, after dinner, lying down before sleep), I want to feel something warm, maybe cry a little and have it end well, and be reassured that loyalty and goodness are real and that someone misjudged gets vindicated. Then I close the app lighter, with something sweet to send to my family group."*

- **Functional:** fill a short gap with a story that finishes.
- **Emotional:** a squeeze, then relief. This is the 虐→爽 shape. 逆袭 (underdog vindicated) is the #1 genre by ad-heat in China (MEASURED, DataEye, via Wikis `2026-09-25-chinese-vertical-microdrama-formula`).
- **Social:** something safe and kind to forward to a daughter or a family LINE group (GUESS; no Thai forwarding data exists).
- **Who:** women 35+. On microdrama YouTube channels, women 35–44 account for 20.8% of streams and women 45–54 for 15.7%. That is MEASURED third-party (Digital i, 20 markets, not Thailand alone) [R].
- **The pet angle:** Thailand has ~3.45M pet dogs and ~1.94M pet cats. These are ESTIMATES by a third party (Kasikorn Research projection for 2025, via Nation) [W]. Cats are growing faster: +28%/yr against +19% for dogs, 2021–2024 (MEASURED third-party, same source) [W].

**Competitors for the same three minutes**

| Competitor | Why a viewer picks it for this job | Evidence | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scrolling past / doing nothing** | Costs nothing, so it is the default | FB Reels ranking predicts *skip* and *see less* (MEASURED, Meta [A] B1) | The first 3 s are the whole battle |
| **แมวซีรี่ย์ / กาฟิวซีรีย์** | Same job, same format, a character people already love | 210k + 44k followers; last 20 reels ≈ 5.1M displayed views (MEASURED [T]) | Proves the job exists at scale on FB |
| **Real-pet pages**, e.g. «น้องอั้ม ละครสั้น» | Authentic: a real animal, no AI label | 140k followers (MEASURED [T] §9) | Our weak spot is authenticity; §4 "Create" answers it |
| **Chinese anthropomorphic cat/dog AI dramas** (spreading to Thailand) | Rags-to-riches melodrama with pets | One creator: 1M+ followers on some accounts, ~20,000 yuan/month. Rabbits, parrots and monkeys "not as popular as dogs and cats" (MEASURED, journalism, Bangkokbiznews 2026-09-28 [W]) | Dogs and cats are the proven animals; the genre is being imported now |
| **AI fruit / moral dramas** | Fast, absurd lakorn plots with a moral | Ai Cinema: 2.5M followers in under 10 days, ~15M views per clip (MEASURED, journalism [R]) | The moral shape travels even with absurd characters |
| **Lakorn clips / dubbed drama compilations** | Real actors, a known genre | "Shop ละคร": 3.43M subs, 641M views (MEASURED third-party [R]) | Real-person drama still out-pulls AI: 25× views per title in China (MEASURED [R]) |
| **Dharma-story pages / AI monk** | Reassurance that goodness is rewarded | หลวงตาบุญจริง: 12M views and 50k followers in 3 days (MEASURED, journalism [R]) | Same emotional job, and a religious-sensitivity trap we avoid (no monks) |
| **Generic Thai AI-drama pages** | Romance and social drama | A 44k page had reels at 1.9M and 2.5M; weak pages averaged 1.8k–2.6k per reel (MEASURED [P]) | Gives the floor and ceiling for our thresholds (§11) |
| **Real hero-dog news clips** | True stories of a dog saving someone | No number found | A viewer-submitted-story line (§4) competes with these directly |

## 3. Animal choice

Scores run 1–5 and are **all GUESSES**; the reason for each sits in the row below the table.

| Criterion | Dog | Baby elephant | Buffalo | Rabbit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thai emotional fit | 5 | 5 | 4 | 2 |
| Everyday story range (home, market, soi) | 5 | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| AI consistency, easy to hold | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 |
| Rival space open | 4 | 5 | 5 | 3 |
| Cover / thumbnail pull | 5 | 5 | 2 | 4 |
| Production and policy safety | 4 | 2 | 4 | 4 |
| **Sum (GUESS)** | **26** | **22** | **19** | **19** |

**Dog (recommended)**
- **Fit:** the biggest owner base, at ~3.45M pet dogs (ESTIMATE, third-party). Loyalty is the most familiar animal-story engine (Hachiko).
- **Range:** a dog goes everywhere a person goes, the market, the soi, the field, so there is an unlimited supply of "looks bad, was love" premises.
- **Consistency:** dogs vary a lot, so identity has to be anchored. The coat and ear colours are symmetric so a mirrored frame cannot move a patch. One neckerchief comes from a reference plate.
- **Rivals:** the teardown ran 4 FB searches and a prior 8-page scan and found **no dog AI-drama page at scale** (MEASURED absence [T] §9). **«น้องหมา เค้าบอกว่า» (120k) is UNVERIFIED as a rival**: its content type was never opened, and my web search found nothing about it, because FB pages are not indexed. It needs one read-only look before launch.
- **Cover:** a dog's guilty or teary face plus a big headline is the strongest thumbnail we have (GUESS).

**Baby elephant (runner-up)**
- **Fit:** the national animal, and a real Isan tie: Kui elephant families in Surin. We would use a fictional village, never a real one.
- **Rivals:** one web search found no AI elephant drama page, only news of a fake viral elephant clip (MEASURED absence, weak [W]).
- **Weaknesses:**
  - story range is narrow, since an elephant cannot enter the house, the market or a pharmacy;
  - size will drift, as the platform scale did between shots on film 2 (MEASURED [F]);
  - welfare controversies (chains, riding, shows) must never appear;
  - Flow's behaviour on elephants is untested.
- **Best use:** a guest or spin-off once the dog proves the format.

**Buffalo.** The Isan nostalgia is strong (ไอ้ทุย). But buffalo look alike, which makes identity hard. It lives outdoors, which limits range. And "ควาย" is an everyday insult, which puts titles at risk. Good for a one-off guest, weak as a series lead.

**Rabbit.** Weak Thai everyday fit. The imported Chinese genre already uses rabbits, and they are "not as popular as dogs and cats" (MEASURED, journalism [W]).

**Duck.** Considered and dropped: narrow range and weak identity.

**Flow risk shared by every animal (unmeasured).** Nothing in our Flow skills measures an animal as a speaking or lead character. The smoke test in §11 is the first data.

## 5. Character bible v0 (words only; no image generation)

**ข้าวเหนียว (Khao Niao, "sticky rice")**
- **Look:** Thai village dog of mixed breed, medium size, about 3 years old. Short cream-white coat, **both ears dark brown** (symmetric, so a mirrored frame cannot move a patch), amber eyes, curled tail. A **faded red-and-white checked neckerchief** (ผ้าขาวม้า style) knotted at the neck. If the check pattern drifts in the smoke test, fall back to a plain faded red.
- **One saturated colour:** red. They use a green collar, and we never use one.
- **Voice:** first person, "ผม", with Isan particles (เด้อ, สิ, บ่). Warm young-adult male, slightly husky, light Isan lilt that a Central-Thai viewer still follows.
- **The mechanic:** **humans cannot hear him.** His lines are an inner voice only the audience hears, which is what makes the misdirection work.
- **Personality:** stubborn, lives for sticky rice, fiercely loyal to ยายคำ, terrified of thunder, secretly soft on the grumpy neighbour. He knows more than the humans and cannot tell them.
- **Never:** hurt, chained, beaten or starving on screen. The danger is implied, never shown. AI animal clips already draw "harms animals" criticism (Sanook, via [W]).

**Recurring humans (≤ 3; at most 1 guest per episode)**

| Name | Age | Look (one saturated colour) | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ยายคำ** | 68 | Indigo cotton shirt, checked sarong, betel-stained smile | Grows vegetables and sells them with ข้าวจี่ at the morning market. Sharp tongue, soft heart. The dog's person |
| **ภูมิ** | 26 | Mustard-yellow work T-shirt, phone always in hand | Grandson, back from a Bangkok factory job. He has not told anyone the factory closed; that secret is the season line |
| **ลุงเสาร์** | 63 | Khaki shirt, frayed straw hat | Widowed neighbour and rice farmer who says he hates dogs. The misdirection target, and he softens slowly |

**No child as a recurring lead.** On Flow, a child's reference images were deleted in 6 of 6 prompts, and child + money in one prompt was deleted (MEASURED on film 2 [F]).

**Home location.** ยายคำ's raised wooden house on stilts beside the paddy, with an open ใต้ถุน underneath: a bamboo แคร่, a clay water jar, กระติ๊บ baskets. Weathered grey boards and a patched zinc roof. Image models prettify poverty ([F] memory), so the plate prompt must name the patches and the clutter.

**Second location:** the village morning market or the paddy bank. Never write "dyke" in an English prompt, because Flow refuses it (MEASURED on film 4 [F]).

**Never copy.** Not their names (กาฟิว, ฟิว, กาแฟ). Not an orange tabby, not a green collar, not their logo or their title-card typography. Not their five premises: the "villain" pet that is really protecting, the pet a man never meant to keep, dream job versus pet, grandpa's old shoes, the stray running off with medicine. We saw only 5 of their episodes, so an operator must do one read-only keyword check of their page before each script locks.

**Never use** the name ทองแดง, or any royal reference. Never use the dog-meat-trade stereotype about Isan.

## 6. Episode formula + 10 loglines

**Beat sheet: 3:20 (200 s), about 32 shots** (ESTIMATE: 200 s ÷ ~6.25 s per cut; the competitor cuts every 5–8 s, MEASURED on 2 of 5 [T])

| # | Beat | Seconds | Shots | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title hook** | 0–5 | 1 | Big Thai headline plus a one-line curiosity subtitle, burned over the first moving shot of the "bad" act. Example: «ข้าวเหนียวขโมยมือถือยาย!» / «ยายโกรธจนจะเอาไปปล่อยวัด». Conflict on screen within 10 s [S] |
| 2 | **Misdirection** | 5–40 | 2–6 | The act, the humans' anger, and the dog's inner voice hinting without explaining: «ผมรู้ ยายโกรธ แต่ผมบอกบ่ได้เด้อ» |
| 3 | **Pressure** | 40–100 | 7–16 | The humans settle on a consequence: tie him up, give him away. At ~60 s one line leaves a live question |
| 4 | **Turn** | 100–150 | 17–24 | Someone follows the dog or catches part of the truth ("a corner of the truth" hook [S]) |
| 5 | **Reveal** | 150–180 | 25–29 | The "bad" act was love or protection. The humans' faces change |
| 6 | **Payoff** | 180–193 | 30–31 | A material change, not just an apology: savings kept, a man rescued, a family reunited |
| 7 | **Ending line / CTA** | 193–200 | 32 | Signature sticky-rice ritual (a reused shot), the dog's first-person closing line, and one season-line hint. The follow line goes only in the Page's pinned first comment, with no engagement bait [S][A] |

Writing rules carried over from `CMO_Standard_Story_ThaiMoralDrama` [S]:
- 80–90% of shots carry a line, and never two silent shots in a row;
- the character acts WHILE speaking, and the prompt never says "then";
- a space at each phrase boundary in Thai lines;
- a particle between a number and a vocative;
- a loud emotion line in every shot;
- the continuous-take sentence in every dialogue shot (it removed in-clip cuts in 0/9 → 0/10 clips, MEASURED on film 4 [F]).

The karma and villain rules do **not** carry over. This format is misjudgement → vindication, not crime → punishment.

## 7. Production recipe + cost per episode

**Machines.**
- **Flow runs on winbox's Chrome only, never on the Mac** (CEO 2026-09-26 [S]).
- The Mac edits.
- Clips move to the Mac as finished files through Drive or scp.
- **No H3.** Per second, H3 at 360p (~$0.12 per 15 s = $0.008/s, MEASURED median [S]) costs the same as a Flow 360p test (6 credits × ฿0.35 = ฿2.10 ≈ $0.062 per 8 s = $0.0078/s, ESTIMATE at ฿34/$). But H3 adds a paid pod session (~$0.38 boot, MEASURED [S]) that the CEO has to open, and its results do not transfer to Flow.

**Credits per episode: ESTIMATE, maths shown**
- Shots: 200 s ÷ 6.25 s = **32 shots** (ESTIMATE).
- Raw: 32 × 12 credits (Omni 720p 8 s, MEASURED [S]) = **384 credits**. This is the floor, with no re-fires.
- All-in rate from **film 4**: 1,036 credits ÷ 72 shots = **14.4 credits/shot**. The 1,036 is MEASURED as a balance delta (4,334 → 3,298) that includes a ~12-credit voice test [F]. So 32 × 14.4 = **461 ≈ 460 credits: the central estimate.**
- Pessimistic rate from **film 2**: ~1,437 credits ÷ 75 shots = 19.2/shot (MEASURED sum of act spends: 401 + 524 + 32 + 480 [F]; it included vanished takes and re-shoots). So 32 × 19.2 = **613 credits**.
- In money: 460 credits × ฿0.35 (฿3,500 ÷ 10,000 credits, MEASURED plan price [S]) = **฿161 ≈ $4.7 per episode** at ฿34/$. The range is ฿134–฿215 (≈ $3.9–$6.3).
- **These credits are prepaid and do not roll over** [S]. The real cost is the ILAG films those credits would otherwise shoot. One 10-minute ILAG film ≈ 1,036 credits [F], so **one dog episode ≈ 0.44 of an ILAG film** (ESTIMATE: 460 ÷ 1,036).

**Monthly load at 4 episodes/week:** 4 × 52 ÷ 12 = 17.3 episodes × 460 ≈ **7,970 credits, about 80% of the 10,000 pool** (ESTIMATE). A "continue" therefore needs a credit decision (§12).

## 10. The scary question

**"Do people follow กาฟิว, a specific cat they have watched for months (and, by the page's own post, one tied to a real cat's life), rather than the format? If yes, a new dog gets the format's views but not its follows, and the page never compounds."**

**How the test answers it.** All of these are fixed before the first post:

| Signal | What it means | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Follows per 1,000 3-s views (sum over 8 episodes) | Does the character convert viewers into returners? | **Kill < 0.5 · continue ≥ 2** (§11) |
| Share of views from followers, EP5–8 against EP1–4 | Are people coming back for *him*? | It must rise. Flat or falling counts against "continue" |
| Comments naming ข้าวเหนียว, per 100 comments on EP5–8 | Attachment to the character, not the plot | ≥ 10 per 100 (GUESS). Recorded, not a hard gate |
| Views pass but follows fail | Result: **the format pulls and the character does not** | Stop, or re-plan as an anthology. Do not scale |

**Two other killers, named so they do not surprise anyone:**
- **A look-alike penalty.**
  - Meta's originality rule targets reuse and duplicates of *other people's* content. AI is allowed if the work is original (MEASURED [PR]). Our dog, our stories and our footage are original, so Meta's risk is low (GUESS).
  - YouTube's "mimics existing formats… interchangeable" wording is the bigger risk (MEASURED text [PR]). The mitigation is rotating reveal types, the season line and the Isan identity.
  - The test's gate: any "unoriginal / not recommendable" flag in the Professional Dashboard = kill.
- **Can Flow make the dog talk at all?** Flow cannot do an off-frame voice-over (MEASURED [S]). An animal's inner voice is untested. **The 18-credit smoke test answers this before any episode is shot.**

## 11. Cheapest test

**Step 0: free, now**

**Step 2: the shoot, after the refill (~2026-10-18, ESTIMATE)**

- **Stop rule:** any single episode passing 615 credits (the film-2 pessimistic bound) → stop and report.

**Schedule**
- Day 1 is a Monday.
- Posts go out on days 1–4 and 8–11 at 19:30.
- Each episode is read in FB Insights at **72 h**, with a day-7 read as a cross-check.
- EP8's 72-h read falls on day 14. **The decision is made on day 15.**

**Metrics** (FB Insights, per episode): 3-second views · average watch time · follows per 1,000 3-s views · shares per 1,000 3-s views · share of views from followers · comments naming the dog · account and recommendation status · credits spent.

**Thresholds, fixed now (all GUESSES; the reasons follow the table)**

| Metric (72 h) | KILL | CONTINUE |
|---|---|---|
| Median 3-s views over the 8 episodes | **< 3,000 and** best episode < 20,000 | **≥ 10,000, or** any episode ≥ 100,000 |
| Follows per 1,000 3-s views (8-episode total) | **< 0.5** (even if views pass) | **≥ 2** |
| Average watch time (of 200 s) | < 15 s = the hook is failing. Fix it; this alone does not kill | **≥ 40 s** (20%) |
| Shares per 1,000 3-s views | < 0.5 = warning | ≥ 3 = supporting signal |
| Policy | Any unoriginal / not-recommendable flag or AI-policy strike = **kill** | None |
| Cost | Median > 615 credits/episode or the 3,800 cap broken = **kill** | Median ≤ 500 credits/episode |

- **Kill** if any KILL cell fires.
- **Continue** if every CONTINUE cell in the rows for views, follows, watch time, policy and cost holds.
- **Anything else = iterate once:** 4 more episodes, cap 1,900 credits, change exactly one lever (the one behind the weakest metric), same thresholds. No third round without the CEO.

**Why these numbers (GUESS reasoning)**
- **3,000:** the weakest Thai AI-drama pages averaged 1.8k–2.6k views per reel (MEASURED [P]: 18,163 ÷ 10 and 26,071 ÷ 10). Being at that level after 8 episodes means the format is not pulling for us.
- **10,000:** about half the ~21,000 per episode that the FB proxy target needs (§9).
- **100,000:** about a tenth of the competitor's best reel (1M, MEASURED [T]), and below Natthika's top reel (153k, MEASURED [P]). One such hit shows the ceiling is reachable.
- **Follows:**
  - at 0.5 per 1,000, reaching 10k followers takes 20M views, which is out of reach at kill-level reach;
  - at 2 per 1,000 it takes 5M, which one competitor-sized hit (1M) plus steady episodes can reach (ESTIMATE: 10,000 ÷ 0.5 × 1,000; 10,000 ÷ 2 × 1,000).
- **Caveat:** 14 days may be too short for a zero-follower page to warm up (GUESS). That is why an "iterate once" band exists.
