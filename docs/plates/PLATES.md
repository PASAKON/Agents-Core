# Valder plates — what each image ACTUALLY shows

Written by looking at the files in this directory, not at the specs they were
generated from. **The two are not the same thing, and the gap between them is
where the mistakes live.**

Rule this exists to serve (R-24): look at the plate, write down what is in it,
*then* write the prompt. Anything below marked ⚠️ is a place where a prompt
described something the image does not contain.

**Read this file instead of re-opening the images.** An image read stays in
context and is paid for on every later turn; this file is cheap.

Progress: **17 of 46 viewed.** The rest are listed at the bottom as not yet seen.

### `char_grandma` 🔴 REGENERATE
Very old woman, deeply lined. Layers of faded knitwear — headscarf over cardigan
over cardigan. **Enormous canvas bag.** Worn boots. Stooped. Warm skin.
🔴 **She reads as destitute, not as poor-but-cared-for.** The bag is visibly

She is additionally **a whole class poorer than the rest of the family**, who
read as respectable working people. She appears in S2, S3, S4, S6 and S7A, so
the mismatch shows in five scenes.

## Props

### `prop_mark` ✅ — the canonical trademark
A capital **V in brushed matte warm gold**, flat, on a plain warm-grey ground.
**Thick strokes, wide at the top, tapering to a sharp point at the bottom** — an
aggressive, wide-shouldered taper. No bevel, no crossbar, no serif, no 3D
extrusion, no outline. Left stroke very slightly heavier than the right, which
reads as cast or struck rather than drawn.
Distinct enough from real fashion marks: the angle is far wider and there is no
crossbar. Change nothing — this is the reference every other appearance copies.

### `prop_frame` ✅
Ornate gilt frame with rococo scrollwork, hung flat on a warm neutral wall.
Behind glass: **a sheet that was crumpled into a ball and flattened out again** —
deep creases in every direction, curled corners, **a torn corner at the top
right**. On it, **a child-like house in plain black line: one square, a triangle
roof, a door, two windows.** Below it an **enormous sweeping signature.**
*The image invented something better than the spec:* **the signature BEGINS with
the same wide-shouldered tapered V as the trademark** before running away into an
illegible flourish. His handwriting and his maker's mark are the same letterform.
Now stated in every prompt that shows the frame.

### `prop_shopping_bags` ✅
**Three or four rigid structured carrier bags in deep navy**, flat navy ribbon
handles, standing upright on a warm neutral ground. **A large gold V centred on
the face of each — the only marking. No text of any kind.** Same V as `prop_mark`.
Change nothing.
