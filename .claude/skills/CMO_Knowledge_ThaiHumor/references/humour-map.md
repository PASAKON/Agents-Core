## Why Thai audiences laugh (the common root)
1. **สนุก is a value, not a bonus.** Fun is expected in any setting, even news, sales or a sad story. Comedy that
   barges into a serious frame (news desk, court, lakorn, army) lands harder than comedy in a comedy frame.
2. **Hierarchy play under เกรงใจ.** Daily life forbids talking back to elders, bosses, sergeants. Comedy lets the
   low-status person win with ความกวน (cheek), safely. The audience laughs at the release.
3. **Tonal language = cheap wordplay.** Five tones, many near-homophones, a 200-year คำผวน tradition (หนังตะลุง, ลิเก).
   Thai viewers are trained to catch a sound twist in under a second.
4. **The laugh cue is shared.** Café/ลิเก comedy taught a cue system: ตบมุก drum, ตึ่งโป๊ะ, a slap, a freeze. The cue
   tells the room "laugh now", and edited Shorts copy it with sound effects and zoom-ins.
5. **Group, not solo.** Thai comedy is mostly a คณะ (troupe) or a pair: one straight man, one กวน. The straight
   man's reaction is often the real punch.
