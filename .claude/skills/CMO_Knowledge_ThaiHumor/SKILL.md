## Why Thai viewers laugh (2026-10-29, humour-map.md)
1. สนุก is expected everywhere, so comedy that breaks into a serious frame (news desk, court, lakorn, army, temple)
   lands harder than comedy in a comedy frame.
2. Hierarchy play under เกรงใจ: the low-status person wins with polite ความกวน (ครับ/ค่ะ/ขอรับ kept on). The laugh is release.
3. A tonal language makes sound play cheap: คำพ้องเสียง, mishearing, clean คำผวน. Viewers decode in about 1 s.
4. A shared laugh cue (ตบมุก drum, ตึ่งโป๊ะ, a slap sound, a freeze) tells the viewer "now". Shorts copy it with SFX + zoom.
5. Comedy is a pair or troupe: the straight man's frozen face is often the real punch.
6. Theory that fits all of it: a violation that is at the same time benign (McGraw & Warren, Psychological Science 2010);
   too real or too cruel stops being funny.

## The 12 mechanisms (each: timing, voice, best job; evidence and links in references/humour-map.md)
| # | Mechanism | Timing that works | Voice / cue | Best job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ตบมุก + sound cue | setup 3-6 s, 0.3-0.5 s silence, punch, cue on the punch frame | straight man rises on the last word | mid, ending |
| 2 | Slapstick (objects, falls) | 1-2 s anticipation, hit, 0.5-1 s frozen face | denial line before the pain | hook (motion), blooper |
| 3 | มุกแป้ก on purpose | punch, then 1-2 s dead silence or crickets | teller proud, room flat | mid, ending (weak hook) |
| 4 | คำผวน (clean only) | say it straight, 1-1.5 s decode pause, second voice repeats | no wink | mid, ending |
| 5 | Mishearing / double meaning | wrong answer within 0.5 s, 0.5-1 s frozen face | sincere, never smug | hook (≤4 s), mid |
| 6 | Literal answer (ความกวน) | question 2-3 s, answer 0.5 s after, no pause | flat, quick, polite particle | hook (question), ending |
| 7 | Deadpan | no pause before the absurd word; 0.5-1 s after | low, official, locked camera | hook (best muted) |
| 8 | เอ๊ะ reversal / หักมุม | flip at 70-80% of runtime, 1 s reaction, cut | everyone sincere until the flip | ending; the title is the hook |
| 9 | Lakorn parody | 3-5 s straight lakorn, one ordinary line breaks it | lakorn vibrato, then normal | hook (drama zoom) |
| 10 | Dub-over / cheeky subtitle | first line at 0 s, a new line every 2-3 s | casual, fast vs a grand image | hook (strongest measured) |
| 11 | Market banter (แม่ค้า) | a line every 0.8-1.5 s, calm one-liner ends it | customer loud, vendor calm | hook (mid-fight), mid |
| 12 | Accent play, kindly | from inside, with warmth | speaker of that dialect | mid only |

## The first 3 seconds (2026-10-29, references/thumbstop.md)
Facts, opened at the source: TikTok asks for the content proposition in the first 3 s, the hook within 6 s, on-screen
text at 5-10 words/s, and people on screen (ads.tiktok.com creative best practices). Google ABCD (5,000+ YouTube ads,
correlational): surprising visuals early, tight framing on people, more than two frames in the first 5 s, people
speaking to camera. TikTok/Nielsen 2020 (US): 88% say sound is vital on TikTok. Facebook silent-view figures
(82-85%) are 2016 and secondary: assume Reels may be muted. Our heatmaps: short Thai clips are rewound at second 0-4.
No Thai sound-on/off figure exists (searched 2026-10-29).

Checklist for every hook:
1. Frame 1 is a face, close, mid-expression.
2. Frame 1 already shows something wrong (a person in the wrong role or place, an absurd object).
3. Frame 1 moves. No still title card, no fade-in.
4. Frame 1 carries a big subtitle of 6-12 Thai words inside the safe zone.
5. The first spoken word is the premise. No greeting, no logo.
6. One audio cue in second 0-1 for sound-on viewers.
7. Seconds 1-2 open a loop (a question, a mishearing, a promise the end pays).
8. The first cut (the reaction face) by second 2-3.
9. By second 3 the viewer knows what the clip is about; the punch lands by second 6.
10. Write it to work twice: with sound (voice + cue) and muted (face + subtitle).

## How to write a hook with this skill
1. Pick the channel's job (ILAG moral drama, KhaoHom survival, Jade Tally drama dub, B-roll opener) and 1-2 mechanisms
   from the table that fit a hook.
2. Write frame 1 first (checklist 1-4), then the line, then the timing in seconds, then the voice direction.
3. Run the benign check (below), then `scripts/check-post-text.sh` on anything public.
4. Copy the format of `references/hooks-20.md`. Those 20 are ours to reuse; test 2-3 per channel before scaling.

## Rules that apply here (pointers)
- Others' jokes are ideas only (CEO 2026-10-11): learn the mechanism, never copy a line, catchphrase or sketch.
- No crude words (no มึง/กู, no swearing), even though many popular clips use them. Never write blush/หน้าแดง/หูแดง.
- No punching down. **DON'T USE** forms seen in the sample: jokes on gender identity (common in พากย์นรก Shorts),
  body shape or looks, disability, skin colour, ethnicity, region-as-insult accent mockery, and bawdy คำผวน whose
  decoded form is sexual. Hitting a person: show the sound or the freeze before contact, never the hit.
- No guillemets, em dashes, middle dots or arrows in Thai public text (`scripts/check-post-text.sh`).
