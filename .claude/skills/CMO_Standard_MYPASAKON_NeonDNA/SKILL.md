# What a good MYPASAKON (`@mypasakon`) Neon Green video is

This standard defines the **Minimalist Luxury Neon Green (`#040A07` Obsidian × `#7CFF00` Acid Neon)** visual identity, the proprietary **`Style A: RAZOR CHAMFER`** thick-to-thin frame architecture, the **`@keanu.visuals` single-focus progressive pacing**, and the **Google Flow `Omni 1.2 Flash`** content-matched B-roll workflow for **`MYPASAKON` (`@mypasakon`)** `9:16` (`1080x1920`, `30fps`) short-form videos. Approved and locked by the CEO across five iterative rulings on 2026-11-09 (`Q-20261109-0447-822a`, `Q-20261109-0458-5221`, `Q-20261109-0513-4cd4`, `Q-20261109-0547-41a6`, and `Q-20261109-0646-e1b5`).

## When to invoke / when NOT to

- **Invoke** whenever designing UI/UX overlays, generating Google Flow `Omni 1.2 Flash` B-roll, or editing any `9:16` vertical video for **`MYPASAKON` (`@mypasakon`)**.
- **Do NOT invoke** for `BLACK LIQUIDITY` (`CMO_Workflow_BlackLiquidity`), `RED SEAL ARCHIVE` (`CMO_Standard_GhostChannel_DNA`), or vertical moral drama (`CMO_Standard_Story_ThaiMoralDrama`).
- **Refusal precondition:** Refuse to render or ship any cut if (a) a visual/B-roll clip does not directly illustrate the exact words spoken at that second, (b) a subtitle exceeds 4 words or overlaps the speaker's face, or (c) the circular avatar crops into the speaker's head/face.

## The spec

### 1. Editorial core law — One Single Focus & 100% Content-Matched Visuals (`Q-20261109-0547-41a6`)

1. **HARD — Every visual on screen must directly explain and prove the spoken words at that exact second (`Q-20261109-0547-41a6`).**
   **Why hard:** scope — the CEO ruled on 2026-11-09: *"เราต้องรู้ว่าแต่ละวินาทีจะให้คนดู focus อะไรกันแน่ แล้วภาพนั้นจะอธิบายอะไรให้คนดู เป็นภาพประกอบเนื้อหายังไง ไม่ใช่ทำ footage แบบส่งๆ แบบนี้ ไม่เข้ากับเนื้อหาเลย keep minimal ให้ดูเรียบง่าย แต่ดูแพง น่าเชื่อถือ ทุกอย่างที่เอาเข้ามาแสดงให้คนดูต้องมีเหตุผล และทำให้คนดูเข้าใจสิ่งที่จะสื่อจากคำพูดมากขึ้น"*. Never use mood/color reference images (such as the CEO's neon green Mercedes-AMG birthday photo) as literal B-roll footage when the script talks about AI trading or code.
2. **HARD — One single focal point per second and 2–4 words max per subtitle (`Q-20261109-0547-41a6`).**
   **Why hard:** scope — fast-cut vertical reels fail if the viewer has to read competing headers, English paragraphs, or multi-box clutter. At any second, show **1 visual focus + 1 short pill label (1–3 words) + 1 single-line subtitle (2–4 words)**.
3. **HARD — Zero top-right `/MY PASAKON` watermark and zero date stamp (`Q-20261109-0447-822a`).**
   **Why hard:** scope — explicitly rejected by the CEO on 2026-11-09 (`"ไม่เอา 9 พ.ย. 69 และไม่เอา /MY PASAKON ข้างบนขวา"`).

```bash
ffmpeg -y -i <output.mp4> \
```
