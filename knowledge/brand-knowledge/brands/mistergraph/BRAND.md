## What it is
Short Thai lessons on **free built-in TradingView indicators**, one indicator per video, explained the way a friend
would explain it over a chart. Education only.
- **Job it does for the viewer:** "show me one tool I can switch on tonight and read correctly."
- **Real competitor:** free signal groups and "แม่นมาก" indicator clips that promise a shortcut. We win on
  honesty: every video says when the tool fails.

## Type (OFL only)
- Captions and cards: **Kanit** SemiBold (rounded geometric Thai, close to his old captions).
- Wordmark "Mr. Graph": a white bold serif, **Playfair Display** Bold, matched to his badge.

## Format (one video)
- 60–90 s, 1080x1920, **one indicator, one lesson**.
- 0–3 s: the chart already moving + a two-line title card (white line + orange line) that names the indicator.
- Mascot: a 2 s badge sting at the start, then he points at the chart; he is never a talking head.
- **Chart ≥ 55% of the frame height.** Smooth push-in on the candles every 6–12 s, no hard jumps.
- Draw on the chart in white (ellipse, arrow, price path). Green = up, red = down.
- Captions: two lines in the upper third, white + one coloured line, new text every 2–4 s.
  Spell-check every line.
- One honest "when it fails" beat in every video.
- Last 4 s: a question to the viewer, or a "next: <indicator name>" card.
- Voice mastered to about −14 LUFS.

## Hard rules
1. **Education only.** No profit promise, no "buy/sell now", no signal. Titles never claim accuracy or a win rate
   ("แม่นมาก", "ชนะ 90%"); name the tool and the situation instead.
2. **No broker or sponsor card, logo or name, anywhere in a public video or caption** (CMO 26 Oct 2026, under the
   CEO's Sept 2026 ruling: broker links only in closed channels). His old broker end card is not carried over.
3. Every video carries the red risk card once, mid-video: "คลิปนี้ทำเพื่อการศึกษาเท่านั้น ไม่ได้ชักชวนลงทุน".
4. **Built-in indicators only** (`indicators-20.md`), so a viewer can repeat it in one click. Every setting and
   reading rule is checked against TradingView's own help page, and the page is listed in the episode notes.
5. **TradingView's brand:** follow their published rules for showing charts. When in doubt, draw our own chart
   from real public OHLC data and name TradingView as the source. Never fake their UI.
6. Nothing is posted without the CEO; posting to his account is his call.
7. No em dash in public copy (IRON-RULES §39).
