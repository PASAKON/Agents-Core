# Competitor teardown — CC Ai Flow (เร่งธุรกิจโตด้วย Ai)

Researched 2026-07-24 via Meta Ad Library (page_id 788312664356142). Closest real
match to BrandPrompt TH's model: page-brand, zero face, selling a low-price
AI knowledge product. 52 active ads live at time of research, only ~3 unique
copy variants rotated across those IDs (they relaunch the same 2-3 winners
under fresh ad IDs repeatedly — a spray tactic, not 52 distinct creatives).

## Offer
- Product: "Claude Skills Ultimate Bundle" — 500+ prebuilt Claude Skills, 16
  business categories, install once, works in Claude Chat/Code/Cowork
- Price: 997 THB one-time (anchored against "มูลค่าจริง 23,993 บาท")
- Guarantee: 7-day 100% refund, no conditions (stated repeatedly)
- Social proof: "ลูกค้ากว่า 1,000+ รายใช้งานอยู่ตอนนี้"
- Site: cc.aiflowlab.co

## Page identity (why it matches our model)
- Zero human face anywhere — profile pic is a robot mascot icon
- Page name is a benefit statement ("เร่งธุรกิจโตด้วย Ai"), not a person's name
- First-person "ผม" narrative voice in copy, but never shows who "ผม" is

## Ad copy — 2 core formulas seen (rotated across 52 ad IDs)

**Formula A — personal-testimonial hook:**
1. Hook: "#claudeskills ผมอึ้งที่จะบอกว่า..." + specific before/after number (3-5 ชม → 2 นาที)
2. `=========` divider line
3. Reframe common belief ("หลายคนคิดว่าแค่ prompt ดีก็พอ") then 4x ❌ pain bullets
4. Mechanism explainer ("Skills ไม่ใช่ prompt มันคือระบบ...")
5. 3x ✅ differentiator bullets
6. Social proof line + guarantee line
7. Price + CTA link repeated
8. "ปล. เม้น 'สนใจ'..." comment-bait
9. Hashtag block

**Formula B — pain-headline / persona-list:**
1. Headline: "ทำงานหนักแต่ไม่โตสักที?" — pain question, not testimonial
2. Solopreneur-overload narrative (ต้องควบทุกตำแหน่งคนเดียว)
3. Product intro
4. 5-7x persona-specific benefit bullets (ฟรีแลนซ์/เอเจนซี่/ครีเอเตอร์/ผู้ขาย/สตาร์ทอัพ...)
5. 3-step simplicity claim (ดาวน์โหลด → ถาม Claude → เสร็จ)
6. Price anchor + guarantee (same block as Formula A)
7. CTA button copy: "เรียนรู้เพิ่มเติม" (Learn More objective, NOT Send Message —
   differs from อ.เก่ง ภูวนัย's Messages-objective approach found earlier)

Both formulas: heavy emoji + ❌/✅ bullets in ADS specifically (consistent with
our existing finding that ads register ≠ organic register — organic stays
emoji-free per PLAYBOOK.md §6).

## Visual style — differs from our current approach
Creative is a **flat infographic**, not a photorealistic AI-generated scene:
- Timeline/schedule mockup (icons + time slots, e.g. 08:00/10:30/14:00 rows)
- Big crossed-out price numbers (8,997 → 500 style strike-through)
- Robot mascot brand mark, flat clean UI-style graphic, no photography
- This is NOT what our poster templates do (we build photorealistic
  product-hero/lifestyle scenes) — worth testing a flat-infographic ad
  variant alongside our existing scene-based ones.

## What NOT to copy verbatim (CEO flagged wanting "100% copy" — pushed back)
- Their exact sentences/hooks — original copyrighted text, legal risk
- Their numbers (1,000+ customers, 23,993 THB value, 7-day refund) — these
  are THEIR real claims. Using them as ours is a false/fabricated claim
  (we have zero customers at this point) — Meta ad-review + trust risk.
- Adopt structure/rhythm/formula 1:1. Swap in BrandPrompt TH's real facts.
