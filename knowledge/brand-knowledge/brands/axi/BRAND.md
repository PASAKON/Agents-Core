# Axi — Competitor Creative Reference (teardown)

Captured 2026-06-16 by CMO. 8 Thai-market posters saved in `images/`.

**What this is:** a *creative-craft benchmark*, NOT an offer template to copy.
Axi = a broker (AxiTrader LLC). MoonieX = an IB / rebate house. We do not
compete with Axi on its own offers (deposit bonus, funded capital) — we sit
*on top of* brokers like XM/Exness. Mirroring these posters would make MoonieX
look like a broker echo with no reason to exist. Steal the *craft*, reject the
*positioning*. See "Strategic note" at the bottom.

## Brand signature
- **Palette:** crimson red (`axi` logo) + near-black/charcoal gradient bg + a
  warm **gold-yellow CTA pill** (their conversion colour). Profit visuals go
  green; danger/loss red. Heavy red = energetic, slightly aggressive ("push").
- **Logo:** lowercase `axi` red wordmark (white variant on busy art).
- **Format discipline:** one offer per poster, one big-number hero, one CTA.
- **Compliance:** every post carries a risk-disclaimer bar (AxiTrader LLC, OTC
  derivatives high-risk). Consistent. We should copy this discipline.
- **3D render quality:** premium gold rocket / chrome Starship / shield —
  cinematic, high contrast. This is the bar.

## The funnel these 8 form (why Axi runs them)
- **Reach (cheap):** 07/08 SpaceX newsjack — topical, no offer cost.
- **Trust:** 04 segregated funds — de-risk the switch for scam-wary TH traders.
- **Activate (low cost):** 01 $10 deposit, 06 $30 bonus — top-of-funnel volume.
- **Aspire / retain (moat):** 02/03/05 Axi Select + Edge Score — broker-as-prop
  hybrid; gamified progression that competitors can't copy by *name*.
Full-funnel, disciplined, one-job-per-asset. That's the lesson.

## Steal vs reject
**Steal:** one-message-per-poster; big-number hero; high-contrast CTA pill;
mandatory risk-disclaimer bar; newsjacking (we already have SPCX samples);
3D-render premium feel.
**Reject:** red palette (we stay navy+gold = premium/trust, not "gambly");
bonus-gimmick framing; vanity metric as the hero ("Edge Score" is a number,
not a result).

## Strategic note — the scary question for MoonieX
If brokers like Axi now offer $10 entry + $30 bonus + funded $1M + a gamified
Edge Score **directly**, why would a Thai trader route through MoonieX (an IB)
at all? Answer MoonieX must own in every campaign: **recurring rebate cashback
(real money back per lot, forever) + Thai trader-mindset/edge coaching + LuNar
AI assistant on LINE.** A one-time $30 bonus expires; $15/lot back never does.
Posters that don't say what *only MoonieX* gives are invisible. See campaign
concepts (CMO session 2026-06-16).
