## What it is

- A **series brand** on the existing YouTube channel **ILAG Studio** (`@ILAGStudio`, `UCrr-ePuA_M5peOw96wx5T8w`). It is **not** a new channel and the channel is **not** renamed.
- Written form: `The Old Telling` (title case, never "TOT", never all caps in running text). Lockup: `The Old Telling · by ILAG Studio`.
- Format: narrated world-mythology and folklore stories, 8 to 12 min, chapters on every upload (a section every 2 to 3 minutes), AI B-roll made in the H3 studio, one story per video.

## Job and promise

- Job the viewer hires us for: lean-back story listening, with the feeling of hearing **the real, darker version** of a story they thought they knew. (Not job A/B/C of the forex lanes.)
- Promise line (EN): **The version they softened.**
- Promise line (TH, draft v1, CEO ear-check): **ตำนานฉบับเต็ม ที่คนมักไม่เล่า**. (v0 "ฉบับก่อนถูกทำให้เบาลง" was a literal copy of the English line and the CEO could not follow it.) **Every language gets its own tagline written in that language, not a translation** (see the decision note, section Localisation).
- Real rivals: TikTok and Reels scroll, Netflix, podcasts, sleep audio, doing nothing. Direct rivals: Mysteries of Mythology, Jon Solo, See U in History.

## Voice

- Serious, atmospheric, measured, dry. A storyteller who knows the ending and does not hurry.
- Sentences short. One image per sentence. No shouting, no "you won't believe", no horror jump-scare tone.
- Dark truth means the source's real cruelty, cost and moral weight, told straight. It does not mean gore for its own sake.
- **Narrator**: a recurring fictional character, "the Teller", keeper of the old telling. Never a real person, never claims to be a historian. Ritual line (same every episode, about 6 s, spoken 15 to 25 s in, AFTER a 15 s hook that states a stake and asks a question; the 2026-10-09 teardown found the rival title line lands at 20 to 37 s): in the frame world, a lit room, a shelf of unlabelled books, hands only, spoken in each language (Thai v1: "นี่คือ The Old Telling ตำนานฉบับเต็ม ที่คนมักไม่เล่า."). Frame 0 is already inside the hook. Close: "what the softened version left out".
- **Picture text that carries story meaning is also spoken (CEO 2026-10-10).** The master has English picture text and no burned subtitles, so a viewer who does not read English loses a black time card ("Seven months later") or any place, date or name shown only as text. Every dub track speaks that line in the Teller's voice while the text is on screen, after the previous sentence ends and before the next starts. Decorative text and lines the narration already says (the series ritual line, the title lockup) need no voice.
- Every episode names its source (translation, collector, century) in the description. Pre-1931 translations or oral collections only. Never copy a modern retelling (Disney, Riordan, Fagles).

## Voice, Thai track (draft v2, 2026-10-09, from ONE channel: World of Legend)

Wiki `research/2026-10-09-cmo-world-of-legend-top5-style-teardown.md` (supersedes the mixed 5-channel note, which the CEO rejected as the wrong method: "ไม่ใช่ให้เอาหลายๆ Style ของหลายๆ ช่องมารวมกัน"). These rules sit on top of Voice above; where they clash, the Thai track follows this section.

- Title = "ตำนาน" + a name the Thai viewer already owns, or a picture they know (ยมโลก, งู, นาค). 5 of 5 top videos do this.
- No hello, no teaser, no unnamed "he". After a short title card the first sentence says plainly WHO, WHAT, WHERE or WHEN ("ในกรีซโบราณ มีนักดนตรีคนหนึ่งชื่อ ..."). The brand ritual line comes after that.
- Tell it in order: who, what was done to them, what they do now, the rule, where it stands today. No flash-forward.
- The narrator speaks neutral written Thai: short, plain, จึง, ซึ่ง, เมื่อ…ก็…, the hedge "เชื่อกันว่า" for folk tales. No slang, no particles, no questions to the viewer, from the narrator. (CORRECTED: the earlier rule "spoken Thai with นะ and เลย" came from the mixed set and is retired.)
- Colloquial Thai lives only in a voiced character line, at an emotional peak.
- One real place, year or count per paragraph.
- Show the person inside the monster: name, reason, what was done to them. A small act with a huge price is the spine.
- No ครับ or ค่ะ until the CEO picks the Teller's voice (one World of Legend video has a female narrator).
- The serious tone stays (CEO 2026-10-09): no comic skits, no "ว้าว", no begging for likes before the end.
- **Comprehension gate:** three Thai readers see the title card and the first 60 s of text cold and say who the story is about and what happens to them. Fail = rewrite. No voice or render spend before it passes.

## Content guardrails

- **Out**: scripture of living religions (Bible, Quran) and any depiction that mocks a worshipped figure. YouTube's January 2026 sweep removed an AI Bible-story channel.
- **Case by case, CEO decides**: epics with living religious roots (Ramayana / Ramakien, Mahabharata). Tell as epic literature, never as doctrine.
- **In**: Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Celtic, Slavic, Japanese, Southeast Asian folk literature (for example Thai: Khun Chang Khun Phaen, Phra Aphai Mani), regional folktales.
- Vary the plot shape, thumbnail and title skeleton from episode to episode. No more than 2 videos in a row share a title skeleton. (YouTube's inauthentic-content review is per channel and punishes templates.)
- H3 hard rules bind every brief: adult characters are 18+; never red face or red ears; never print prompts, story text or entity pictures in logs or chat; status, counts and ids only.

## Palette (LOCKED for briefs)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night Ink | `#0F0C0A` | Backgrounds, thumbnail ground, end cards |
| Parchment | `#E8DDC4` | Primary text, title type |
| Hearth Ember | `#C9772B` | Accent: tag, rule lines, one highlighted word |
| Ash | `#8C8277` | Secondary text, borders, captions |
| Deep Verdigris | `#2F4A47` | Cold counter-tone panels, shadows in key art |

Contrast measured 2026-10-09 (WCAG ratio): Parchment on Night Ink 14.45, Ember on Night Ink 5.72, Ash on Night Ink 5.17, Parchment on Verdigris 7.11, **Ember on Verdigris 2.81 (fails: never put Ember text on Verdigris)**.

Banned: pure black `#000000`, pure white `#FFFFFF`, neon, gradients on type, any red as a brand colour.

## Type

- Latin titles: **EB Garamond** SemiBold (free, SIL OFL). Small labels: **IBM Plex Mono** Regular.
- Thai tracks: **Noto Serif Thai** SemiBold for titles, **Noto Sans Thai** Regular for body text.
- One accent word per thumbnail in Hearth Ember. Max 4 words of type on a thumbnail.

## Visual rules

- **Brand-strict assets** (thumbnails, title cards, lower thirds, end cards, channel art): manual composition only, HTML to Playwright, PIL or SVG, exact hex. **No image-gen API** for these.
- **B-roll** (inside the video): H3 studio only. **Cinematic drama with one lead per episode** (CEO 2026-10-09): the lead's identity comes from the source story and is carried by a signature prop, a lighting rule and a colour rule, so the face is not the only thing that must stay consistent. Painterly-cinematic look (film grade, lens, grain), firelit palette drawn from the tokens above. No photoreal human faces unless the CEO overrides (harder platform review, weaker H3 face consistency). Candidate leads: `Wikis/decisions/2026-10-09-the-old-telling-mythology-series.md`, section First stories.
- Live mark and any logo: none yet. A series wordmark is the first design task.
- Thumbnail: dark ground, one subject, one hot light source, one Ember word. Series name appears as a small Ash tag, never in the title.
