## What it is
A Facebook Page that explains Thai news events, mainly accidents and hazards.
- **Formula:** a Poster Hook of a real news image, then a Google Flow AI reconstruction, labelled "ภาพจำลอง" throughout, then real footage used with the owner's permission, with a source credit.
- **Job it does for the viewer:** "how could this happen, and how do I avoid it?" It is not breaking news.

## Mark
- A magnifier with an amber zig-zag "path" inside it, plus the word ไขเหตุ, in a dark circle.
- Hand-built SVG only. No image-gen for the mark or the posters' brand elements.
- Font: Noto Sans Thai 800.

## Hard rules
1. Every AI frame carries a visible "ภาพจำลอง" tag. Never present AI footage as real.
2. Never generate an identifiable real victim, suspect or official. Use generic characters.
3. Third-party footage only with the owner's permission. Credit on screen at first use and in the caption.
4. Do not name a person as at fault unless an official source says so (defamation risk).
5. Post with Meta's AI disclosure on.
6. No silent padding to stretch the runtime.
