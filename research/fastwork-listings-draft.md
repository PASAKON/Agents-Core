Not submitted. No account created. Nothing published. These are drafts only.

## Why these three, and why not the three approved earlier

The approved set was `ai-video` + `video-editing` + `seo`. Two measurements
landed after that choice and both point the same way.

**1. The median listing in every category sells zero.** Share of listings that
ever cleared 10 sales: seo 18%, motion-graphics 7%, social-media-ads 5%,
videography 4%, ai-video 4%, video-editing 3%. Choosing a category does not
decide whether we earn; being in the top decile of one does. So the category
should be the one where our edge is real, not the one with the prettiest ratio.

**2. The word "AI" caps the price.** Median price among sellers who have
actually sold (≥10 units):

| listed as | median price of proven sellers | p90 of all listings |
|---|---|---|
| ai-video-editing | ฿800 | ฿1,290 |
| ai-video | ฿949 | ฿3,500 |
| video-editing | ฿1,000 | ฿1,500 |
| motion-graphics | ฿3,000 | ฿5,000 |
| videography | **฿4,500** | **฿18,000** |

The same finished ad sells for 4.7× under `videography` as under `ai-video`.
Buyers price "AI video" as a commodity and "คลิปโฆษณา" as production. AI is our
cost advantage — it belongs in the margin, not in the headline.

Combined with the CEO's own steer ("AI for production สำหรับทำโฆษณา อันนี้เรา
ถนัดมาก" / "short movie AI Production"), the set becomes:

| # | category | price | role in the shop |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | videography | ฿4,500 | flagship — the money listing |
| 2 | motion-graphics | ฿3,000 | second income, best break-through odds after seo |
| 3 | ai-video | ฿590 | review farm — cheap, fast, feeds trust to #1 and #2 |

`seo` is kept as a candidate but not drafted here: its top sellers are backlink
packages, which is link-farming rather than the technical/AEO work we are good
at. A separate AEO/GEO listing is worth drafting once the video shop has
reviews — noted, not dropped.

## What must happen before any of this goes live

1. **CEO approves the copy** — nothing is submitted without it.
2. **Portfolio: 3 clips per listing, 9 total.** Zero marginal cost on the
   existing Higgsfield grant, but it is real generation time and it is the
   actual gate — a Fastwork listing with no samples does not sell.
3. **One account only.** The ToS prohibits holding more than one; all three
   listings hang off the same shop.
4. **Decide who answers buyers.** Fastwork ranks partly on response time (the
   index carries `response_time` per seller). This is the one part of the
   operation that cannot be generated, and it is the CEO's time unless we
   decide otherwise.

## Honest risks

- **The portfolio is the whole product.** Buyers pick on samples. If the 9 clips
  are not visibly better than a ฿999 competitor's, the price argument collapses.
- **฿4,500 with zero reviews is a hard sell.** Expect the first jobs to come to
  listing 3, and the flagship to stay quiet for weeks. That is the design, not a
  failure — but it means month 1 revenue is near zero and should be planned as
  such.
- **Turnaround is a promise, not a hope.** "24 ชม." on listing 3 binds us. If
  generation queues are busy that day, it is a late delivery and a bad review on
  the account that carries the flagship.
