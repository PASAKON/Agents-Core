# Research brief — Leopold Aschenbrenner / Situational Awareness fund collapse

**For:** second `script_writer` clip (TRADER UNCUT channel)
**Compiled:** 2026-08-04 by CTO, via WebSearch/WebFetch (no paid API)

## Story in one line

A 24-year-old ex-OpenAI safety researcher built a $45B hedge fund betting
correctly that AI infrastructure would boom — then extreme leverage on that
same correct bet wiped out most of it in a single month.

## VERIFIED — safe to state as fact (multiple mainstream outlets: CNBC ran
## two dedicated pieces, plus IBTimes, Fortune, Inc.com — much stronger
## sourcing than the Jadoodoo story)

- **Leopold Aschenbrenner**, 24 (IBTimes; one other summary said 25 —
  minor discrepancy, use 24 per the more detailed source). German-born to
  physician parents, skipped multiple grades, finished high school at 15,
  Columbia University valedictorian at 19.
- **OpenAI**: joined the Superalignment (AI safety) team. Dismissed in
  2024 — OpenAI's stated reason was "improper disclosure of internal
  information"; Aschenbrenner disputes this, saying he shared a "largely
  nonconfidential planning document." Both sides of this dispute should be
  represented if mentioned — don't state either version as settled fact.
  He is widely known for a long essay titled "Situational Awareness: The
  Decade Ahead," predicting a massive AI infrastructure buildout.
- **Fund**: founded **Situational Awareness**, July 2024. Seeded with
  **$225M**, investors included Stripe co-founders and Nat Friedman.
- **Thesis**: increasingly powerful AI needs a huge expansion of chips,
  memory, data centers, and electricity — went long infrastructure names
  (**SK Hynix, CoreWeave** named specifically), short software companies
  seen as vulnerable to AI disruption.
- **Peak**: fund reportedly reached **~$45 billion AUM** "earlier this
  month" (i.e. within July 2026), using leverage reported as high as
  **400%**.
- **Collapse**: in **July 2026** the fund lost roughly **67% of its
  value** in the month, driven by a semiconductor-stock selloff and
  margin calls. AUM fell from ~$45B to **~$10B**. Forced to sell most of
  its ~$16–20B public-equity book to **Citadel (Ken Griffin) at a
  discount**.
- **Not wiped out personally**: the fund retains its **private
  investments (~34% of the former portfolio)**, including a
  "multibillion-dollar stake in **Anthropic**" — unaffected by the public-
  market selloff.

## Recommended script framing

1. Hook on the irony: a 24-year-old who correctly predicted the AI boom
   still got wrecked by his own leverage — the thesis wasn't wrong, the
   position sizing was.
2. Use the verified numbers ($225M seed → $45B peak → ~$10B after July,
   -67% in a month, sold to Citadel at a discount) as the spine — these
   are independently confirmed by multiple mainstream outlets, unlike the
   Jadoodoo story.
3. Hedge the gain-percentage claim explicitly (439% vs 1,000%+) — pick a
   source and name it, or state it as a range.
4. Do not claim he is "broke" or "ruined" — he retains a private,
   multibillion-dollar Anthropic stake and the fund still has ~$10B AUM.
   The honest framing is "the public/leveraged bet imploded," not
   "personal bankruptcy."
5. This is a real, named, still-active public figure in tech/finance
   media — keep the tone factual/documentary, not mocking, given the
   story is still unfolding as of this research (2026-08-04).
