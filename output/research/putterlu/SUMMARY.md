# Putterlu (@Putterlu) — Ads E-Commerce Playbook Extract
Source: youtube.com/@Putterlu, 7 videos, TH auto-caption transcripts in this dir.
Model: 24yo Thai, China-import → test → brand, sells via TikTok/Shopee/own Shopify+Meta ads.

## 1. Business Model (macro loop)
Source product from China (cheap MOQ now, even 50pcs at factory price via Alibaba/1688) →
test small on Facebook ads (no stock yet) → if profitable, stock + scale → if it scales big,
turn into own brand (private label, own packaging/IP).

## 2. Winning Product — 7-factor checklist (video: lRx3bZOJ4gY)
1. **Problem Solving** — actually fixes a real pain, not just novelty
2. **Wow Factor** — visually striking within first 1-3 seconds of a clip
3. **Mass Market** — appeals to a broad audience, not a tiny niche (needed to scale)
4. **Content-Friendly** — easy to film, naturally shareable/viral format
5. **Blue Ocean** — few/no competitors; if in Red Ocean, needs a real USP to escape it
6. **Word of Mouth** — customers/influencers naturally want to tell others
7. **Margin + Perceived Value** — cost must look far cheaper than the price it commands
   (e.g. cost 150-165 THB, sells 1,290-1,600 THB — customer perceives full retail value)

## 3. Product Sourcing Method (video: OGi5toYaaJM EP1)
- Use **Facebook Ads Library**, search by ad category/keyword, US market first
  (US e-commerce ~100x ahead of Thailand — trends surface there first)
- Filter to ads that have been running **2-3+ months** = proof it's profitable for the
  original seller (fresh ads = no proof yet)
- Cross-check Thai market: search same keyword on TikTok/Shopee/Lazada/FB Library —
  if no exact match exists in Thailand, it's still Blue Ocean here
- Check true factory cost: screenshot the product photo → reverse image search on
  Alibaba → compare cost vs. foreign retail price (gap = perceived-value proof)
- Paid tool: **"Spy"** ad-spy tool (~99–3,000 THB/mo) — lets you download competitor
  video ads directly and filter by **Shares** (best engagement signal, better than
  likes/comments) — plain Ads Library can't download video or sort by shares

## 4. Validate-Before-Stock (videos: t0LojwzpoAA, OngHERwMB3s)
- Never bulk-order inventory on a guess. Post a real ad on Facebook, spend a small
  budget, measure 3-7 days.
- Decision rule: ROI ≥3x → stock immediately and scale. ROI ~1.3x (near breakeven) →
  drop it, cancel any pending customer orders, move on.
- This is the core risk-reduction trick — data before capital, unlike e.g. opening a
  restaurant where you can't "test" before committing.

## 5. "Stealing Winning Ads" — script framework (video: t0LojwzpoAA, most detailed)
Not copying an ad outright — borrowing the **proven structure/script** from a big
foreign brand's tested ad, then reskinning it for your product + Thai audience.

**Step 0 — answer 6 questions before writing any ad:**
1. Avatar — exact customer (age, specific pain, situation)
2. Angle — which angle you're selling from (e.g. side-sleepers vs. back pain vs. moms)
3. Concept — format (he recommends **voice-over**, easier than talking-head)
4. Mechanism/Desired Result — what outcome the customer wants
5. Customer Belief — what they already tried and failed at (address that skepticism
   before pitching, or they won't believe a fresh claim)
6. Multiple hook versions to A/B test

**Build 3-4 "Starting Documents" via AI (ChatGPT Deep Research / Claude) before any ad copy:**
1. Research Report — deep dive on product/market/competitors
2. Avatar Document — detailed target profile: behavior, wants, fears
3. Offer Brief — USP, positioning, proof points, offer structure (bonuses/promos)
4. Core Belief Document — customer's pre-existing beliefs/objections

Load all 4 into a persistent **Claude Project** so it "knows" the product/brand for
every future ad without re-pasting context.

**Then, per winning ad found:**
1. Find the winning ad (via Spy tool, sorted by Shares — highest engagement)
2. Download it, transcribe free via **Riverside.fm** ("Transcript Now")
3. Paste transcript into the Claude Project → ask it to analyze structure (hook →
   problem agitation → mechanism reveal → offer/guarantee → CTA)
4. Ask Claude to rewrite a **new Thai-language script**, same proven structure,
   reskinned to your product/avatar/angle — not a literal translation
5. Generate **multiple hook variants** to test (hook = first 3-5 sec; job of a hook is
   only to stop the scroll, not to sell)
6. Ask for **Visual Instructions per scene** too (shot description per line) to hand to
   an editor or an AI video-gen tool (he uses "Hi Field" for AI b-roll/voiceover video)

Reported result: 355,000 THB in 22 days, ad spend <2,000 THB/day — attributed to
having intent/structure behind every ad rather than mass-producing unfocused creative.
His stated principle: **"Intense over volume"** — 10 intentional ads beat 50 random ones.

## 6. Platform Economics (videos: UrzGMsfJdxw, OngHERwMB3s)
- TikTok Shop fees compound: commission + ad commission + affiliate commission +
  "campaign" fees + vague platform fees. Real example: 2M THB revenue → 650k THB total
  fees (not even counting COGS) → net ~1.1-1.19M received.
- Moved primary scaling to **own Shopify site + Meta (Facebook) ads** — no platform
  commission, only ~1,000 THB/mo Shopify plan + payment/shipping fees.
- Did NOT fully quit TikTok — kept it running organically via influencer/UGC creators
  (paid only via commission, so ~zero risk) because it still drives brand awareness that
  spills over into Shopee sales (**halo effect**: customer sees product on TikTok/FB,
  doesn't buy there, later searches it on Shopee where — if you're the only seller with
  that exact product — they just buy from you with zero ad spend).
- Runs it as a **"One Person Company"**: no in-house packing (uses 3PL fulfillment), 1
  hired person for bookkeeping/order entry, AI for content — his only real jobs are
  sourcing new products and managing ad spend/creative.

## 7. Live Case Study — "แม่และเด็ก 35+" niche (found via FB Ads Library, 2026-07-04)
Searched US FB Ads Library for "postpartum belly wrap" (matches the requested niche:
mom & baby, age 35+). Confirms the whole framework works with just the free Ads Library
— no paid spy tool needed to get started.

**Candidate 1 — "Solniera"** (postpartum compression garment, started ~23 Jun 2026 —
recent, so weaker "proof" by Putterlu's 2-3mo rule, but ranked near top by impressions).
Full long-form story-style ad copy, structurally:
1. **Hook**: quotes her own PT dismissively saying "It's diastasis recti, just keep doing
   the exercises" — calls out the exact frustration of the target avatar in her own words
2. **Failed-solution montage**: tried 3 different specialists/PTs over 4 months, did
   everything asked, no real result — builds credibility + agitates the "I've tried
   everything" belief (this IS the Core Belief Document step in practice)
3. **Discovery/named mechanism**: she researches clinically and finds "the 23-hour gap"
   / "circumferential support deficit" — a pseudo-scientific label for why PT alone
   isn't enough. Same technique as Revoget's "Misalignment Lock" — **naming an invented
   mechanism is a repeating winning-ad pattern**, not a one-off.
4. **Differentiator**: explicitly rules out lookalike solutions first ("not a velcro
   belt, not a binder over clothes") before revealing the real product — high-waisted
   garment, 360° compression, wearable under any outfit
5. **Cultural credibility anchor**: "Bengkung-inspired — the same wrap Malaysian women
   have used after every birth for 500 years" — ties a modern product to an old
   tradition for instant trust. **Direct Thai localization angle**: Thai postpartum
   culture has its own equivalent (ผ้ารัดหน้าท้องหลังคลอด, "อยู่ไฟ" tradition) — same
   credibility trick reskins natively for a Thai audience, arguably stronger than a
   translated Malaysian reference.
6. **Offer**: 30-day money-back guarantee, "every dollar comes back"

**Candidate 2 — "The Mommy Care Kit"** (started 18 Aug 2025, still active ~11 months
later, has multiple ad versions running) — the stronger proof-of-profitability pick by
Putterlu's own rule (2-3mo+ runtime). Not yet analyzed in depth — next step if continuing
this niche.

**Other candidates seen in same search**: Ibu Ayurveda ("The Postpartum Ritual you
shouldn't miss" — belly wrap carousel ad), Lifepro, Whatyam-C (shapewear, video ad,
"Buy 2 Get 2 Free" style offer) — all in the same postpartum-recovery-garment space,
confirming it's a validated, currently-running ad category, not a one-off.

## Applicability to a new "Ads E-Commerce" project
This channel is basically a tactical SOP for exactly that: product discovery via ad-spy
+ Ads Library, a hard validate-before-stock gate, and a repeatable AI-assisted
"steal-the-structure" ad-script pipeline. Concrete next steps if building this out:
- A small internal tool/script that pulls FB Ads Library + filters by ad-age (2-3mo+)
  could semi-automate step 3.
- The "4-document → Claude Project → reskin winning ad" ad-script pipeline is directly
  buildable as a repeatable prompt-chain (could live as a skill or a small script here).
- Validate-before-stock gate (ROI≥3x/3-7 days) is a clean decision rule to encode into
  any product-testing tracker.
