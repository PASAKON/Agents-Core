# Research brief — Nick Pinto / $TRUMP memecoin loss

## Story in one line

A 26-year-old content creator turned a $7,000 memecoin bet into a peak
position worth $480,000 — then spent big to attend an exclusive dinner
with the token's namesake, watched the coin crash, and locked in roughly
$250,000 in losses.

## VERIFIED — safe to state as fact (widely corroborated: Forbes,
## Forbes Australia, The Daily Beast, Fortune, Yahoo Finance/Moneywise,
## Invezz, AOL all cover this same story independently)

- **Nick Pinto**, 26, a social-media content creator, invested **$7,000**
  shortly after the $TRUMP memecoin launched.
- **$TRUMP memecoin itself (independently verified, multiple crypto-news
  outlets — Yahoo Finance, KuCoin, CCN, TheStreet):**
  - Launched on **Solana, January 17, 2025**.
  - Peaked within 48 hours at **$73.43**; some sources cite **$74.27 on
    January 19, 2025** (inauguration day), fully-diluted valuation
    briefly near **$75 billion**.
  - Crashed roughly **98% from peak**; by end of 2025 was below $5
    (~93% down); a **May 2025 "holder dinner" announcement** caused a
    temporary price spike (~doubled) before retracing again; October
    2025 crash brought it near $3.80; fresh all-time low of **$1.50 in
    June 2026**.
  - Across all holders: ~989,000 wallets collectively lost **$3.81
    billion**, with roughly two-thirds of all wallets that ever bought
    the token losing money net.
- **Pinto increased his position to approximately $480,000 at its peak**
  — reported specifically in the context of qualifying for a Trump-hosted
  dinner for major token holders. A closely related figure: he
  **"accumulated more than $360,000 in cryptocurrency" specifically to
  attend** that dinner (see Flagged section — these two numbers likely
  describe different things, not a contradiction).
- **He attended the exclusive holder dinner** at a Trump golf club in
  Virginia (this lines up with the independently-verified May 2025
  "holder dinner" price-spike event above). His own texted reaction to
  the food, quoted directly and corroborated across outlets: **"Trash"**
  and **"Walmart steak, man."**
- **He later sold most of his holdings, locking in losses of roughly
  $250,000.**

## FLAGGED — minor ambiguity, handle carefully

- **$480,000 vs $360,000+** — one figure ("$480,000") is described as his
  peak position value; the other ("$360,000+") is described as what he
  "accumulated... to attend" the dinner. These are plausibly two
  different measurements (total peak holdings vs. the specific amount
  spent/held to qualify for dinner access) rather than conflicting
  reports — but no single source reconciles them explicitly. Script
  should either use one clearly-labeled figure as the headline number
  ($7,000 → $480,000 peak → ~$250,000 locked-in loss is the cleanest,
  most-repeated arc) and mention the $360,000 dinner-qualification detail
  as a supporting fact, not a competing headline number.
- **"Attended both Trump memecoin events... Virginia golf resort and
  Mar-a-Lago"** — the Virginia golf-club dinner (May 2025) is
  well-corroborated. A second Mar-a-Lago event appears in one summary
  but wasn't independently re-confirmed across multiple primary sources
  in this research pass — mention only with a light hedge if at all, or
  omit and stick to the one confirmed dinner.
- **Political framing** — this is inherently a Trump-branded product
  story (his memecoin). Per CEO direction, proceed with it as a financial/
  investing story (leverage of a celebrity/political brand into a
  volatile asset, retail investor risk), not as political commentary —
  no opinion on Trump himself, stay strictly on the trading/investing
  mechanics and Pinto's personal financial outcome.

## Recommended script framing

1. Hook on the multiplier arc: $7,000 → $480,000 (nearly 69x) → ~$250,000
   locked-in loss. That swing is the dramatic spine.
2. Use the memecoin's own documented boom-bust timeline (launch spike to
   $73+, 98% total collapse, the May 2025 dinner-announcement price pop)
   as the "market mechanics" backdrop — this is a real, independently
   documented memecoin volatility case study, not just one person's bad
   luck.
3. The dinner detail (spending big specifically to buy access, then
   getting "Walmart steak") is a strong human/ironic beat — verified via
   his own quoted words, safe to use directly.
4. Keep it about investing behavior (chasing hype, doubling down to buy
   access/status, no exit discipline) rather than politics.
5. No claim about his current total net worth or overall portfolio status
   beyond what's stated (locked in ~$250K loss on this position) — don't
   extrapolate to "he's broke overall" beyond the quote already on record
   from a different investor in the same article (Vadim Fistikan said
   "I'm now broke" — that quote belongs to Fistikan, NOT Pinto; do not
   attribute it to Pinto).
