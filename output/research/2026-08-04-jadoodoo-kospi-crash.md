## VERIFIED — safe to state as fact

- **KOSPI crashed -10.84% on 2026-07-28** (closed 6,023.66, -732.09 pts),
  triggered by a semiconductor-sector shock (news around China's Changxin
  Memory). Circuit breakers hit both KOSPI and KOSDAQ. The very next day
  (2026-07-29) saw *another* circuit-breaker halt (-5.98%) — the first-ever
  back-to-back circuit-breaker days in Korean stock market history.
  Sources: [Herald Corp](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10824151),
  [FN News](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202607281840063710),
  [Hankyung](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026072930836),
  [Money Today](https://www.mt.co.kr/stock/2026/07/28/2026072816223678548).
- **Jadoodoo (자두두)** is a real, established Korean livestreamer
  (originally SOOP/AfreecaTV, ~800 avg viewers historically; also active on
  YouTube `@jadoodoo` and X/Twitter), known specifically for crypto/futures
  trading content. Documented history of large trading swings:
  - 2021 saga (Namu Wiki): made ~30M KRW → Bitcoin crash wiped it → rebuilt
    from ~8M KRW via futures (RSI/Elliott Wave) to ~100M KRW → forced
    liquidation on an ADA short position (~Sept 2021) when ADA spiked.
  - KBS TV segment saga (~9 months before a later post): started with 10M
    KRW → grew to 100M KRW → lost the seed too; second TV appearance: made
    50M KRW, donated 13M KRW of it.
  - Source: [Namu Wiki 자두두](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9E%90%EB%91%90%EB%91%90)
    (WebFetch blocked it directly — facts above are from the search-result
    summary of that page; re-verify by reading the page manually if a
    precise quote is needed).
- **A viral claim ties her specifically to the 2026-07-28 crash**: an X
  post (@cryptojourneyrs, "Rodney") states that during "yesterday's sharp
  selloff" she had a public on-stream meltdown, losing roughly
  **3,000,000 KRW (~$2,100 / ~77,000 บาท)** on a leveraged futures
  position. This matches the CEO's brief closely and is the likely origin
  of the story. [X post](https://x.com/cryptojourneyrs/status/2069814157789253657)
  (could not open directly — X blocked the fetch; text captured via search
  snippet only).

## FLAGGED — do not state as fact; use "reportedly" / "ตามที่มีการแชร์กันไวรัล"

- **ABAB News** (aggregator, published 2026-07-29) is internally
  inconsistent: headline says "**$3 Million** Futures Position
  Liquidation" (almost certainly a mistranslation/clickbait inflation of
  "3 million **won**"), body text separately claims the crash date was
  "**June 23**" — which contradicts both its own publish date and every
  primary Korean news outlet's July 28 dating. Treat none of this
  outlet's specific numbers as reliable.
  [ABAB News](https://www.ababnews.com/news/4137c84c-8636-475a-93f6-3a49635f49e8)
- A separate **$11,255 (~400,000 บาท) Bitcoin-short, no-stop-loss** loss
  story appears in some summaries — this looks like a **different,
  probably older/unrelated incident**. Do not conflate it with the
  KOSPI-crash-day story.
- **No direct source clip found.** Her most recent 20 YouTube uploads
  (`@jadoodoo/videos`, checked 2026-08-04) are gaming/lifestyle content
  (Lineage, Dubai trip, etc.) — nothing about this incident. The viral
  spread looks like a stream-clip repost on X/TikTok rather than a
  dedicated YouTube upload; TikTok discovery page exists
  ("Jadoodoo Losing Crypto") but wasn't independently opened.
- The **3,000,000 KRW figure traces to one secondhand X post**, not to
  Jadoodoo herself or a primary Korean news report. Treat as
  probable-but-unconfirmed.

## Recommended script framing

1. Use the **2026-07-28 KOSPI crash** as the real, citable hook (historic
   back-to-back circuit-breaker event — strong on its own).
2. Reference Jadoodoo's loss with a hedge: "รายงานว่าเธอขาดทุนไปกว่า 3
   ล้านวอน" / "ตามคลิปที่แชร์กันไวรัล" — never assert the figure as a
   confirmed, sourced fact.
3. Do not use the "$3 million" figure anywhere — it is very likely a
   unit-confusion error from a low-quality aggregator.
4. Do not mention the $11,255 Bitcoin-short story as if it's the same
   incident.
5. The real lesson (leverage risk, no stop-loss, a bad day catching an
   experienced trader) stands regardless of the exact loss figure — the
   script doesn't need to over-index on a single disputed number to land.
