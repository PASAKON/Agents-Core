01 · Voice

Thoughtful, crafted, quietly smart.

Not a fast, punchy SaaS voice. The brand sounds like someone who took their time — sentences that breathe, language about the result, not the mechanism.

01

05 · Product — mobile first

Few buttons. Every one load-bearing.

375px is the default canvas, not an afterthought. One accent color drives every primary action — if everything is accented, nothing is.

06 · Must-pass

Run before shipping anything customer-facing.

  • ✓No wash color on a button, nav bar, input, or UI chrome
  • ✓Only one accent (dusk) driving primary actions on the screen
  • ✓Display face used for headline only, never body copy
  • ✓Wordmark lowercase; mark has exactly one node
  • ✓Checked at 375px before anything wider
  • ✓No em dash, no AI-generic language, in shipped copy
