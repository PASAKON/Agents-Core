# The Odyssey Parody Trailer — Full Scene Prompt Library

All scenes retrofitted to the `seedance-scene-prompt` skill format (REFERENCE +
VISUAL + DIALOGUE + AUDIO/SFX). Character sheets defined once below — every
scene references them by `@CharacterN` tag instead of re-describing from
scratch. Generate each `@CharacterN` once in Seedance 2.0, save it, and reuse
the tag across every scene that character appears in.

---

## Character Sheets

### @Character1 — The Hero
Bearded man, early-to-mid 40s. **Face:** weathered, sun-worn, deep creases at
the eyes from squinting into sun and wind, strong jaw softened by a full but
neatly-kept dark beard streaked with grey at the chin and temples. **Hair:**
dark brown, damp/sweat-slicked in most scenes, cut short but slightly
overgrown, grey at the temples. **Eyes:** deep-set, dark brown, tired but
alert. **Skin:** sun-worn olive-tan, weathered texture, small old scars on the
forearms, a faint scar through the left eyebrow. **Build:** broad-shouldered,
muscular but leaner than a bodybuilder — a functional warrior's build,
roughly 180cm/5'11". **Personality read:** guarded, weary, dry rather than
showy, quiet authority, softens only around family. **Default wardrobe:** dark
bronze-and-leather sleeveless lamellar cuirass over a linen tunic, sword
sheathed at the hip, no cape unless noted.

### @Character2 — The Son
Lean young man, early twenties. **Face:** softer features than his father's,
not yet weathered, clean-shaven or very light stubble. **Hair:** dark brown,
curly, worn a little long and unruly. **Eyes:** dark brown, more openly
emotional than his father's guarded look. **Skin:** lightly tanned,
unweathered. **Build:** lean and athletic rather than heavily muscled,
roughly 178cm/5'10". **Personality read:** earnest, projecting more
confidence than he fully feels, protective toward his mother. **Default
wardrobe:** simple leather-strapped tunic, or partial bronze/leather armor in
later, more battle-ready scenes.

### @Character3 — Penelope (the wife)
Woman, late thirties. **Face:** composed, striking rather than soft, high
cheekbones, steady unreadable expression. **Hair:** dark brown, pulled back
in a low elegant bun, a few loose strands. **Eyes:** dark, watchful, guarded.
**Skin:** fair, indoor-pale compared to the hero. **Build:** slender, upright
posture, roughly 165cm/5'5". **Personality read:** restrained, dignified,
carries grief and worry privately. **Default wardrobe:** deep teal/petrol
pleated gown with draped cape-like sleeves, wide gold choker necklace, gold
earrings.

### @Character4 — The Island Woman (not the wife)
Woman, early thirties. **Face:** calm, open, unguarded. **Hair:** natural
sun-warmed blonde, loose over the shoulders. **Eyes:** calm grey-blue.
**Skin:** sun-tanned, warm undertone. **Build:** average height, roughly
165cm/5'5", relaxed posture. **Personality read:** self-possessed, patient,
finds the hero's urgency mildly amusing — she belongs here, he doesn't.
**Default wardrobe:** worn draped linen and faded silk matching the shelter
around her.

### @Character5 — The Villain (lead suitor)
Man, mid-to-late forties. **Face:** soft, well-fed, smug half-smile that
rarely fades, groomed short beard. **Hair:** dark, greying, neatly kept.
**Eyes:** pale, cold, calculating. **Skin:** indoor-soft, untanned. **Build:**
heavier-set, comfortable rather than athletic, roughly 175cm/5'9".
**Personality read:** unhurried, savoring his own power. **Default wardrobe:**
richly robed in looted fine fabric, gold rings on multiple fingers, wine cup
usually in hand.

### @Character6 — The Pink General
Man, unnaturally tall — noticeably taller than every other soldier around
him, easily 200cm+/6'7"+. **Face:** strong-jawed, composed, played completely
seriously by his own performance — the joke is only ever the contrast with
everyone around him, never his own demeanor. **Hair:** dark, cropped short
under the helmet. **Build:** broad, imposing, the largest soldier in any
scene. **Wardrobe (fixed, never varies):** tall horsehair-crested helmet and
long draped cape in the same silhouette as other commanders, rendered
entirely in a bold, deliberately out-of-place vivid pink — jarring against
the muted bronze/leather/firelit tones of everyone else.

### @Character7 — The Armored Guard (uniform type, not an individual — use for any number in a scene)
Roughly two meters tall — about twice the height of an average man, about
half the height of surrounding trees, heavier than any human but not
monstrous in scale. Encased head to foot in featureless articulated
iron-grey plate armor, full masked helmet hiding any face — no visible skin
or eyes, no individual identity. **Movement:** eerie, synchronized, unhurried
calm rather than urgency — reads like an advancing machine, not a man.

## Scene 1 — Bedroom Farewell ("Why... will you come back?")

```
REFERENCE
@Character1 — The Hero (see Character Sheet)
@Character3 — Penelope (see Character Sheet)

VISUAL
Cinematic close-up two-shot inside a modest Bronze-Age stone bedroom with
faded ochre fresco walls, lit only by a single unseen oil lamp flickering
just off-frame, warm orange firelight casting soft moving shadows across
both faces.

The hero is bare-headed, damp with sweat, wearing his default sleeveless
cuirass over a linen tunic, no cape. The hero stands very close to
Penelope, both lit by the same warm flame. The hero's jaw is tight, eyes
tired and resigned — he has just answered her, quietly, and now holds
still, barely breathing, firelight flickering across his skin and damp
hair.

Penelope searches the hero's face, unmoving, her expression caught
between hope and dread.

Camera holds a slow, almost imperceptible push-in, shallow depth of
field with both faces sharp and the background dissolving into warm
near-black. Fine film grain, large-format historical epic realism, warm
practical torchlight only. No modern elements, no text, no on-screen
graphics.

DIALOGUE (spoken audio, not rendered as on-screen text)
PENELOPE (soft, searching, hoping he'll say otherwise):
"Why... will you come back?"

[silence, 2-3 seconds]

THE HERO (quiet, flat, resigned — no anger, almost apologetic):
"No."

AUDIO/SFX
Quiet ambient bed under the dialogue, no music, no score. Low crackle
and pop of the oil lamp, faint hiss of burning oil, distant creak of old
wooden beams settling, soft shuffle of feet on stone. Hold the silence
around the pause long enough to feel the tension.
```

armor and full face masks. Weapons are rusted swords and bows. Blades

revealing the torchlit army waiting outside ready to charge in.

combat rhythm, the hero's face stays clearly readable in every shot that

text, no on-screen graphics.

AUDIO/SFX
Sound effects only, no music, no score, no dialogue. Sharp, heavy clash

```
REFERENCE

confirming it.

shallow depth of field with the torn silk and driftwood soft in the warm

DIALOGUE (spoken audio, not rendered as on-screen text)
THE HERO (quiet, hoarse, almost afraid to ask):
"How long... am I here?"

[she thinks for a moment, studying his face before answering]

THE ISLAND WOMAN (calm, measured, delivered like it's significant):
"Twenty..."

[a small beat]

THE ISLAND WOMAN (continuing, deflating it):
"...days."

AUDIO/SFX
Quiet ambient bed under the dialogue, no music, no score. Distant surf
rolling steadily outside, faint wind moving through the loose silk walls

## Scene 10 — Suitors' Hall Climax (Bowstring Reveal)

```
REFERENCE
@Character1 — The Hero (see Character Sheet — disguised, described
below)
Unnamed suitors (including one oblivious comic-relief suitor)

VISUAL
A grand feasting hall lit warm and golden, torches and oil lamps along
the walls, long tables piled with food, dozens of arrogant suitors
lounging and drinking, a lyre player performing in the corner, the room
loud with careless laughter and music.

At the edge of the hall stands a ragged, hunched beggar — the hero in
disguise, his established bearded face hidden beneath grime and a worn
cloak, deliberately unrecognizable to the room. He is handed an old,
heavy hunting bow that none of the suitors could string earlier as a
mocking dare. The hero straightens slowly, shrugging off the disguise's
stoop, and draws the string back in one smooth, effortless pull that
makes the whole room go quiet — the lyre falters and stops.

In the stunned silence, one suitor near the food table, still oblivious
to what just happened, loudly complains that someone knocked over a
platter of food during the commotion — gesturing at the mess, indignant,
completely missing the real danger in the room.

That beat of comic relief is cut short as the hero's arrow releases, and
the room erupts — suitors scrambling for weapons, tables overturned,
chaos breaking out.

Camera: slow tightening push-in on the hero as he draws the bow and the
room falls silent, quick cut to the oblivious complaining suitor, then a
sudden wide handheld shot as the hall erupts into violence. Fine film
grain, large-format realistic epic-drama look, warm torchlit palette. No
modern elements, no text, no on-screen graphics.

AUDIO/SFX
Sound effects and diegetic room sound only, no overlaid score. Loud
feast chatter, clinking cups, a live lyre playing casually in the
background, all fading out as the hero draws the bowstring — the lyre
note bending and stopping awkwardly. A creak of the bow under tension,
then dead silence for a beat. The oblivious suitor's indignant complaint
about the spilled food, slightly too loud for the silence. Then the
sharp twang of the released bowstring, and the room instantly exploding
into overturned tables, scraping chairs, shouting, and the ring of
swords being drawn.
```

## Scene 13 — "LOST SIGNAL" Phone Gag (Over-the-Shoulder)

```
REFERENCE
@Character1 — The Hero (see Character Sheet — seen from behind only,
face not required in frame)
@Character4 — The Island Woman (see Character Sheet — seen from behind

VISUAL
Inside the same driftwood-and-faded-silk shelter from Scene 6, warm
late-afternoon light filtering through the worn fabric.

The hero is seen from behind, over his shoulder, his back and shoulders
filling most of the frame. The Island Woman sits beside the hero, also
turned away from camera, her natural blonde hair visible from behind.
Both face out toward the shelter's opening and the sea beyond.

Close on the hero's hand and the phone he holds in front of him —
jarringly out of place against the ancient wood, silk, and firelight
around it, held completely naturally, as if this were the most normal
object in the world. The hero unlocks it and opens a map application.
The screen glows pale blue-white, visible over his shoulder. A loading
spinner turns for a moment, then the screen flatly displays "LOST
SIGNAL" in plain white text on the app's interface — not a caption
overlaid on the footage, just what's actually showing on the phone's
screen in-frame.

The hero's shoulders and back tense visibly, head tilting down toward
the screen with quiet, controlled frustration — no exaggerated reaction,
just stillness and tension read through posture alone since his face
isn't visible. Without a word, his arm draws back and hurls the phone
hard, out of frame toward the sea. The Island Woman does not turn around
or react, still facing the water, unbothered.

Camera: static or very slight handheld over-the-shoulder framing behind
the hero for the entire shot, the phone screen and his tensing back the
focus throughout, then following the throw's motion as his arm releases.
Fine film grain, large-format realistic epic-adventure look, warm
